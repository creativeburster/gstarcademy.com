Neutral navigation—not another closed course wall.

We organize public CAD learning sources, add short editorial context, and keep literacy articles vendor-neutral in the knowledge base.

What we publish

Curated links, short editorial notes, CAD news roundups, and glossary-style articles. We add structure and context—we do not replace original courses or channels.

What we do not do

We do not host, download, transcode, or mirror third-party videos or full tutorials. Visitors always leave our site to view content at the source platform.

Editorial process & sourcing

Each knowledge-base article is researched from primary vendor documentation, drafted with AI assistance, then verified line-by-line by a human review desk (AEC, Mechanical, or Data Exchange) before publication. Every page carries the responsible review desk byline, the publication date, and the last-reviewed date. We publish our workflow in detail — read the full editorial process and source list.
