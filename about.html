Corporate Profile & Mission

About GstarCAD Academy

GstarCAD Academy (gstarcademy.com) is the official global educational, skill certification, and customer enablement hub of Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd. (苏州浩辰软件股份有限公司).

30+ Years of Independent CAD Innovation

Founded in 1992 and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange Science & Technology Innovation Board (STAR Market, Stock Code: 688657), Gstarsoft is one of the world's few CAD platform vendors possessing an independently developed, proprietary 2D/3D CAD geometry kernel.

Unlike vendors who merely wrap third-party software engines, Gstarsoft has continuously invested over three decades into low-level graphics algorithms, database serialization, and multi-core processor optimizations. Today, Gstarsoft delivers industry-standard CAD, vertical AEC/manufacturing packages, and mobile/cloud drawing solutions trusted by over 500,000 corporate clients across 100+ countries.

The Academy's Global Mission

1. Zero-Friction AutoCAD® Migration

Empowering global engineering firms to escape onerous recurring subscription price hikes. We provide comprehensive compatibility matrices, command parity guides, and LISP migration benchmarks to ensure smooth transitions with zero downtime.

2. Professional Skill Certification

Standardizing CAD drafting competence worldwide. Through our 289-question diagnostic engine spanning 6 engineering tracks, professionals diagnose skill gaps and earn accredited GstarCAD Academy digital credentials.

3. Vertical Engineering Solutions

From GstarCAD Mechanical with its comprehensive ISO/ANSI standard parts and dynamic BOMs, to GstarCAD Architecture's parametric walls and doors, we train engineers to maximize domain-specific design productivity.

4. Global Developer Ecosystem

Supporting Independent Software Vendors (ISVs) worldwide. We provide GRX C++ SDK documentation, .NET wrappers, and LISP scripting support, allowing developers to port ObjectARX applications in days.
