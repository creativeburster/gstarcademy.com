Editorial Team

GstarCAD Academy is written and maintained by the GstarCAD Academy editorial team at Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd. (SSE: 688657), the company that develops GstarCAD, GstarCAD Mechanical, GstarCAD Architecture and DWG FastView. Articles are published under the team name rather than individual bylines.

Drafts are prepared with the help of AI writing tools and then edited against official vendor documentation and the standards each page cites. That means mistakes are possible. If you find one, please tell us through the contact page. The full workflow is described in our editorial process.

What we cover

The knowledge base is organised into six subject areas.

2D drafting, DWG & developer APIs

GstarCAD and AutoCAD-compatible drafting workflows, DWG/DXF file handling, command aliases, AutoLISP, .NET and the GRX C++ SDK.

Main references: ISO 128 drawing conventions; official GstarCAD and Autodesk developer documentation.

BIM & AEC

Revit, Archicad, GstarCAD Architecture, IFC exchange, clash detection and common data environment workflows.

Main references: ISO 19650, ISO 16739-1 (IFC 4.3) and buildingSMART specifications.

Mechanical CAD & manufacturing

Parametric modeling in SOLIDWORKS, Inventor, Creo, NX and GstarCAD Mechanical; drawings, BOMs, GD&T and CAM handoff.

Main references: ASME Y14.5, ISO 1101, ISO 286 and ISO 10303 (STEP).

Civil & geospatial

Surfaces, alignments, corridors, survey data and GIS coordinate systems in Civil 3D, OpenRoads and DWG-based tools.

Main references: AASHTO geometric design guidance, LandXML and IFC 4.3 alignment.

Simulation & CAE

Introductory FEA and CFD concepts: meshing, boundary conditions, convergence and result interpretation.

Main references: ASME V&V 10/20 and NAFEMS benchmark literature.

Visualization & rendering

Rendering, materials, lighting and colour management for architectural and product visualization.

Main references: ACES / OpenColorIO, glTF 2.0 and OpenUSD specifications.
