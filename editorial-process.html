Editorial process & sources

How GstarCAD Academy articles are researched, drafted, checked, labelled and corrected — including where AI tools are used and what we do not verify. We publish this so readers can judge how much weight to give each page.

Last updated: 2026-10-27 · Owner: GstarCAD Academy Editorial Team

1 · Sources

Articles start from primary sources: official vendor documentation (help.autodesk.com, support.sw.siemens.com, 3ds.com support, gstarcad.net), standards bodies (ISO, ASME, buildingSMART) and published SDK documentation (GRX, ObjectARX, .NET APIs). Each page links to the sources it relies on.

2 · Drafting with AI assistance

The editorial team uses AI writing tools to prepare first drafts. Drafts are then edited for accuracy against the cited documentation, checked for broken links and formatted for readability. AI-assisted drafting can introduce errors, so we treat every reader report seriously.

3 · What we check, and the limits

  • GstarCAD commands, settings and workflows are checked against official GstarCAD documentation.
  • Content about third-party software reflects that vendor's public documentation; those vendors do not review it.
  • Cost comparisons and performance figures are estimates unless a source is cited.
  • Case studies labelled Illustrative Scenario are composite examples, not specific customers.

4 · Updates

The date shown on each article is its last update. We revise pages when vendors ship new releases, when standards change, or when readers report problems through the contact page.

Source attribution

Each article links externally to the primary vendor documentation, release notes, and authoritative third-party references it draws on. We never embed vendor logos, product screenshots, or material covered by copyright restrictions without explicit license. Our writing about a vendor product is original commentary; the vendor remains the authoritative source for product specifications.

Sources we use most often

  • Autodesk Help (help.autodesk.com)
  • Autodesk University recordings
  • PTC Support (support.ptc.com) and PTC University
  • Siemens Solutions Center (support.sw.siemens.com)
  • Dassault Systèmes 3DS.com product support
  • Gstarsoft support center (gstarcad.net/support)
  • buildingSMART IFC specification documents
  • ISO/STEP and PDF/3D PDF specification documents
  • Vendor partner ecosystem documentation (where directly relevant)
  • Recognized industry journals (AEC Magazine, Engineering.com)

Who writes GstarCAD Academy

Articles and course material are maintained by the GstarCAD Academy editorial team at Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd. (SSE: 688657), developer of GstarCAD, GstarCAD Mechanical, GstarCAD Architecture and DWG FastView. Pages are published under the team name; see the editorial team page for the subject areas we cover.

Because GstarCAD Academy is run by a CAD vendor, pages comparing GstarCAD with other products are not neutral. We try to state facts that can be checked, and we recommend testing your own drawings with the free trial before making a purchase decision.

Compliance & corrections

Copyright & trademarks. Vendor names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. We do not host vendor copyrighted material. See Copyright & disclaimer for details.

Takedown SLA. If a copyright owner identifies a republication concern, we will review the notice promptly and remove or revise the affected content where appropriate. See the contact path on the legal page.

Corrections. When we correct a substantive error we update the article's last-updated date. Minor fixes (typos, broken links) are made without notice.

Why we publish this in detail

A site can claim authority. A site that publishes its workflow is making that claim testable. We invite readers, vendors, and reviewers to challenge our articles — email the address on the contact page with corrections, sources, or specific concerns. We respond.
