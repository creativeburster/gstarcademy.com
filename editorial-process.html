Editorial process & sources

The human and AI-assisted workflow we use to produce CAD knowledge articles — how we research, write, review, attribute, and revise. We publish this in detail so readers can judge the trustworthiness of every page on Gstarcademy.

Last reviewed: 2026-05-24 · Owner: Gstarcademy Editorial Team

1 · Sourcing

Every knowledge article begins from primary vendor sources: official documentation (help.autodesk.com, ptc.com/en/support, plm.sw.siemens.com, 3ds.com/support, gstarcad.net), release notes, vendor blogs and recorded sessions (Autodesk University, SOLIDWORKS World, COFES, Hannover Messe presentations), and licensed reference works.

We do not republish vendor documentation. We link to it. The text on Gstarcademy is original editorial commentary written specifically for this site.

2 · Drafting (AI-assisted)

We use large language models as research and drafting accelerators. The LLM never ships content to readers unmodified. A human editor:

  • Verifies each factual claim against current vendor documentation.
  • Rewrites prose for clarity, voice consistency, and CAD-specific accuracy.
  • Adds context that the source documentation lacks (e.g., common pitfalls, decision criteria).
  • Removes hallucinated or stale information.

3 · Technical review

Each article receives a technical review by Gstarsoft Co., Ltd — a CAD software developer and the maker of GstarCAD — verifying technical claims against current vendor documentation before publication. Review is organized into domain tracks:

  • AEC / BIM — Revit, AutoCAD, Civil 3D, Navisworks, ArchiCAD, BIM standards.
  • Mechanical — SOLIDWORKS, Inventor, Fusion 360, CATIA, Creo, NX, GstarCAD Mechanical, CAM/PLM.
  • Data exchange — IFC, STEP, DWG, file format round-trips, interop standards.
  • Editorial — voice, structure, link integrity, accessibility, on-page SEO compliance.

4 · Publication & revision

Every published article carries a Last reviewed date, a publication date, and a technical-review byline crediting Gstarsoft Co., Ltd. Vendor release cycles trigger periodic re-reviews: we maintain a calendar of vendor major releases (annual for most platforms) and refresh affected articles within four weeks of release.

Reader feedback (technical inaccuracies, broken links, outdated information) is welcomed via contact. We aim to acknowledge within 5 business days.

Source attribution

Each article links externally to the primary vendor documentation, release notes, and authoritative third-party references it draws on. We never embed vendor logos, product screenshots, or material covered by copyright restrictions without explicit license. Our writing about a vendor product is original commentary; the vendor remains the authoritative source for product specifications.

Sources we use most often

  • Autodesk Help (help.autodesk.com)
  • Autodesk University recordings
  • PTC Support (support.ptc.com) and PTC University
  • Siemens Solutions Center (support.sw.siemens.com)
  • Dassault Systèmes 3DS.com product support
  • Gstarsoft support center (gstarcad.net/support)
  • buildingSMART IFC specification documents
  • ISO/STEP and PDF/3D PDF specification documents
  • Vendor partner ecosystem documentation (where directly relevant)
  • Recognized industry journals (AEC Magazine, Engineering.com)

Editorial team & technical review

Articles are written by the Gstarcademy editorial team and receive technical review by Gstarsoft Co., Ltd — a CAD software developer and the maker of GstarCAD. Review is organized into the domain tracks below, and bylines on every article credit Gstarsoft Co., Ltd as technical reviewer.

AEC / BIM track

Covers BIM, Revit, AutoCAD, Civil 3D, Navisworks, ArchiCAD, and standards-related material, verified against current vendor documentation.

Mechanical track

Covers SOLIDWORKS, Inventor, Fusion 360, CATIA, Creo, NX, GstarCAD Mechanical, and downstream CAM/PLM workflows.

Data exchange track

Covers file-format interoperability, IFC/STEP/DWG round-trips, and CAD data exchange standards.

Editorial track

Voice and structure, link integrity, accessibility, on-page SEO compliance, and overall publication discipline.

Compliance & corrections

Copyright & trademarks. Vendor names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. We do not host vendor copyrighted material. See Copyright & disclaimer for details.

Takedown SLA. If a copyright owner identifies a republication concern, we will remove or revise the affected content within 48 hours of valid notification. See the contact path on the legal page.

Corrections. Substantive corrections are logged with the article's revision history (date, summary of change). Minor fixes (typos, broken links) are made silently.

Why we publish this in detail

A site can claim authority. A site that publishes its workflow is making that claim testable. We invite readers, vendors, and reviewers to challenge our articles — email the address on the contact page with corrections, sources, or specific concerns. We respond.
