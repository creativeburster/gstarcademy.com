Editorial process & sources

The human and AI-assisted workflow we use to produce CAD knowledge articles — how we research, write, review, attribute, and revise. We publish this in detail so readers can judge the trustworthiness of every page on Gstarcademy.

Last reviewed: 2026-10-16 · Owner: Gstarcademy Technical Review Board (TRB) & Editorial Team

1 · Primary Sourcing & Standard Tracking

Every knowledge article and exam question begins from authoritative standards committees and primary vendor sources: ISO/IEC (ISO 19650, ISO 1101, ISO 21920, ISO 16739-1), ASME (ASME Y14.5, ASME B1.1), AIA, buildingSMART International, official vendor documentation (help.autodesk.com, support.sw.siemens.com, 3ds.com/support, gstarcad.net), and registered software development kits (GRX, ObjectARX, OpenFOAM, OpenColorIO).

We do not scrape or summarize secondary blog posts. We cross-reference exact standard clauses and execute real-world benchmark scripts.

2 · Drafting & Reproducibility Benchmarking

We leverage modern language models strictly as research acceleration engines. No content is published without physical bench verification. A domain engineer:

  • Runs all AutoLISP routines, C++ GRX APIs, and console commands directly in physical CAD test workstations (GstarCAD 2027 Pro, Revit, SolidWorks).
  • Verifies mathematical formulas and boundary conditions (Delaunay circumcircles, clothoids, virtual conditions).
  • Validates step-by-step diagnostic workflows against real corrupt DWG files, broken balloon references, and graphic acceleration crashes.
  • Eliminates hypothetical or untested theoretical claims.

4 · Continuous Governance & Version SLA

Every published article carries a verified Last reviewed date, explicit standards citations, and full corporate attribution to Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd. We monitor international standard updates (ISO, ASME, buildingSMART) and major CAD software annual releases, issuing refreshes within 30 days of release.

Feedback from certified engineers is tracked via our engineering review channel (contact). Inquiries are acknowledged within 2 business days.
