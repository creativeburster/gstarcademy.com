Part 1

Mobile App Fast Start & Navigation (iOS & Android)

Experience lightning-fast loading of complex structural, MEP, and civil drawings on job sites

1. Opening Large Drawings & Offline Caching

DWG FastView utilizes an independently developed multi-core mobile rendering engine that opens complex 100MB+ DWG sheets in under 3 seconds. Before traveling to remote construction sites where cellular reception is weak, tap Save Offline on any drawing from your cloud drive (Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, or FastView Cloud).

💡 Pro-Tips for Fast Navigation:
  • Model & Layout Switching: Tap the bottom bar to instantly switch between infinite Model Space and production title-block Sheet Layouts without reload delays.
  • Layer Management & Isolation: Tap the Layer icon to freeze or hide heavy background layers (like contour lines, furniture, or hatch blocks) to instantly accelerate rendering speed on low-memory mobile devices.
  • Text Search & Quick Jump: Use the global Text Search tool to immediately highlight and jump to specific column grid references (e.g. Axis A-5), room numbers, or equipment tags.
Part 2

Precision Field Measurement Suite

Never carry paper blueprints and scale rulers to the field again
📏

Linear & Continuous Distance

Activate the Distance tool. An optical magnifying loupe appears under your finger, snapping automatically to exact endpoints and intersections. For curved piping or corridor paths, use Continuous Measurement to view segment lengths and cumulative totals without resetting.

📐

Polygon Area & Perimeter

Select the Area tool and tap consecutive corner vertices of any room, floor slab, or paving boundary. DWG FastView dynamically shades the interior polygon in translucent green and reports exact surface area (m² or ft²) and boundary perimeter.

📐

Ramp Slope & Point-to-Line Distance

Use the Slope tool to calculate ramp percentages and vertical drops across two elevations. Use Point-to-Line to determine the true perpendicular offset from an isolated structural pile to any site boundary line.

⭕

Radius & Auto-Recorded History

Tap any three points along a circular curve or fillet to display exact radius and arc length. All measurement values are automatically logged in the Measurement Record Book and can be exported as a structured CSV spreadsheet.

Part 3

Next-Gen Field Markups: Video, Photo & Voice Pins

Document discrepancies and site defects with rich multimedia pinned to exact DWG coordinates

🎥 Revolutionary Video Annotation (In-App Video Pins)

Stop trying to explain complicated on-site piping leaks, structural cracks, or clearance conflicts with static text messages. With DWG FastView's exclusive Video Annotation:

  1. Select Video Annotation from the markup toolbar.
  2. Tap the exact spatial location on the drawing where the construction issue is situated.
  3. Record up to 30 seconds of high-definition video directly within the app, demonstrating the dynamic issue and walking around the physical obstacle.
  4. The video is converted into an interactive camera pin anchored onto the DWG entity. When design engineers open the file in the office, clicking the pin plays the exact site clip.

📸 Photo Pins & Audio Voice Memos

Attach high-resolution photos taken on your smartphone camera directly to drawing pins. Record contextual voice audio briefings for colleagues without typing tedious field logs.

🎨 Free Mobile Hatch Tool & Revision Clouds

Draw standard revision clouds around defect zones. Utilize the newly released 100% Free Mobile Hatch Tool to apply ANSI, concrete, and solid fills to any closed boundary on the go.

Part 4

Troubleshooting & Data Extraction Tools

Fix missing SHX fonts, extract CAD tables to Excel, and export multi-page PDFs

🔤 Fix Missing Font "???"

When drawings display unreadable question marks (???), it is caused by missing CAD shape fonts (.SHX) or Asian bigfonts. Enable Automatic Cloud Font Retrieval in DWG FastView settings, or upload your proprietary fonts to Cloud Font Storage to restore perfect legibility across all devices.

📊 CAD Table to Excel

Tired of re-typing drawing schedules? Select the Table to Excel tool, drag a window around door/window tables, rebar schedules, or BOMs on your drawing, and FastView automatically reconstructs cell rows, columns, and numbers into an editable .xlsx spreadsheet.

📄 Multi-Page PDF Export

Batch convert multiple sheet layouts or custom window crops into a single combined multi-page PDF document. Configure paper sizes (A0–A4), monochrome CTB plot styles, and high-DPI vector precision for client submittals.

Part 5

14+ 3D Formats & Web Cloud Collaboration

Inspect SolidWorks, Revit, and CATIA models on mobile, and collaborate via web without plugins

Pocket 3D Viewer: SolidWorks, CATIA, Revit & Rhino

DWG FastView has upgraded its 3D graphics pipeline to support over 14+ industrial 3D formats. View assemblies and parts directly on mobile or web without buying expensive workstation licenses:

SolidWorks (.SLDPRT, .SLDASM) Autodesk Revit (.RVT 2025) CATIA (.CATPart, .CATProduct) Rhino (.3DM) STEP / STP IGES / IGS STL / OBJ OpenBIM (.IFC)
  • 3D Dynamic Sectioning: Cut dynamic clipping planes along X, Y, and Z axes to inspect hidden internal components in complex mechanical machinery.
  • One-Tap QR Scan Login: Use the DWG FastView mobile app to scan the web login QR code on any computer to sign in immediately without remembering passwords.
  • Automated Revision Compare: Upload two drawing revisions; newly added geometry is rendered in bright Green and deleted geometry in Red.
