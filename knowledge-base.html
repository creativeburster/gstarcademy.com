Knowledge Base Philosophy: Structured Literacy

Inspired by the Structured Literacy and Atomic Knowledge approach (as seen in LearnBuffett), this hub prioritizes "the map before the app." We move beyond linear tutorials to extract core concepts—atomic units of knowledge that are linked across software and industry domains.

Every card in our Glossary and every node in our Knowledge Graph is a verified atomic unit, derived from official documentation and AI-assisted analysis (e.g., our pilot integration of GstarCAD 2026 feature sets). Our goal is to provide a mental model that persists even as your software tools change.
