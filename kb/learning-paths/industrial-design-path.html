Industrial Design & 3D Printing Learning Path

From ergonomic freeform sketching and Class-A NURBS surfacing to Design for Additive Manufacturing (DFAM), lattice structures, and 3D printing preparation.

Career Track Overview

Modern product development blends aesthetic emotional appeal with extreme manufacturing precision. This learning journey takes designers from concept sketches to production-grade curvature-continuous surfaces and cutting-edge 3D printing technologies.

Level 1: Concept Modeling & Ergonomics (Foundation)

Primary Tools: Rhino 3D (SubD), Blender, Fusion 360 (T-Splines).

  • Subdivision (SubD) Surface Modeling: Rapidly pushing and pulling organic quadrilateral polygon cages to sculpt handheld ergonomics, headphones, and automotive styling.
  • Form Transition to Brep: Converting organic SubD models into analytical solid CAD bodies without mathematical surface tearing.
  • Visual Rendering & CMF: Setting up Color, Material, and Finish (CMF) palettes, HDR lighting, and exploded assembly views for design review approval.

Key Milestone: Sculpt, convert, and render an ergonomic wireless computer mouse with battery compartment tolerances.

Level 2: High-Precision Class-A NURBS Surfacing (Intermediate)

Primary Tools: Rhino 3D, Autodesk Alias, SOLIDWORKS Advanced Surfacing.

  • Curvature Continuity (G0, G1, G2, G3): Understanding positional, tangent, curvature, and torsion continuity using zebra strip diagnostics and curvature graphs.
  • Draft Angles & Tooling Splits: Ensuring all plastic housing surfaces maintain 1.5° to 3° draft angles for injection molding die extraction.
  • Snap-Fits & Boss Design: Designing cantilever snap-fits, ultrasonic weld ribs, and screw bosses according to plastic material datasheets.

Key Milestone: Construct a G2 curvature-continuous consumer drone canopy ready for high-pressure injection molding.

Level 3: Design for Additive Manufacturing & Topology (Professional)

Primary Tools: Grasshopper, nTopology, Fusion 360 Generative Design, PrusaSlicer/Bambu Studio.

  • DFAM Rules: Designing overhang angles (<45° self-supporting), internal drain channels for resin/powder, and anisotropic layer adhesion strength.
  • Conformal Lattice Generation: Generating TPMS (triply periodic minimal surface) gyroid lattices for ultra-lightweight structural stiffness and heat exchange.
  • Mesh Repair & 3MF Slicing: Manifold checking, boolean union of multi-million polygon meshes, and color-gradient 3MF export protocols.

Key Milestone: Design a topology-optimized titanium aerospace bracket with 40% mass reduction and verified FEA factor-of-safety.
