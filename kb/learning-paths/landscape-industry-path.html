Landscape Architecture & Site Civil Path

From 2D master planning and hardscape grading to Digital Terrain Modeling (DTM), dynamic planting schedules, and stormwater management coordination.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by GstarCAD Academy Editorial Team Last reviewed 2026-09-16 Editorial Process & Testbed

Career Track Overview

Landscape architects shape the outdoor built environment, harmonizing natural ecology with architectural hardscape, pedestrian mobility, and civil stormwater hydrology. This guide outlines the essential CAD & BIM skills required from concept to construction administration.

Level 1: 2D Site Planning & Hardscape Detailing (Foundation)

Primary Tools: AutoCAD, GstarCAD Architecture, Vectorworks Landmark.

  • Site Survey Georeferencing: Aligning CAD project origins with national coordinate grid systems (State Plane / UTM) and GIS boundary shapefiles.
  • Hardscape & Paving Patterns: Dimensioned paving patterns, expansion joints, retaining wall details, and accessibility (ADA) ramp slopes.
  • Planting Schedules & Legends: Automated block counting for tree and shrub species, mature canopy spread overlays, and scientific nomenclature tagging.

Key Milestone: Produce a fully coordinated park plaza landscape plan with paving hatch patterns and dynamic plant schedules.

Level 2: 3D Terrain Grading & Stormwater Hydrology (Intermediate)

Primary Tools: Autodesk Civil 3D, Rhino 3D (Lands Design), SketchUp Pro.

  • Digital Terrain Modeling (DTM): Generating existing vs. proposed TIN surfaces from contour lines, spot elevations, and LiDAR point clouds.
  • Cut & Fill Earthwork Volumes: Calculating balanced earthwork quantities to minimize soil export costs across terraced building pads.
  • Stormwater Surface Runoff: Modeling swales, bioswales, retention ponds, and calculating overland catchment drainage flow vectors.

Key Milestone: Grade a 5-hectare commercial campus with balanced earthwork volumes and verified stormwater pond capacity.

Level 3: Landscape BIM & Microclimate Simulation (Professional)

Primary Tools: Revit (Environment for Revit), Vectorworks Landmark, Autodesk Forma.

  • Landscape Information Modeling (LIM): Associating biological growth rates, watering requirements, and carbon sequestration data to 3D plant objects.
  • Pedestrian Microclimate Comfort: Simulating seasonal tree shade canopy performance, wind protection corridors, and urban heat island mitigation.
  • IFC openBIM Coordination: Exporting site terrain, utility sleeves, and outdoor lighting into federated building models for clash audits.

Key Milestone: Deliver an openBIM federated site model passing all underground utility clash checks with full solar radiation analysis.
