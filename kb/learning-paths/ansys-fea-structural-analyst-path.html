ANSYS Mechanical FEA Structural Analyst 8-Week Path

Rigorous 8-week Finite Element Analysis (FEA) learning path in ANSYS Mechanical. Master CAD defeaturing, hex-dominant meshing, mesh convergence studies, nonlinear frictional contacts, modal/harmonic vibration, and ASME Section VIII stress linearization.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by Dr. Marcus Vance, Ph.D. (Lead Computational Mechanics & FEA/CFD Reviewer) Last reviewed 2026-10-12 Editorial Process & Testbed
Duration

8 weeks (7-9 hours / week)

Target Level

Intermediate to Advanced

Prerequisites & Setup Requirements

Solid mechanics fundamentals (stress, strain, Hooke's Law) and 3D CAD modeling proficiency.

Structured Learning Progression (Stage by Stage)

Week 1: Geometry Preparation, Defeaturing & Mid-Surface Extraction

Stage 1

Core Topics Covered:

  • Importing STEP AP242 / Parasolid assemblies into ANSYS SpaceClaim / DesignModeler
  • Removing cosmetic fillets, embossed logos, and micro-sliver faces that ruin mesh quality
  • Extracting shell mid-surfaces for thin-walled sheet metal and weldments
  • Shared Topology (conformal meshing across multi-body parts) vs Contact pairs

🛠️ Practical Hands-on Assignment: Clean and defeature a complex cast iron pump housing and extract conformal shell mid-surfaces for a welded support frame.

Key Commands / Tools: SpaceClaim, MidSurface, ShareTopology, Repair

Recommended Concepts: quality-assurance-cadiso-128-technical-drawings-rules

Week 2: Element Formulations, Hex Meshing & Mesh Quality Metrics

Stage 2

Core Topics Covered:

  • 1D Beam/Link, 2D Shell, and 3D Solid elements (SOLID186 quadratic 20-node hex vs SOLID187 10-node tet)
  • Sweep meshing, MultiZone hex-dominant decomposition, and local Body/Face/Edge sizing
  • Inflation layers and local sphere-of-influence refinement around stress risers
  • Evaluating Element Quality, Aspect Ratio (<5), Jacobian Ratio, and Skewness (<0.6)

🛠️ Practical Hands-on Assignment: Decompose a crankshaft geometry into sweepable bodies to achieve >85% hexahedral mesh coverage with maximum skewness below 0.55.

Key Commands / Tools: Mesh, Sweep, MultiZone, Sizing, Inflation

Recommended Concepts: quality-assurance-caddwg-file-format

Week 3: Boundary Conditions, Mesh Convergence & Stress Singularities

Stage 3

Core Topics Covered:

  • Applying realistic supports (Compression Only, Cylindrical, Elastic, Remote Displacement)
  • Understanding St. Venant's Principle and avoiding over-constrained rigid boundary artifacts
  • Conducting systematic h-refinement Mesh Convergence Studies (1%, 2%, 5% stress delta)
  • Distinguishing true physical peak stresses from mathematical re-entrant corner singularities

🛠️ Practical Hands-on Assignment: Execute a 4-level mesh convergence study on a notched tensile bracket and plot Maximum Principal and von Mises stress vs DOF count.

Key Commands / Tools: RemoteDisplacement, Convergence, Probe, PathPlot

Recommended Concepts: quality-assurance-cadiso-128-technical-drawings-rules

Week 4: Contact Mechanics, Bolted Joints & Pre-Tension Loads

Stage 4

Core Topics Covered:

  • Linear contacts (Bonded, No Separation) vs Nonlinear contacts (Frictionless, Rough, Frictional)
  • Contact formulations: Pure Penalty, Augmented Lagrange, and Normal Lagrange
  • Contact Pinball Radius, Initial Contact Status tool, and Chattering/Penetration control
  • Modeling structural Bolt Pretension (Load vs Adjustment lock steps) across multi-step analyses

🛠️ Practical Hands-on Assignment: Simulate a 6-bolt pressurized pipe flange assembly with Stage 1 bolt preload (45 kN) and Stage 2 internal hydro-test pressure (16 MPa).

Key Commands / Tools: ContactTool, BoltPretension, AugmentedLagrange, Substeps

Recommended Concepts: quality-assurance-cadattributes-blocks

Week 5: Nonlinear Material Plasticity & Large Deflection Buckling

Stage 5

Core Topics Covered:

  • Bilinear and Multilinear Isotropic/Kinematic Hardening (von Mises yield criterion)
  • Hyperelastic elastomer modeling (Mooney-Rivlin, Ogden, Neo-Hookean)
  • Geometric nonlinearity (`Large Deflection = On`) and Newton-Raphson force convergence plots
  • Linear Eigenvalue Buckling vs Nonlinear Riks Arc-Length post-buckling analysis

🛠️ Practical Hands-on Assignment: Perform an elasto-plastic overload and unload simulation on a steel lifting shackle to quantify permanent plastic set deformation.

Key Commands / Tools: LargeDeflection, BilinearHardening, EigenvalueBuckling, NewtonRaphsonResiduals

Recommended Concepts: quality-assurance-cadiso-128-technical-drawings-rules

Week 6: Modal, Harmonic Response & Random Vibration (PSD)

Stage 6

Core Topics Covered:

  • Free and pre-stressed Modal Analysis (extracting natural frequencies and participation factors)
  • Mode-superposition vs Full Harmonic Response under sinusoidal rotating unbalance loads
  • Random Vibration (PSD - Power Spectral Density) per MIL-STD-810 / aerospace standards
  • Response Spectrum Analysis for seismic earthquake excitation

🛠️ Practical Hands-on Assignment: Run a pre-stressed Modal and Harmonic Response analysis on an electronics PCB chassis to eliminate resonance between 20 Hz and 2,000 Hz.

Key Commands / Tools: Modal, HarmonicResponse, RandomVibration, ParticipationFactor

Recommended Concepts: quality-assurance-caddwg-file-format

Week 7: Steady-State / Transient Thermal & Thermo-Mechanical Coupling

Stage 7

Core Topics Covered:

  • Conduction, Convection film coefficients, and Thermal Radiation view factors
  • Transient thermal heat-up and cool-down duty cycles
  • One-way coupled Thermal-Structural stress analysis (importing body temperature fields)
  • Thermal expansion mismatch stresses in multi-material assemblies

🛠️ Practical Hands-on Assignment: Couple a Steady-State Thermal analysis of a disc brake rotor under heavy braking into a Static Structural simulation to evaluate thermal cracking stress.

Key Commands / Tools: SteadyStateThermal, Convection, ImportedBodyTemperature, ThermalStrain

Recommended Concepts: quality-assurance-cadiso-128-technical-drawings-rules

Week 8: Fatigue Life Prediction & ASME Stress Linearization

Stage 8

Core Topics Covered:

  • Stress-Life (S-N High-Cycle) and Strain-Life (e-N Low-Cycle) fatigue evaluation
  • Mean stress corrections (Goodman, Soderberg, Gerber) and Rainflow cycle counting
  • Defining Stress Classification Lines (SCL) through pressure vessel wall thicknesses
  • Decomposing Primary Membrane ($P_m$), Primary Bending ($P_b$), and Secondary ($Q$) stresses per ASME Section VIII

🛠️ Practical Hands-on Assignment: Perform ASME Section VIII Div 2 stress linearization across a nozzle-to-shell weld junction and verify a 1,000,000-cycle fatigue life.

Key Commands / Tools: FatigueTool, LinearizedEquivalentStress, ConstructionGeometry, Path

Recommended Concepts: quality-assurance-cadiso-128-technical-drawings-rules

Capstone Graduation Portfolio Project

Pressure Vessel & Crane Boom Nonlinear FEA Verification Report: Perform a mesh-converged nonlinear elasto-plastic and bolted-contact simulation in ANSYS Mechanical, execute stress linearization along critical weld paths per ASME BPVC Section VIII Div 2, and validate buckling load multipliers.
