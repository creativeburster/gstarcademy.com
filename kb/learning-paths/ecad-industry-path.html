Electrical & ECAD Engineering Learning Path

From single-line electrical schematics and cabinet layout standards to multi-layer PCB design and synchronized 3D ECAD-MCAD enclosure fitment.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-09-16 Editorial process

Career Track Overview

Electrical and Electronic CAD (ECAD) forms the nervous system of modern industrial machinery, building power distribution, and smart hardware products. This roadmap bridges industrial control panel schematics with high-density electronics and mechanical enclosures.

Level 1: Electrical Schematics & Drafting Standards (Foundation)

Primary Tools: AutoCAD Electrical, GstarCAD Electrical, ZWCAD Electrical.

  • Single-Line & Ladder Diagrams: Master IEC 60617 and ANSI/IEEE standard graphical symbols for circuit breakers, relays, contactors, and terminal blocks.
  • Wire Tagging & Cross-Referencing: Automated real-time wire numbering, parent/child contact associations, and multi-sheet terminal jump tables.
  • Control Cabinet Enclosure Layout: 2D physical footprint placement on DIN rails, wire duct fill calculations, and terminal strip report generation.

Key Milestone: Produce a complete 10-sheet industrial motor control center (MCC) drawing set with dynamic BOM and panel wiring reports.

Level 2: PCB Layout & High-Speed Electronics (Intermediate)

Primary Tools: Altium Designer, KiCAD, Autodesk Fusion 360 Electronics.

  • Schematic Capture & Footprint Libraries: Defining IPC-7351 compliant component footprints, pin assignments, and SPICE simulation nets.
  • Multi-Layer Routing & Design Rules: Trace impedance matching, ground planes, differential pair routing, and signal integrity considerations.
  • Design for Manufacturing (DFM): Generating Gerber X2, drill files, Pick-and-Place data, and IPC-2581 open exchange datasets for fabrication.

Key Milestone: Design and verify a 4-layer microcontroller breakout board passing comprehensive DRC design rule checks.

Level 3: 3D ECAD-MCAD Co-Design & Harness Routing (Professional)

Primary Tools: Altium CoDesigner, SOLIDWORKS Electrical 3D, Siemens NX Wiring Harness.

  • Bidirectional Push-Pull: Real-time synchronization of rigid-flex PCB boards, keep-out volumes, and component heights directly into mechanical assemblies.
  • 3D Electrical Wire Harness Routing: Routing physical cable looms through mechanical chassis, calculating exact cut lengths and bend radii.
  • Thermal & EMC Validation: Verifying airflow clearance and heat sink placement against mechanical enclosure constraints.

Key Milestone: Complete a validated mechatronics product assembly with zero mechanical interference and live connector wire tables.
