FreeCAD Beginner Learning Path

4-week FreeCAD learning path for beginners. Learn parametric modeling, Part Design workbench, assemblies, and technical drawings in the free open-source CAD platform.

Prerequisites: Basic computer skills

This structured learning path for FreeCAD is designed to take you from beginner to beginner level through progressive stages. Each stage builds on the previous one, so follow them in order for best results.

Learning Stages

Stage 1: Week 1: Getting Started

  • Workbench concept
  • Navigation and views
  • Document structure
  • Sketch constraints

addon-manager bim-workbench

Stage 2: Week 2: Part Design

  • Sketcher workbench
  • Pad, pocket, revolve
  • Fillets and chamfers
  • Boolean operations

Related concepts:

fem-workbench path-workbench

Stage 3: Week 3: Assembly & TechDraw

  • Assembly workbenches (A2plus/Assembly4)
  • TechDraw for 2D documentation
  • Dimensions and annotations
  • Export to PDF/DXF

Related concepts:

assembly-workbenches-fc techdraw-workbench

Stage 4: Week 4: Scripting & BIM

  • Python macro basics
  • Custom object creation
  • BIM workbench intro
  • IFC export

Related concepts:

bim-workbench draft-workbench

Tips for Success

  • Practice daily: Even 30 minutes of hands-on practice is more effective than hours of passive watching
  • Use real projects: Apply each stage's skills to a personal or work project immediately
  • Join communities: FreeCAD forums and Discord/Reddit communities accelerate learning through peer support
  • Review our tutorials: Visit the tutorial library for curated video content matching each stage

Next Steps After Completion

Once you complete this path, consider:

  • Exploring further FreeCAD features through our FreeCAD knowledge articles
  • Taking vendor certification exams to validate your skills
  • Contributing to your team's template and standards library
  • Exploring related software through our comparison guides
