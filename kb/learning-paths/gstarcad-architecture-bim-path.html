GstarCAD Architecture Parametric AEC 6-Week Path

Master parametric architectural design in GstarCAD Architecture over 6 weeks. Learn multi-layer wall systems, intelligent doors/windows, custom stairs, associative 3D roof framing, automatic elevations/sections, and room area schedules.

Curriculum Director: Gstarcademy Engineering Editorial Board Updated: 2026-10-12
Duration

6 weeks (6-8 hours / week)

Target Level

Beginner to Advanced

Self-Assessment

Take CAD Quiz →

Prerequisites & Setup Requirements

Basic 2D CAD familiarity and understanding of architectural floor plans.

Structured Learning Progression (Stage by Stage)

Week 1: Architectural Column Grids & Parametric Multi-Layer Walls

Stage 1

Core Topics Covered:

  • Multi-axis rectangular and radial structural column grids with automatic bubble numbering
  • Parametric composite walls (structural core, thermal insulation, exterior cladding)
  • Automatic wall intersection cleanup (T-junctions, L-corners) and baseline justification
  • Standard structural columns, beams, and foundation slabs

🛠️ Practical Hands-on Assignment: Lay out a 7x5 structural column grid and enclose the ground floor with 300mm insulated cavity exterior walls and 120mm interior partitions.

Key Commands / Tools: AXISGRID, WALL, COLUMN, BEAM, SLAB

Recommended Concepts: layers-gstarcaddwg-file-format

Week 2: Intelligent Doors, Windows & Curtain Wall Assemblies

Stage 2

Core Topics Covered:

  • Parametric door and window insertion with automatic wall opening healing
  • Bay windows, corner windows, ribbon windows, and skylight objects
  • Customizing sill heights, frame profiles, swing directions, and associative glazing tags
  • Automated Door & Window Schedule tables linked live to floor plan geometry

🛠️ Practical Hands-on Assignment: Populate the floor plan with fire-rated stairwell doors, double-leaf entry systems, and generate a live Door/Window Schedule table.

Key Commands / Tools: DOOR, WINDOW, WINLABEL, DWSCHEDULE

Recommended Concepts: dynamic-blocks-autocadattributes-blocks

Week 3: Parametric Stairs, Ramps, Railings & Elevators

Stage 3

Core Topics Covered:

  • Straight, L-shaped, U-shaped, scissor, and spiral stair generators
  • Building code riser/tread ergonomy checks and stairwell slab cutouts
  • Accessible ADA ramps, balconies, handrails, and elevator shaft modules
  • Multi-story vertical circulation coordination across floor levels

🛠️ Practical Hands-on Assignment: Model two fire-escape scissor stairwells and a lobby spiral feature staircase with associative glass balustrades.

Key Commands / Tools: STAIR, RAMP, RAILING, ELEVATOR, BALCONY

Recommended Concepts: annotative-scaling-model-paper-spaceiso-128-technical-drawings-rules

Week 4: Complex Roof Systems, Dormers & Skylights

Stage 4

Core Topics Covered:

  • Pitched, hip, gable, mansard, and multi-slope roof generation from boundary polylines
  • Adjusting roof pitch angles, eave overhangs, fascia profiles, and rafters
  • Automatic wall-to-roof trimming and dormer window integration
  • Parapet walls, roof drainage slopes, and waterproofing detail callouts

🛠️ Practical Hands-on Assignment: Construct a multi-gable roof with 30-degree pitch, 600mm eaves, and three pitched dormer windows over the top floor.

Key Commands / Tools: ROOF, DORMER, SKYLIGHT, WALLROOFTRIM

Recommended Concepts: dwg-file-formatlayers-gstarcad

Week 5: Room Spaces, Area Calculation & Interior Finish Schedules

Stage 5

Core Topics Covered:

  • Automatic room boundary detection from enclosing walls and virtual room dividers
  • Net Floor Area (NFA) vs Gross Floor Area (GFA) calculation rules
  • Room tags displaying room name, number, floor material, and ceiling height
  • Exporting master Room Area & Finish Schedule tables to Excel

🛠️ Practical Hands-on Assignment: Tag all 28 rooms on the ground and first floors and produce a code-compliant Gross/Net Area Summary Schedule.

Key Commands / Tools: SPACE, ROOMTAG, AREATABLE, SPACEBOUNDARY

Recommended Concepts: attributes-blocksannotative-objects

Week 6: Multi-Floor Assembly, Automated Elevations & Sections

Stage 6

Core Topics Covered:

  • Project Manager floor stacking (Basement, Ground, Standard Floors, Roof)
  • Generating 2D Exterior Elevations directly from the assembled 3D building model
  • Cutting live Building Sections with automatic structural slab/wall hatching
  • 3D IFC/SKP export and multi-sheet A1 construction document plotting

🛠️ Practical Hands-on Assignment: Assemble all 3 floors into a master 3D building model, generate North/South elevations and Section A-A, and publish to PDF.

Key Commands / Tools: BLDGMANAGER, ELEVATION, SECTION, HLR, PUBLISH

Recommended Concepts: paper-space-autocad

Capstone Graduation Portfolio Project

Multi-Story Commercial Office Building Set: Model a 3-story commercial building with composite cavity walls, curtain wall facades, egress stairs, hipped roofs, automated longitudinal/transverse sections, and dynamic room finish schedules.
