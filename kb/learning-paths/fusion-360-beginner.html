Fusion 360 Beginner Learning Path

5-week Fusion 360 learning path covering cloud-native CAD fundamentals, parametric modeling, assembly design, rendering, and basic CAM toolpaths.

Prerequisites: Basic computer skills, Autodesk account

This structured learning path for Fusion 360 is designed to take you from beginner to developing level through progressive stages. Each stage builds on the previous one, so follow them in order for best results.

Learning Stages

Stage 1: Week 1: Cloud Workspace

  • Data panel and projects
  • Design timeline
  • Sketch constraints
  • Parametric dimensions

  • Direct modeling (push/pull)
  • Sculpt (T-splines)
  • Component vs body

Tips for Success

  • Practice daily: Even 30 minutes of hands-on practice is more effective than hours of passive watching
  • Use real projects: Apply each stage's skills to a personal or work project immediately
  • Join communities: Fusion 360 forums and Discord/Reddit communities accelerate learning through peer support
  • Review our tutorials: Visit the tutorial library for curated video content matching each stage

Next Steps After Completion

Once you complete this path, consider:

  • Exploring further Fusion 360 features through our Fusion 360 knowledge articles
  • Taking vendor certification exams to validate your skills
  • Contributing to your team's template and standards library
  • Exploring related software through our comparison guides
