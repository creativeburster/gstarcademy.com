Career Path Overview

This structured progression guides aspiring BIM Coordinators, Architectural Technology Specialists, and Structural CAD Technicians through the essential tools, standards, and practical skills required in modern AEC delivery.

Level 1: 2D Drafting & CAD Standards (Foundation)

Estimated: 4 Weeks

Master architectural 2D drafting conventions, layer naming, dimension styles, and external references (Xrefs) using industry-standard platforms (AutoCAD / GstarCAD).

Core Knowledge Nodes:

  • GstarCAD Platform & Engine
  • AutoCAD Geometric Constraints
  • Action Recorder Automation

Level 2: Parametric BIM Modeling & Information Delivery

Estimated: 6 Weeks

Transition from 2D lines to 3D smart parametric objects in Revit. Understand Level of Development (LOD 100-400), family editing, and BIM Execution Plans (BEP).

Core Knowledge Nodes:

  • Revit Adaptive Components
  • BIM Execution Plan (BEP)
  • ACC BIM Collaborate CDE

Level 3: Multi-Discipline Clash Coordination & 4D/5D BIM

Estimated: 6 Weeks

Lead inter-disciplinary clash detection (Navisworks / Solibri), export openBIM IFC4.3 models, link construction schedules (4D), and extract quantities for cost estimation (5D).

Core Knowledge Nodes:

  • 4D & 5D BIM Integration
  • openBIM IFC4.3 Schemas
