Product Overview

HousePlan is a residential architecture design tool in the Gstarsoft portfolio, tailored for home builders, interior designers, and small architectural practices. It provides specialized tools for floor-plan layout, room design, and residential construction documentation.

Unlike general-purpose BIM tools, HousePlan focuses on the residential workflow — room templates, door and window placement, staircase design, and automatic generation of construction-ready plans compliant with regional building standards. The tool integrates with GstarCAD for users who need additional drafting capabilities beyond residential design.

Key Features

  • Residential-focused tools: Room layout templates, residential door/window libraries, and staircase generators optimized for home design workflows.
  • Automated documentation: Auto-generated room schedules, area calculations, and elevation views from the floor plan.
  • Regional compliance: Support for local building-code requirements in dimensional standards, fire-egress rules, and accessibility guidelines.

Frequently asked questions (3)

How do I export Houseplan files to GstarCAD?

Use the Export command and select DWG format. Intelligent walls and massing elements will convert to standard 2D/3D entities.

Can I import custom material texture files?

Yes, in the Material panel, add a new material and browse to import PNG/JPG files for diffuse and normal mappings.

How to create visual animations of the 3D model in Houseplan?

Set up multiple camera viewpoints along a path, and click Export Video in the presentation panel to render a walk-through.

Competitive Positioning

HousePlan targets the residential design market, competing with Home Designer (Chief Architect), SketchUp, Planner 5D, and Cedreo in the consumer-to-professional residential design segment.

vs. Chief Architect: HousePlan provides a lower-cost, faster-learning alternative focused exclusively on residential floor plans. Chief Architect offers deeper architectural detailing but requires more training and investment.

vs. SketchUp: While SketchUp is general-purpose 3D, HousePlan specializes in floor plan workflows with built-in room templates, wall auto-detection, and residential furniture libraries—reducing the setup work needed for house planning.

vs. Online planners: Unlike browser-based tools (Planner 5D, RoomSketcher), HousePlan provides CAD-accurate dimensions and DWG export capability for professional handoff to architects and contractors.

Use Cases & Industry Applications

  • Homeowner pre-design: Homeowners create floor plan concepts before engaging architects, saving consultation time by clearly communicating spatial requirements and preferences.
  • Real estate staging: Agents create virtual floor plans for property listings, improving buyer engagement with clear spatial understanding before physical viewings.
  • Renovation planning: Contractors and homeowners document existing layouts and test renovation scenarios (wall removal, room expansion, bathroom relocation) before committing to construction.
  • Interior design: Designers experiment with furniture placement, circulation paths, and spatial proportions in 2D plan view before developing detailed 3D visualizations.
  • Building permit sketches: Small residential projects in many jurisdictions accept well-dimensioned floor plans for permit applications without requiring full architectural drawings.
