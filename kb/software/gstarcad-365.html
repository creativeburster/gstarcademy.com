Product Overview

GstarCAD 365 is Gstarsoft's cloud-connected subscription edition of GstarCAD, providing DWG-compatible 2D/3D drafting with cloud storage, collaboration features, and cross-device access. It bridges the gap between traditional desktop CAD and cloud-based workflows, allowing teams to share drawings and collaborate without manual file transfers.

Unlike standalone GstarCAD, GstarCAD 365 includes cloud-based project management, drawing version history, and team collaboration tools. The subscription model provides automatic updates and flexible licensing that scales with team size.

Key Features

  • Cloud storage and sync: Drawings stored in the cloud with automatic version history and cross-device synchronization.
  • Team collaboration: Share drawings with team members and external reviewers with permission controls and markup capabilities.
  • Subscription flexibility: Flexible licensing without upfront perpetual-license costs, with automatic updates to the latest version.

Frequently asked questions (3)

How do I share a drawing link with external clients?

Select the file in the project manager, click Share, set permissions (view-only or edit-enabled), set expiration date, and copy the link.

Can I view version history and restore old revisions?

Yes, GstarCAD 365 maintains a complete activity log. You can compare any two version revisions visually and restore older versions as the active drawing master.

Does GstarCAD 365 support offline editing?

Yes, drawings can be checked out for offline work. Once you reconnect, changes are merged back as a new revision.

Competitive Positioning

GstarCAD 365 is the cloud-subscription variant of GstarCAD, competing with AutoCAD Web App, DraftSight cloud, and ARES Kudo in the browser-based and subscription CAD market.

vs. AutoCAD Web App: GstarCAD 365 provides full desktop-class DWG editing at a fraction of AutoCAD's subscription cost. While AutoCAD Web is limited to basic editing, GstarCAD 365 includes the complete command set and API support of the desktop version.

vs. traditional perpetual licenses: The 365 subscription model provides always-current software with automatic updates, cloud storage integration, and flexible monthly/annual billing—attractive for project-based firms with fluctuating seat requirements.

Deployment flexibility: GstarCAD 365 supports both online activation (connected licensing) and offline periodic validation, accommodating teams that work on job sites or restricted networks where constant internet access isn't available.

Use Cases & Industry Applications

  • Flexible team scaling: Engineering firms add or remove CAD seats monthly based on project load, avoiding the capital expenditure of perpetual licenses for temporary staff or seasonal demand.
  • Multi-site operations: Teams across multiple offices share a subscription pool, with users activating licenses as needed without hardware-locked dongles or fixed-seat assignments.
  • Always-current compliance: Government and infrastructure projects requiring specific software version compliance benefit from automatic updates that maintain certification status.
  • Startup and SME adoption: Small firms access professional DWG editing without the upfront investment of perpetual CAD licenses, converting capital expenditure to operational expenditure.
  • Remote workforce: Distributed teams activate their subscription from any workstation, enabling work-from-home and client-site flexibility without license transfer procedures.
