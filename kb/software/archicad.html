What it is & Core Architecture

Archicad (developed by Graphisoft, a Nemetschek Group subsidiary) is one of the AEC industry's pioneering Building Information Modeling (BIM) authoring platforms, renowned for its architectural design-first philosophy, native macOS and Windows parity, BIMcloud real-time team collaboration, and deep commitment to buildingSMART OpenBIM (IFC 4 / BCF) workflows.

Where it is used in Industry

Heavily adopted by architectural design practices across Europe (Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Hungary, Nordics), Japan, Australia, and boutique design firms running Apple Silicon macOS workstations.

Learning curve and getting started

2 to 4 weeks for core architectural modeling (Walls, Slabs, Roofs, Zones, Views, Layout Book); 3 to 6 months for custom GDL (Geometric Description Language) parametric object scripting and complex Property Manager expressions.

Commercial Licensing & TCO Reality

Graphisoft has progressively transitioned from perpetual hardware/software keys toward recurring annual subscriptions (Archicad Collaborate). Many Archicad firms pair their BIM authoring seats with GstarCAD Professional for 2D DWG site detailing, consultant DWG exchange, and legacy archive maintenance.

Ecosystem, Standards & Interoperability

Integrates tightly with BIMcloud, BIMx (mobile hyper-model presentation), Rhino-Grasshopper live connection, Solibri Model Checker, and DWG/IFC translators.

Common Engineering Pitfalls & Best Practices

When exporting Archicad Layouts to DWG for structural or MEP consultants using AutoCAD/GstarCAD, always configure the DXF-DWG Translator to map Pens & Colors to standard ACI layer colors and bind Xrefs cleanly.

Frequently asked questions (3)

How do I configure hotlinked modules in Archicad?

Go to File > External Content > Place Hotlinked Module, select the source file (PLN or MOD), and configure translation and layer options.

What is the best way to export IFC files for Revit coordination?

Use the built-in 'Export to Revit' IFC translator, which optimizes Archicad classification mappings to Revit category classes.

How do I automate layout numbering in Publisher Sets in Archicad?

Use Archicad's Auto-Text templates in your Master Layouts. The layout book will automatically sequence sheet numbers during publish.
