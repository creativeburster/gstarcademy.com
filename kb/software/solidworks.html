SOLIDWORKS

Mid-Market MCAD Standard ·苏州浩辰协作同级参考

Dassault Systèmes · Status: Enriched

SOLIDWORKS is the global benchmark for 3D parametric mechanical computer-aided design (MCAD). It enables engineers to construct models, simulate structural behaviors, build complex assemblies, and output production-ready 2D manufacturing drawings.

Core Technical Architecture

SOLIDWORKS differs fundamentally from traditional 2D drafting packages by utilizing a parametric, history-based modeling methodology:

  • Parametric Engine: Geometry is driven by dimensions, relations, and numeric equations. Modifying a dimension propagates changes throughout parts and related drawings automatically.
  • Feature-Based Construction: Models are built incrementally by applying features (e.g., Extrude, Revolve, Sweep, Fillet) onto 2D sketches.
  • History-Tree Hierarchy: Features are recorded sequentially in the FeatureManager design tree. Designers can roll back the history axis to insert or alter features.

Primary Toolsets & Modules

  • Part Modeling: Basic solid extrusion, advanced loft/sweep, sheet metal folding/unfolding, and structural weldment cut lists.
  • Assembly Design: Combining parts using spatial relationships (Mates) like Coincident, Concentric, and Distance to simulate real-world mechanisms.
  • Technical Drawings: Automatic projection of 3D models into orthographic views, section cuts, detailing, and automated Bill of Materials (BOM) creation.
  • Simulation (SIMULIA Core): Built-in Finite Element Analysis (FEA) for linear static stress, fatigue, fluid dynamics, and thermal expansion.

Self-Study Roadmap & Best Practices

Follow a structured progression to master SOLIDWORKS efficiently without developing poor design habits:

  1. Sketching discipline: Always fully constrain (black geometry) sketches before extruding. Utilize relations (Tangent, Equal, Symmetric) instead of hardcoded numbers wherever possible.
  2. Feature reuse: Prefer Mirror and Pattern commands rather than sketching repeated components individually to keep model sizes lightweight.
  3. Avoid Parent-Child loops: Build geometry from stable references (like the default Top, Front, Right planes) rather than nested model faces that might change or get deleted.

Ecosystem Positioning

Understand where SOLIDWORKS sits within the engineering software landscape:

  • AutoCAD vs SOLIDWORKS: AutoCAD excels at universal 2D schematic layouts and architectural drafting. SOLIDWORKS is purpose-built for 3D physical product engineering and complex mechanical assemblies.
  • SOLIDWORKS vs CATIA: Both owned by Dassault Systèmes, but CATIA is scaled for massive aerospace and automotive OEMs requiring complex Class-A surface aesthetics and multi-disciplinary systems engineering. SOLIDWORKS serves the mainstream discrete machinery and consumer device markets.

Typical Outputs & Exchange

  • SLDPRT: Native part file containing parametric features.
  • SLDASM: Native assembly file linking related parts.
  • SLDDRW: Native 2D manufacturing drawings.
  • STEP (AP203/AP214): Standard universal exchange format for 3D geometry transfer.
