What it is & Core Architecture

GstarBIM is Gstarsoft's next-generation 3D Building Information Modeling (BIM) authoring and coordination platform. Engineered on an independent high-capacity 3D parametric graphics kernel, GstarBIM handles ultra-large-scale architectural, structural, and MEP models containing hundreds of thousands of BIM components with smooth real-time navigation and native IFC 2x3 / IFC 4 interoperability.

Where it is used in Industry

Used by large architectural design institutes, hospital/airport infrastructure teams, and MEP coordination contractors seeking high-capacity BIM modeling and local drawing standard compliance.

Learning curve and getting started

Combines parametric family modeling (similar to Revit workflows) with direct CAD-style precision drafting habits, allowing CAD technicians to transition into full 3D BIM authoring in 2 to 3 weeks.

Commercial Licensing & TCO Reality

Provides a cost-effective, domestically and internationally supported BIM alternative with flexible enterprise licensing and full OpenBIM standards compliance.

Ecosystem, Standards & Interoperability

Supports multi-disciplinary worksharing, clash detection, automated 2D construction sheet extraction from 3D BIM views, quantity takeoff schedules, and bidirectional GstarCAD DWG linking.

Common Engineering Pitfalls & Best Practices

Establish a unified BIM Execution Plan (BEP) and shared project base point coordinates before linking architectural, structural, and MEP discipline models.

Frequently asked questions (3)

How do I align BIM coordinate systems with standard DWG files?

Use the Project Base Point tool to register the origin coordinates and rotation angle relative to the survey point before starting modeling.

What IFC schemas are supported for export in GstarBIM?

GstarBIM supports IFC2x3 and IFC4 schemas. Choose the coordination view template recommended by your project team.

Can I create schedules that update automatically?

Yes, BIM schedules are linked to object metadata. Modifying doors, walls, or windows updates the quantities in real time.
