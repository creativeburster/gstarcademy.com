Reality Modeling

ContextCapture

Photos to 3D: Creating precise, textured reality meshes that provide the perfect real-world context for every engineering design.

The Real-World Context

Engineering doesn't happen in a vacuum. Designers need to know exactly what is on the site. ContextCapture uses photogrammetry to turn standard digital photographs (taken from drones or handheld cameras) into high-resolution, georeferenced 3D meshes. This allows engineers to see their 3D designs overlaid perfectly onto the current 'As-Is' state of the world.

Core Reality Tools

  • Automated Processing: Upload photos and let the engine automatically reconstruct the 3D geometry and texture.
  • Hybrid Point Clouds: Combine photogrammetry with LiDAR point clouds for even higher geometric accuracy.
  • Georeferencing: Automatically align the mesh to global coordinate systems (WGS84, etc.) for precise engineering.
  • Scale & Measure: Perform highly accurate distance, area, and volume measurements directly on the 3D reality mesh.

Decision Support

ContextCapture eliminates the need for expensive traditional surveys in early project stages and provides a powerful visual tool for public consultations and stakeholder approvals.
