Inspection (Quality)

SOLIDWORKS Inspection for generating inspection reports, ballooning drawings, and first-article inspection documentation.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by GstarCAD Academy Editorial Team Last reviewed 2026-08-16 Editorial Process & Testbed

Definition & Context

SOLIDWORKS Inspection automates first-article and in-process inspection documentation: it balloons drawings (numbering each characteristic) and generates inspection reports (AS9102, PPAP, or custom) with nominal, tolerance, and measurement columns extracted from the drawing or PDF.

It reads dimensions and GD&T from SOLIDWORKS drawings or imported PDFs/legacy drawings and can import measured values (including from CMM) into the report. It is a quality-documentation add-in, not a metrology tool itself.

Why It Matters

Auto-ballooning and report generation remove the slow, error-prone manual transcription of characteristics into inspection sheets — a major time saver for regulated aerospace/medical work.

Consistent, standard-compliant reports (AS9102/PPAP) are often a customer requirement, so automating them cuts both effort and rejection risk.

Best Practices

  • Balloon from the source drawing/PDF so characteristics match the released document
  • Use the correct report template (AS9102/PPAP) for the customer requirement
  • Import measured values (e.g., from CMM) rather than retyping them
  • Keep the inspection project linked to the released drawing revision

Common Pitfalls

  • Ballooning an out-of-date drawing revision
  • Manually retyping measurements, reintroducing transcription errors
  • Confusing Inspection (documentation) with actual metrology

Common Failure Scenarios & Troubleshooting

Failure / Geometric IssueRoot Cause & Mitigation Strategy
Surface triangulation bridges across steep ravines incorrectlyAdd linear Breaklines along tops/toes of slopes or specify 'Maximum Triangle Length' under Surface Build Properties.
Horizontal curve radius triggers AASHTO violation flagsAdjust minimum transition spiral length or increase curve radius to meet minimum design speed superelevation criteria.
Coordinates offset by several meters after CAD/GIS importVerify projection datum and false easting/northing parameters in MAPCSASSIGN before importing geospatial shapefiles.
