Hydraulic Engineering

WaterGEMS

The digital pulse of the city: Advanced hydraulic modeling for water distribution, fire flow, and energy optimization.

Unified Network Analysis

WaterGEMS is the premier solution for designing and analyzing water distribution networks. It allows engineers to create a complete hydraulic model of a city's water infrastructure, from reservoirs and pumps to the pipes in every street. It is used to ensure adequate water pressure, maintain water quality (water age), and optimize pump energy consumption.

Hydraulic Power Tools

  • Fire Flow Analysis: Verify that the network can provide enough water to hydrants for firefighting during emergencies.
  • Leakage Detection: Use hydraulic anomalies to identify potential pipe bursts or hidden leaks in the system.
  • Energy Optimization: Model pump schedules to minimize electricity costs while maintaining service levels.
  • GIS & SCADA Integration: Link the model to live sensor data (SCADA) and mapping data (ArcGIS) for a real-time 'Digital Twin.'

Resilient Infrastructure

WaterGEMS helps utilities provide clean, safe water to millions of people while reducing operational costs and ensuring long-term infrastructure resilience.
