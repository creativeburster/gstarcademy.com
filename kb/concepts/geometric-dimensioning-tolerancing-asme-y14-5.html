Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (ASME Y14.5)

A symbolic engineering language defining the allowable variation in form, orientation, location, and runout of manufacturing features relative to true datums.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-09-30 Editorial process

1. Definition & Engineering Significance

ASME Y14.5 establishes the definitive mathematical foundation for GD&T. Unlike legacy plus-minus coordinate dimensioning, GD&T guarantees interchangeability and maximizes manufacturing tolerance zones by utilizing cylindrical tolerance fields.

2. Key Technical Characteristics & Rules

  • Feature Control Frame: Structured callout containing geometric characteristic symbol, tolerance value, material modifiers, and primary/secondary/tertiary datums.
  • Elimination of Tolerance Accumulation: Tolerances are tied directly to functional mating surfaces rather than arbitrary coordinate edges.
  • Bonus Tolerance with MMC: Feature size variation directly expands allowable geometric positional error when Maximum Material Condition is invoked.

3. Feature Control Frames & Tolerance Categories

ASME Y14.5 groups geometric tolerances into five distinct functional categories indicated by universal drafting symbols:

  • Form Tolerances: Straightness, Flatness, Circularity, and Cylindricity (evaluated without reference to datums).
  • Orientation Tolerances: Perpendicularity, Parallelism, and Angularity (referenced to datums).
  • Location Tolerances: Position, Concentricity, and Symmetry (controlling feature spacing and alignment).
  • Runout & Profile: Circular/Total Runout and Profile of a Line/Surface, controlling complex geometry envelopes.

4. Drawing Review Checklist & Shop-Floor Verification

When authoring GD&T callouts in GstarCAD or AutoCAD, ensure that all basic dimensions (enclosed in rectangular boxes) link to true datum features. Common errors include placing datum symbols on non-repeatable surfaces or applying Maximum Material Condition modifiers to runout or form callouts where size compensation is mathematically invalid.
