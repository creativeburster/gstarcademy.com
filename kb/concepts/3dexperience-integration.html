3DEXPERIENCE Integration

Connecting 2D drafting to the world's most powerful business innovation platform for enterprise-wide collaboration.

Data Without Boundaries

DraftSight is no longer just a local desktop application. Through the 3DEXPERIENCE platform, your DWG data is stored in a secure cloud 'Space'. This allows stakeholders from engineering, procurement, and management to view, markup, and approve drawings from any device.

Key Integration Values

  • Single Source of Truth: Everyone works on the latest version of the drawing, eliminating 'final_v2_new' file naming issues.
  • Lifecycle Management: Track drawings through 'In Work', 'Frozen', and 'Released' states.
  • Task Management: Assign drafting tasks to team members directly within the DraftSight interface.
  • Communities: Share designs and gather feedback in dedicated social communities (3DSwym).

PLM for Everyone

This integration brings high-end Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) to the 2D drafting world, a level of control usually reserved for complex 3D platforms like SOLIDWORKS and CATIA.
