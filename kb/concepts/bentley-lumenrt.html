Project Visualization

LumenRT

Bringing designs to life: Immersive real-time visualization that communicates project intent to every stakeholder.

The Power of Storytelling

Infrastructure projects are often difficult for non-engineers to visualize. Bentley LumenRT solves this by turning complex CAD and BIM models into beautiful, cinematic environments. It can handle massive 'LiveCubes' with thousands of animated trees, moving traffic, and realistic lighting, allowing designers to present their projects in a way that truly resonates with the public.

Key Visual Features

  • LiveCubes: Export self-contained 3D environments that anyone can fly through using a mouse or VR headset.
  • Dynamic Environments: Add wind-swept vegetation, moving clouds, and realistic water reflections with a single click.
  • Traffic Simulation: Link VISSIM or other traffic data to visualize real vehicle and pedestrian flow around the project.
  • Real-Time Rendering: See changes to lighting and materials instantly, without the need for hours of rendering time.

Stakeholder Approval

LumenRT bridges the gap between engineering and art, helping project teams gain stakeholder buy-in and public support for the world's most ambitious infrastructure projects.
