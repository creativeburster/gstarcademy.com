Generative Design

Transitioning from 'drawing a solution' to 'defining the problem' and letting AI simulate thousands of optimized outcomes.

The Algorithmic Approach

Generative Design in Fusion uses cloud-based solvers to explore potential solutions for a design challenge. By inputting goals (weight reduction, factor of safety) and constraints (material, manufacturing method like CNC or 3D Printing), the engine generates high-performance geometry that often mimics organic, biological structures.

Core Concepts

  • Preserve Geometry: Parts of the model that MUST remain (e.g., bolt holes, mounting surfaces).
  • Obstacle Geometry: Areas the design MUST NOT enter (e.g., clearance for moving parts).
  • Manufacturing Constraints: Ensuring the result can actually be made (Milling, Casting, Die Casting).
  • Synthesis: The process of combining multiple load cases into a single optimized part.

Important Warning

Most generative solvers use linear-static Finite Element Analysis (FEA). Always perform a separate non-linear or buckling simulation if the generated parts will be subjected to complex or high-compression loads.
