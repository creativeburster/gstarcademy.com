Mold Wizard

NX Mold Wizard for injection mold design: parting, core/cavity, sliders, lifters, and standard mold base libraries.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-07-28 Editorial process

Definition & Context

NX Mold Wizard is a specialized injection-mold-design application inside NX: it analyzes and scales the part for shrinkage, builds parting surfaces and core/cavity, and adds mold-base, sliders, lifters, ejector, cooling, and runner systems from standard vendor libraries (DME, HASCO, and others).

It automates the full moldbase assembly with associative links to the product model, going well beyond generic core/cavity extraction.

Why It Matters

Mold Wizard compresses mold design by automating parting, standard components, and the moldbase, so mold makers configure rather than model every plate and pin.

Standard-library components plus associativity keep the tool design consistent and updatable when the part changes.

Best Practices

  • Set the correct shrinkage and pull direction before parting
  • Use standard mold-base and component libraries for manufacturable, consistent tooling
  • Design cooling and ejection deliberately, not as an afterthought
  • Keep associativity to the product so part edits propagate

Common Pitfalls

  • Wrong shrinkage or pull direction baked into the tool
  • Incomplete parting/shut-off surfaces stalling core/cavity
  • Hand-modeling components that belong in standard libraries
