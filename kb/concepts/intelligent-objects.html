Intelligent Objects

Data-rich, parametric components that understand their role and relationship within a design ecosystem.

Geometry vs. Intelligence

In basic CAD, a door is just four lines and an arc. In GstarCAD Architecture or Mechanical, a door (or a gear) is an Intelligent Object. It contains metadata (dimensions, material, manufacturer) and logic (it knows it must be hosted by a wall).

Behaviors and Benefits

  • Self-Healing: Moving a smart door automatically closes the opening in the old wall location and creates a new one.
  • Automatic Annotations: Balloons and labels update their text automatically when the object's properties change.
  • BOM Integration: Intelligent parts are automatically counted and categorized in the Bill of Materials.
  • 2D/3D Linkage: Modifying a 2D plan view updates the underlying 3D BIM model data simultaneously.

Cross-Platform Compatibility

Modern CAD verticals ensure that these intelligent objects remain "smart" even when shared across different DWG-native platforms (like AutoCAD Mechanical compatibility in GstarCAD Mechanical).
