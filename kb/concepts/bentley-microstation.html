Platform Excellence

MicroStation

The engine of infrastructure: Scaling from the smallest detail to the world's most complex projects with unrivaled precision.

Unlimited Scale

MicroStation is Bentley's flagship CAD platform. Unlike generic CAD software, it is optimized for the unique requirements of the AECO (Architecture, Engineering, Construction, and Operations) industry. It is famous for its ability to handle massive datasets—entire cities, global rail networks, or complex power plants—in a single, high-performance environment.

Core Technical Strengths

  • DGN Format: Native 64-bit precision allows for millimeter-level accuracy across hundreds of kilometers, without coordinate 'drift.'
  • Federated Modeling: Superior reference management (XREFs) that allows thousands of users to work on a single shared model.
  • Reality Integration: Seamlessly overlay 2D designs onto 3D reality meshes and point clouds.
  • Parametric Modeling: Functional components that respond to change while maintaining engineering intent.

Standard for Infrastructure

Used by 90% of the world's top infrastructure design firms, MicroStation is the trusted platform for projects that define the modern world.
