Hybrid Geometry

Convergent Modeling

The mesh-solid hybrid: Working with scanned data and generative output as easily as traditional parametric geometry.

Unified Modeling

Traditionally, CAD users had to perform 'Reverse Engineering' to turn 3D scanned mesh data into solid geometry. Convergent Modeling in Siemens NX eliminates this step. It allows you to treat facet (STL/mesh) data as if it were a solid. You can perform Boolean operations, add rounds, and create holes directly on mesh data without any surface reconstruction.

Key Applications

  • Generative Output: Edit the complex, organic mesh results from Generative Design studies directly within your assembly.
  • Medical Design: Combine 3D-scanned anatomical data (like a bone scan) with parametric medical implants.
  • Legacy Repair: Quickly modify STL files or legacy mesh data without the need for the original source CAD.
  • Facet Operations: Use standard NX tools like 'Subtract,' 'Intersect,' and 'Union' on a mix of mesh and B-Rep solids.

Seamless Integration

Convergent modeling represents a fundamental shift in CAD, allowing designers to move freely between the worlds of scan data, AI-generated shapes, and precision engineering.
