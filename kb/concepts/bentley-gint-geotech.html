Subsurface Engineering

gINT Geotechnical

Understanding the ground: The global standard for managing and reporting subsurface geological information.

Data Beneath the Surface

Every infrastructure project starts with the ground. Bentley gINT is the industry's most flexible and powerful geotechnical data management software. It allows engineers to centralize data from borehole logs, well construction, and lab testing into a single database. It is famous for its professional, highly customizable reports that bring geological data to life.

Geotechnical Power Tools

  • Borehole Logs: Automatically generate professional, standardized logs with soil and rock classifications.
  • Cross-Section Generation: Create 2D and 3D geological fences and profiles from distributed borehole data.
  • Lab Test Management: Integrate results from sieve analysis, Atterberg limits, and triaxial testing directly into the log.
  • GIS & CAD Export: Move subsurface data into Civil 3D, ArcGIS, or Bentley OpenRoads to inform the final foundation design.

Foundation Reliability

gINT provides the structural and civil engineering teams with the high-quality geotechnical data they need to design safe, cost-effective foundations for the world's tallest buildings and longest bridges.
