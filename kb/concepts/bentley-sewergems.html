SewerGEMS

Managing the flow: Advanced modeling for urban sanitary, storm, and combined sewer systems.

Urban Water Resilience

Urban wastewater systems are under constant pressure from population growth and climate change. Bentley SewerGEMS is a comprehensive modeling tool that allows engineers to design new pipe networks and analyze existing ones. It specializes in simulating peak flow events to prevent sanitary sewer overflows (SSOs) and combined sewer overflows (CSOs), ensuring environmental compliance.

Sewer Modeling Tools

  • Hydraulic Solver Selection: Choose from GVF-Convex, GVF-Rational, or fully dynamic solvers (EPA SWMM) based on project requirements.
  • Capacity Analysis: Quickly identify bottlenecks in the sewer network where pipes are undersized for future demand.
  • Hydrogen Sulfide (H2S) Modeling: Predict where harmful and corrosive gases will form in the system to design better ventilation.
  • Low Impact Development (LID): Model green infrastructure like rain gardens and porous pavement to reduce storm runoff.

Sustainable Urban Drainage

SewerGEMS helps cities manage their hidden infrastructure more effectively, protecting public health and the environment from the impacts of urban flooding and wastewater pollution.
