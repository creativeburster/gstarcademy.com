OpenRoads Designer

Designing the path forward: The industry standard for complex highway, site, and subsurface infrastructure.

Process-Driven Design

OpenRoads Designer replaces legacy tools like InRoads and GEOPAK. It provides a single environment for survey, drainage, utilities, and roadway design. It allows engineers to work in 3D while simultaneously generating 2D plans and profiles, ensuring that every cross-section and earthwork calculation is perfectly synchronized.

Key Design Pillars

  • Geometric Modeling: Intelligent horizontal and vertical alignments that update in real-time.
  • Corridor Modeling: Automated generation of complex 3D road surfaces with dynamic earthwork volumes.
  • Subsurface Utilities: Integrate drainage and utility networks (SUE) directly into the civil model.
  • Plan Production: Automated sheet cutting and labeling based on international engineering standards.

Highway Precision

OpenRoads is the core tool for Department of Transportation (DOT) agencies worldwide, ensuring that global transportation networks are safe, efficient, and durable.
