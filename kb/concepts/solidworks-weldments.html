Solid Modeling

SOLIDWORKS Weldments

Structural steel framing, trim-extend operations, and automated cut lists.

Core Concepts

SOLIDWORKS Weldments is a specialized solid modeling module designed for steel framing and structural shapes. By projecting standardized structural profiles along simple 2D or 3D sketch skeletons, it automates weldment design, corner trimming, and structural drawings.

Key Advantages & Applications

  • Profile Library: Extensible library of international standards (ISO, ANSI, DIN) for beams and pipes.
  • Automatic Cut Lists: Instantly generate manufacturing lists containing exact lengths, angles, and quantities.
  • Joint Management: Automated trim, extend, and miter-cut tools for clean frame corners.

Industry Best Practices

Always construct structural frame skeletons using a single master 3D sketch. Organize weldment features chronologically to avoid rebuild loops.
