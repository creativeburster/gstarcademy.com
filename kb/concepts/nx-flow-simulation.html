Fluid Dynamics

NX Flow Simulation

Virtual Aerodynamics: Simulating fluid behavior and heat transfer to optimize performance and thermal stability.

The Science of Flow

Whether it is air moving over a wing or coolant flowing through an engine block, fluid behavior is critical to product performance. NX Flow is a Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) solution integrated within the NX environment. It allows engineers to predict pressure drop, velocity profiles, and heat transfer without the need for expensive wind tunnel testing.

CFD Capabilities

  • Internal Flow: Optimize ducting, piping, and valve designs to minimize pressure loss.
  • External Aerodynamics: Calculate lift and drag on vehicles and structures.
  • Electronic Cooling: Simulate natural and forced convection in electronics enclosures to prevent overheating.
  • Coupled Thermal-Flow: Simultaneously analyze how fluid flow and solid heat transfer interact in complex systems.

Validation-Led Engineering

By integrating CFD into the NX design process, engineers can identify flow bottlenecks and thermal issues early, leading to more efficient, reliable, and high-performance products.
