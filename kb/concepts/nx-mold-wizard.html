NX Mold Wizard

Automating the intricate: Using industry-specific intelligence to streamline the design of complex plastic injection molds.

Process-Driven Tooling

Mold design is a high-stakes, time-consuming process. NX Mold Wizard provides a step-by-step workflow that automates the most difficult tasks. From analyzing draft angles and wall thickness to automatically generating parting lines and surfaces, it guides the designer from the 3D part to a complete, manufacturing-ready mold base.

Wizard Features

  • Core & Cavity Split: Automatically identify 'pull' directions and create complex shut-off surfaces.
  • Standard Parts Library: Drag and drop mold bases, ejector pins, and cooling components from leading manufacturers (DME, HASCO, Futaba).
  • Runner & Gate Design: Automate the creation of flow paths with built-in libraries and validation tools.
  • Cooling Analysis: Design complex cooling channels and verify their effectiveness in protecting the part from distortion.

Industrial Scale Efficiency

Mold Wizard allows companies to standardize their tooling processes and reduce design cycles by 50% or more, while ensuring that every mold produced is optimized for performance and durability.
