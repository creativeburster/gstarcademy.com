IFC Interoperability

Industry Foundation Classes (IFC): The open, vendor-neutral standard for sharing BIM data across different software platforms.

Breaking Vendor Silos

IFC allows a Revit architect, a Tekla structural engineer, and an ArchiCAD interior designer to exchange models without losing the 'intelligence' of the objects. It describes not just geometry, but also the relationships and properties of building elements.

Key Technical Concepts

  • MVD (Model View Definition): A subset of the IFC schema designed for a specific use case (e.g., Coordination View, FM Handover).
  • IFC Classes: Standardized definitions for elements (e.g., IfcWall, IfcWindow, IfcSpace).
  • LOD Mapping: Ensuring the 'Level of Detail' in the native model translates correctly to the IFC export.
  • buildingSMART: The international organization that manages and develops the IFC standard.

Version Check

IFC 2x3 remains the industry workhorse for coordination, while IFC 4 offers better geometry support and specialized infra-classes. Always agree on the version with project partners before export.
