Markup & Collaboration

Digital redlining: Streamlining the feedback loop between designers, engineers, and project stakeholders.

The Feedback Loop

Design review is a critical stage in any project. DraftSight's Markup Tools allow reviewers to add comments, revision clouds, and freehand sketches directly on the drawing without modifying the original design geometry. This creates a clear digital trail of requested changes.

Markup Capabilities

  • Revision Clouds: Standardized clouds to highlight areas of change, available in Rectangular, Polygonal, and Freehand shapes.
  • Digital Annotations: Add text-based 'sticky notes' that can be resolved or hidden as the design progresses.
  • Voice Notes: (On mobile) Record verbal feedback that remains attached to specific coordinates in the DWG.
  • Overlay Management: Markups are typically stored on separate layers or as non-printing entities to maintain drawing clarity.

Closing the Gap

By moving the markup process inside the CAD environment (instead of on physical paper or separate PDFs), DraftSight reduces the risk of misinterpretation and accelerates the revision cycle.
