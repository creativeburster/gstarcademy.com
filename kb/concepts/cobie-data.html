COBie (Construction Operations Building Information Exchange)

COBie data standard for facility management handover: structured spreadsheet format linking BIM data to operations.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by GstarCAD Academy Editorial Team Last reviewed 2026-08-16 Editorial Process

Definition & Context

COBie (Construction Operations Building Information Exchange) is an open, spreadsheet-based schema for delivering non-graphic asset data — spaces, equipment, systems, warranties, spares, and maintenance information — from design and construction into facilities management.

COBie is a subset of IFC and is exported directly from Revit, ArchiCAD, and other BIM tools. Data is captured progressively across project stages so that at handover the owner receives a structured, populated asset register instead of boxes of PDFs.

Why It Matters

COBie turns the BIM model into usable operational data, saving owners the cost of re-surveying assets and enabling CMMS/CAFM systems to be populated automatically at handover.

The catch is data discipline across the whole project: COBie is only as good as the parameters entered during design and construction, so responsibility for populating each field (who, at which stage) should be assigned in the BEP rather than scrambled together at handover. Because it is a defined IFC subset, COBie can be exported and validated automatically — but a model full of unnamed spaces and untyped equipment produces an empty spreadsheet no matter how good the geometry is.

Best Practices

  • Capture COBie data progressively at each stage rather than in a rush at handover.
  • Agree required parameters and classification (e.g. Uniclass/OmniClass) early in the EIR.
  • Validate the COBie export against a checker before submission.
  • Map model parameters to COBie fields consistently across disciplines.

Common Pitfalls

  • Leaving all data entry to the end of the project.
  • Exporting incomplete or mis-mapped parameters that fail validation.
  • Delivering COBie no one can import into the owner's FM system.
  • Ignoring classification codes required by the client.
