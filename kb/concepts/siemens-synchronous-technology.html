Synchronous Technology

The best of both worlds: Combining the freedom of direct modeling with the intelligence of parametric design.

Breaking the History Barrier

In traditional parametric CAD, modifying a feature requires regenerating the entire history tree, which can lead to failures if dependencies are broken. Synchronous Technology (pioneered by Siemens) allows you to select geometry directly and move, rotate, or delete it. The software automatically recognizes geometric relationships (like tangency or symmetry) and maintains them, regardless of how the part was originally built.

Core Capabilities

  • Live Rules: Automatically detect and preserve geometric intent (e.g., keeping two holes coaxial while moving one).
  • History-Free Editing: Perfect for editing 'dumb' solids imported from other CAD systems via STEP or Parasolid.
  • Dimension Control: Drive direct geometry changes using precise 3D driving dimensions.
  • Mixed Mode: Use synchronous features alongside traditional history-based features in the same part.

Industrial Impact

Synchronous Technology allows engineering teams to iterate up to 100x faster than traditional methods, making it the preferred tool for rapid prototyping and multi-CAD design environments.
