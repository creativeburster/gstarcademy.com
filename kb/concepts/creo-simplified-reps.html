Simplified Representations

Managing the massive: Selective loading and display of assembly components for high-speed design and review.

The Anti-Lag Strategy

Loading a complete assembly with 10,000+ parts can overwhelm even the strongest workstation. Simplified Representations (Simp Reps) allow you to load only a subset of an assembly into RAM. You can exclude components by name, by spatial volume, or by 'rules' (e.g., 'exclude all fasteners').

Rep Types

  • Master Rep: Loads everything with full detail. Best for final checks and mass properties.
  • Graphic Rep: Loads only the visual 'tessellated' data. Very fast for zooming and panning, but cannot be used for modeling.
  • Geometry Rep: Loads only the surface data. Good for assembly mating without the weight of the full feature history.
  • Custom Rep: User-defined list of included/excluded parts for specific tasks (e.g., 'Engine Only' or 'Frame Only').

Working at Scale

Simplified Reps are what allow Creo to handle projects of extreme scale, such as entire ships or aircraft, while keeping the user interface responsive and stable.
