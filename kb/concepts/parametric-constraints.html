Parametric Constraints

Defining design intent through mathematical rules and geometric relationships.

Core Definition

Parametric constraints are rules that govern the shape and size of drawing objects. When you modify one dimension, other constrained parts of the drawing update automatically to maintain the defined relationships (e.g., keeping two lines always parallel).

Geometric Constraints

Control relationships like: Perpendicular, Parallel, Concentric, Tangent, and Coincident.

Dimensional Constraints

Control sizes like: Distance, Radius, Angle, and Diameter via mathematical variables.

Software Support

GstarCAD 2026

Introduces a powerful Parameters Manager. Allows users to create complex formulas (e.g., width = height * 0.5) to drive designs.

AutoCAD

Full support via the Parametric tab. Deeply integrated with Dynamic Blocks for advanced reusable content.
