Subassemblies (Civil 3D)

Parametric building blocks of a cross-section — Lane, Kerb, Sidewalk, Shoulder, Daylight, etc., scripted in Subassembly Composer.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Reviewed by AEC Review Desk Last reviewed 2026-05-24 Editorial process

Definition

Subassemblies are AutoLISP/.NET coded geometry generators with parameters (lane width, slope, sub-base depth, kerb type). They attach at a connection point and emit links and points labelled by code. Civil 3D ships with the Subassembly Catalog (corridor stock library); Subassembly Composer authors custom subassemblies visually.

Why it matters

Subassemblies are the rules that produce road geometry. Custom subassemblies encode local design standards (DOT-specific kerb types, drainage profiles).

Common pitfalls

  • Authoring subassemblies that don't expose useful parameters — corridor needs surgery for each variation.
  • Forgetting to assign codes consistently — code-set styles fail to colour the right surfaces.
  • Recursive parametric loops in custom subassemblies — corridor generation hangs.
