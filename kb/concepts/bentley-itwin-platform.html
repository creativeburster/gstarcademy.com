Digital Twin

iTwin Platform

The future of infrastructure: Creating living, 4D digital replicas that bridge the gap between physical assets and digital intelligence.

Beyond Static Models

A traditional CAD model is a snapshot in time. The Bentley iTwin Platform creates a 'Living' digital twin. It synchronizes engineering data from CAD, reality meshes, and IoT sensors into a single cloud-based repository. It tracks changes over time (the 4th Dimension), allowing owners to see how an asset was designed, how it was built, and how it is performing right now.

Key iTwin Capabilities

  • Data Synchronization: Harmonize data from diverse sources (BIM, GIS, IoT, ERP) into a single unified view.
  • Timeline Analytics: Rewind the model to see past configurations or fast-forward to predict future performance.
  • IoT Integration: Connect real-time sensors (flow, temperature, vibration) to the digital model for instant operational insight.
  • Immersive Web View: Explore massive 3D/4D twins in a browser without needing any specialized software.

Digitalizing Operations

The iTwin platform enables infrastructure owners to optimize maintenance, reduce operational risks, and extend the lifespan of their most critical assets.
