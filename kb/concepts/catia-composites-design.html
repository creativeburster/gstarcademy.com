CATIA Composites Design

Composite ply layup, draping simulation, and advanced materials engineering.

Core Concepts

CATIA Composites Design is a dedicated workbench tailored for aerospace and marine composite structural engineering. It manages the entire process from structural surface zones, ply draping simulation, to exporting flat patterns for carbon-fiber laser projection systems.

Key Advantages & Applications

  • Draping Simulation: Predict fiber deformation and warp shear over highly curved surfaces before layups.
  • Ply Table Generation: Automatically generate exact ply books, sequencing charts, and engineering layups.
  • Core Samples: Extract precise material core thickness profiles at any physical point on the composite shell.

Industry Best Practices

Ensure the underlying mold surface is completely healed and continuous. Define clear fiber orientation reference axes to maintain ply structural alignment.
