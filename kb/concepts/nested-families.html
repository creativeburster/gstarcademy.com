Nested Families

Building modular BIM components by embedding families within a 'Host' family for improved management and parametric control.

Component Hierarchy

A door family doesn't need to have the handle geometry modeled from scratch inside it. Instead, you can 'Nest' a separate door handle family. This allows you to swap handle types globally by updating only the nested handle family, rather than editing every door.

Shared vs. Non-Shared

  • Non-Shared (Default): The nested component is treated as part of the host. You cannot tag or schedule the handle separately from the door.
  • Shared: If 'Shared' is checked in the nested family's properties, it can be scheduled, tagged, and even swapped with other shared families of the same category within the project environment.
  • Parameter Mapping: You can link parameters of the nested family to parameters of the host family to maintain parametric control across levels.

Best Practice

Avoid nesting more than 2-3 levels deep. Deep nesting hierarchies can lead to 'circular reference' errors and significantly degrade model performance when regenerating views.
