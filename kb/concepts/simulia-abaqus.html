SIMULIA Abaqus FEA

Industry-leading nonlinear finite element analysis and structural solver.

Core Concepts

SIMULIA Abaqus FEA is an advanced suite for finite element analysis. It is globally recognized for its exceptional capability to handle highly nonlinear structural problems, including complex contact, plastic deformation, material failure, and dynamic impact events.

Key Advantages & Applications

  • Nonlinear Solver: Master class handling of geometric, material, and boundary nonlinearities.
  • Abaqus/Explicit: Specialized solver for transient dynamic events like crashworthiness and drop tests.
  • Co-Simulation: Couple electromagnetic, fluid, and structural analyses for multiphysics fidelity.

Industry Best Practices

Start with a simplified 2D or symmetric model to validate boundaries. Mesh critical zones finely while utilizing structural transitions to keep solve times reasonable.
