Mechanical Design

Frame Generator

Rapidly designing structural skeletons by mapping standardized profiles to wireframe 3D sketches.

Skeleton-Driven Modeling

Instead of modeling each beam individually, you create a 'Skeleton' (a 3D sketch or assembly edges). Frame Generator then 'wraps' these edges with real geometry from the Content Center (I-beams, C-channels, Square tubes).

Core Tools

  • Insert Frame: Place profiles along selected edges with specific orientation and offset.
  • Miter & Trim: Automatically clean up intersections between beams for manufacturing.
  • Notch: Create complex cutouts where one profile meets another (e.g., a round tube meeting a flat I-beam).
  • End Caps: Automatically close the open ends of hollow profiles.

BOM & Cut List

Frame Generator automatically calculates the Cut Length for every member, which is then populated into a specialized 'Cut List' in your assembly drawing for the fabrication shop.
