Quality Control Cad

Professional guide to quality control cad in CAD/BIM engineering workflows.

By Gstarcademy Editorial TeamUpdated 2026-06-30

Definition & Context

CAD-based quality control drives inspection from the model: CMM and 3-D-scan measurement compared against nominal CAD/GD&T, first-article inspection (FAI), and SPC. Model-based inspection uses PMI to generate measurement programs and colour deviation maps.

Software (PolyWorks, GOM Inspect, Zeiss Calypso, Verisurf) aligns measured data to CAD, evaluates GD&T, and produces inspection reports (AS9102 FAI, PPAP), closing the loop from design to verification.

Why It Matters

Model-based QC verifies that what was made matches what was designed, using GD&T-aware inspection. It catches defects, feeds SPC, and provides the documented conformance customers require.

Tools & Software

  • PolyWorks / GOM Inspect
  • Zeiss Calypso
  • Verisurf
  • CMM/3-D scanning

Best Practices

  • Inspect against nominal CAD and its GD&T, not ad-hoc dimensions.
  • Use PMI/MBD to generate measurement programs where possible.
  • Align measured data properly (datum reference frame) before evaluating.
  • Produce standard reports (FAI/PPAP) and feed SPC.

Common Pitfalls

  • Measuring arbitrary dimensions instead of the GD&T scheme.
  • Poor alignment/datum handling skewing results.
  • No SPC/trending, so process drift goes unnoticed.
  • Inspection disconnected from the design model/PMI.

Standards & Interoperability

  • AS9102 FAI
  • PPAP
  • GD&T (ASME Y14.5)
