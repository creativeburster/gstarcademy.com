Tube & Pipe Design

Designing intelligent, 3D routed systems for industrial machinery, hydraulic systems, and plant facilities.

Automated Routing

The Tube & Pipe environment in Inventor automates the placement of fittings and the generation of pipe geometry. By defining a path (Route), the engine automatically inserts elbows, tees, and flanges based on a predefined 'Style' (e.g., ASME B16.9 Welded Steel).

Key Routing Types

  • Rigid Piping: Steel or copper pipes with fixed-angle elbows (45/90 degrees).
  • Flexible Hose: Hydraulic or pneumatic hoses with 3D splines that account for minimum bend radii.
  • Bent Tubing: Continuous tubing that is bent to shape rather than using separate fittings.
  • Derived Routes: Paths generated automatically from existing assembly geometry or 3D sketches.

Self-Healing Assemblies

If you move a pump or a tank in your assembly, the linked Tube & Pipe route automatically updates its geometry and fitting positions to maintain the connection, ensuring your BOM stays accurate.
