Runout Tolerances: Circular vs Total Runout

GD&T composite controls that limit variations in circularity, straightness, coaxiality, and perpendicularity of rotating cylindrical surfaces.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-09-30 Editorial process

1. Definition & Engineering Significance

Critical high-speed rotating assemblies (turbines, driveshafts, motor spindles) fail catastrophically if center-of-mass eccentricity causes vibration. Runout callouts inspect surface deviation relative to a datum axis as the part is rotated 360 degrees against a dial indicator.

2. Key Technical Characteristics & Rules

  • Circular Runout (Symbol ↗): Inspects surface deviation along a single circular cross-section cross-slice independently.
  • Total Runout (Symbol ↗↗): Inspects the entire cylindrical or planar surface simultaneously as the indicator traverses axially, controlling taper and wobble.
  • Coaxiality Replacement: Preferred over legacy concentricity controls because runout can be verified quickly on a lathe with a mechanical dial gauge.

3. Dial Indicator Verification & Mechanical Function

Runout tolerances control the composite variation of rotating rotational surfaces relative to a datum axis:

  • Circular Runout (Single Arrow): Evaluates individual 2D cross-sectional circular elements as the part is rotated 360 degrees about its datum axis, controlling circularity and coaxiality.
  • Total Runout (Double Arrow): Measures the full 3D surface simultaneously as the indicator traverses axially while the part rotates, controlling circularity, straightness, coaxiality, angularity, and taper.
  • Dial Indicator Setup: The physical inspection setup mounts a dial indicator perpendicular to the rotating shaft supported on datum centers.

4. Functional Allocation & Manufacturing Costs

Specifying Total Runout on non-critical spacer sleeves unnecessarily inflates grinding costs. Reserve Total Runout for high-speed transmission shafts, turbine rotors, and bearing journals where dynamic centrifugal unbalance causes severe vibration and premature bearing destruction.
