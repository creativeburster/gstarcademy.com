Ordinate Dimensioning in Precision Tooling

A drafting method where all feature coordinates are measured along X and Y axes from a single common datum origin (0,0), eliminating baseline dimension line clutter.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-09-30 Editorial process

1. Definition & Engineering Significance

For CNC punch presses, stamping dies, and dense electronics enclosure faceplates with hundreds of mounting holes, standard linear chain dimensioning creates an unreadable spiderweb of extension lines. Ordinate dimensioning lists running coordinate values directly adjacent to each hole center.

2. Key Technical Characteristics & Rules

  • Zero Tolerance Stacking: Every hole position references the master datum (0,0) independently; drilling errors do not compound across the part.
  • Direct CNC Programming Correspondence: Ordinate dimensions correspond directly to G-code X/Y coordinates, speeding up manual inspection.
  • Automated Hole Tables: High-end CAD packages convert ordinate callouts into structured tables indexing hole letter, diameter, and X/Y coordinates.
