Surface Roughness: Ra vs Rz Parameters & Drawing Symbols

Comprehensive engineering reference for surface texture parameters Ra and Rz per ISO 21920 / ISO 1302 and ASME B46.1. Interpret modern CAD drawing symbols.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-10-12 Editorial process

1. Definition & Engineering Significance

Surface texture and roughness specifications govern the tribological friction, wear resistance, fatigue endurance, and sealing hermeticity of machined mechanical components. In modern engineering drawings compliant with ISO 21920 (superseding ISO 1302) and ASME B46.1, surface finish is denoted using standardized checkmark-style symbols specifying quantitative micro-inch ($\mu\text{in}$) or micrometer ($\mu\text{m}$) limits. Among dozens of metrology metrics, $R_a$ and $R_z$ are the two most universally utilized surface parameters.

2. Technical Architecture & Key Parameters

  • Ra (Arithmetic Mean Deviation): The arithmetic average value of profile absolute deviations from the mean evaluation line:
    Ra = (1 / l) ∫0..l |z(x)| dx
    Dampens individual extreme outliers, providing a stable overview of general process consistency.
  • Rz (Maximum Height of Profile): The average peak-to-valley height across sampling lengths (or absolute maximum height $R_t$ in newer ISO standards). Highly sensitive to isolated deep scratches, voids, and micro-cracks.
  • Symbol Notation Syntax: The basic checkmark symbol is modified with a horizontal bar (material removal mandatory, e.g., milling/grinding) or an enclosed circle (material removal prohibited, e.g., forging/casting).
  • Lay Direction Designators: Parallel (=), Perpendicular ($\perp$), Crossed (X), Multi-directional (M), Circular (C), and Radial (R) indicators specify cutter feed marks.

3. Industry Best Practices & Failure Modes

A common engineering mistake is assuming a low $R_a$ value guarantees fluid sealing performance. A ground hydraulic shaft can exhibit an outstanding $R_a$ of 0.2 $\mu\text{m}$, yet contain a single longitudinal chatter mark with an $R_z$ of 4.5 $\mu\text{m}$ that creates an escape channel for pressurized oil, destroying the dynamic rubber lip seal.

4. Production Governance & Standard Protocols

Modern engineering specifications combine both metrics—for instance, specifying $R_a \le 0.4\, \mu\text{m}$ alongside $R_z \le 1.6\, \mu\text{m}$ for high-pressure bearing journals and $R_a \le 0.8\, \mu\text{m}$ with circular lay (C) for O-ring face seals, ensuring leak-free mechanical operation.
