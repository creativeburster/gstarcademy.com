Cloud & Mobile

Mobile BIM & 3D Viewing

Bringing massive 3D data to the field through lightweight, cloud-assisted visualization technology.

High-Fidelity in Your Pocket

Traditional BIM software (like Revit or Navisworks) requires powerful workstations. DWG FastView uses advanced streaming and occlusion culling to render complex 3D models on standard smartphones. This allows field engineers to walk through as-built models and verify site conditions in real-time.

Core Capabilities

  • 40+ Format Support: View RVT (Revit), STEP, IGES, SolidWorks, and IFC directly.
  • Cross-Sectioning: Cut through floors or walls to inspect internal MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) systems.
  • 3D Measurement: Measure distances, angles, and volumes directly from the 3D geometry.
  • Cloud Sync: Annotations made in the field sync back to desktop CAD for design revision.

Collaboration Workflow

Field Redlines: Field teams mark up 3D clashes -> Sync to Cloud -> Desktop team receives notification in GstarCAD -> Design fixed before next site visit.
