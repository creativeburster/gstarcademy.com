Inter-Part Design

WAVE Geometry Linker

The associative bridge: Connecting geometry across assembly components for seamless top-down design.

Connecting the Parts

In complex product assemblies, parts don't exist in isolation. A bracket must match the holes of a frame; a cover must match the seal of a housing. WAVE Geometry Linker in NX allows you to copy curves, faces, or bodies from one part into another as 'linked geometry.' If the original part changes, the linked geometry in the child part updates automatically.

Advanced Linking Tools

  • Associative Links: Real-time synchronization between source and target components.
  • WAVE Control Structure: Using 'Linker parts' (skeletons) to drive an entire assembly architecture.
  • Position Independence: Choose whether the linked geometry stays at its original global coordinates or moves with the component.
  • Link Browser: A dedicated UI to manage, break, or freeze links across a massive assembly tree.

Architectural Integrity

WAVE is the core engine for Top-Down Design in NX, enabling large-scale collaborative engineering while maintaining a rigid, logical relationship between all components.
