Product Structure (CATIA Assembly)

The hierarchical tree of components, sub-assemblies, and instances in a CATIA assembly (.CATProduct).

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Reviewed by Mechanical Review Desk Last reviewed 2026-05-24 Editorial process

Definition

A Product is a CATIA assembly container. It references CATParts and other CATProducts. Each reference is an instance with a position, an instance name, and possibly publications exposed to the assembly. Multi-instance support means one CATPart referenced multiple times in the Product.

Why it matters

Product structure determines BOM, assembly performance, and access control. A flat Product with 50k CATPart references is unmanageable; a deeply nested structure that matches the real manufacturing tree is.

Common pitfalls

  • All parts at one level — performance and clarity suffer.
  • Instances vs. references confused — editing an instance affects all instances of that CATPart.
  • Mismatched display modes (visualization vs. design) — visual artefacts in large assemblies.
