Sample Lines & Cross Sections

Civil 3D sample lines for generating cross-section views showing existing ground, design surfaces, and corridor assembly.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-07-28 Editorial process

Definition & Context

In Civil 3D, sample lines are placed along an alignment (at stations or intervals) to sample surfaces, corridor sections, and pipe crossings; from them Civil 3D generates cross-section views and section sheets showing existing ground, design surfaces, and the corridor assembly at each station.

Sample line groups control what is sampled; section views and sheets are produced for earthwork volumes (material lists) and documentation.

Why It Matters

Sections are how earthwork quantities and design intent are verified and communicated on linear projects; sampling from the model keeps them synced with the corridor and surfaces.

Because volumes come from sampled sections, correct sample-line spacing and surface selection is what makes earthwork numbers trustworthy.

Best Practices

  • Set sample-line spacing appropriate to terrain variability and required quantity accuracy
  • Sample all relevant surfaces and corridor data needed for volumes and review
  • Regenerate sections after corridor or surface edits
  • Use consistent section-view styles for readable sheets

Common Pitfalls

  • Sparse sample lines missing terrain changes and skewing volumes
  • Forgetting to sample a needed surface or corridor
  • Stale sections left after design edits
