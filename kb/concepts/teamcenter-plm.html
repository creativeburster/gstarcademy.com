Teamcenter PLM

The digital thread: Connecting people and processes across the entire product lifecycle with a single source of truth.

Unified Lifecycle Management

Product development is more than just CAD. It involves requirements, schedules, manufacturing instructions, and service data. Siemens Teamcenter is the Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) system that binds these disparate data types together. It ensures that everyone in the enterprise—from design to procurement—is working on the latest, approved information.

Key PLM Pillars

  • Data Management: Securely vaulting CAD models and managing revisions (e.g., A, B, C) and states (e.g., Working, Released).
  • BOM Management: Synchronizing the Engineering Bill of Materials (EBOM) with Manufacturing (MBOM) and Service (SBOM).
  • Change Management: Formalizing the Engineering Change Order (ECO) process with automated workflows and approvals.
  • Supplier Collaboration: Safely sharing specific data packages with external partners while protecting IP.

Strategic Advantage

Teamcenter reduces cycle times and costs by eliminating data silos, ensuring that product complexity is managed systematically across the entire global organization.
