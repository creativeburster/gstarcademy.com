SYNCHRO 4D

Visualizing time: Linking the 3D model to the project schedule to optimize site logistics and safety.

The 4th Dimension

In traditional construction, the 3D model and the project schedule (Gantt chart) are separate. Bentley SYNCHRO brings them together into a 4D environment. By linking every model component to a task in the schedule, project teams can 'play' the entire construction sequence, identifying spatial clashes and logistics bottlenecks before a single shovel hits the dirt.

Core 4D Workflows

  • Model-Schedule Linking: Associating 3D geometry with P6 or Microsoft Project tasks.
  • Site Logistics Simulation: Model cranes, trucks, and storage areas to ensure there is enough space for every phase of construction.
  • Safety Analysis: Identify potential safety hazards (like working under a crane load) by visualizing the sequence.
  • Progress Tracking: Use mobile apps to update task status directly from the site, reflecting real-time progress in the 4D model.

Predictive Planning

SYNCHRO transforms construction from a reactive process to a predictive one, ensuring that complex projects are delivered safely, on time, and within budget.
