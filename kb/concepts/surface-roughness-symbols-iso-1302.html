Surface Roughness & Texture Symbols (ISO 1302)

Standardized graphical symbols specifying required surface finish, micro-irregularity limits (Ra, Rz), manufacturing process, and lay directions.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by GstarCAD Academy Editorial Team Last reviewed 2026-09-30 Editorial Process

1. Definition & Engineering Significance

ISO 1302 governs surface texture indication on technical drawings. High surface finish reduces friction and fatigue wear in rotating shafts, while rougher textures improve paint adhesion or gasket sealing.

2. Key Technical Characteristics & Rules

  • Ra vs Rz: Ra represents the arithmetical mean deviation of the profile; Rz measures the average height of five highest peaks and five deepest valleys.
  • Production Method Callout: Specifies whether material removal is required (machining checkmark), prohibited (casting/forging circle), or non-specific.
  • Lay Direction Symbols: Indicates the direction of tool marks (parallel =, perpendicular ⊥, crossed X, circular C, multidirectional M).

3. Surface Texture Parameters & Drawing Annotation

ISO 1302 and ASME B46.1 specify standardized graphical symbols and parameters to control surface finishes on machined components:

  • Ra (Roughness Average): The arithmetic mean deviation of the assessed surface profile heights from the center line.
  • Rz (Maximum Peak-to-Valley Height): Measures the vertical distance between the highest peak and deepest valley within a sampling length, catching sharp scratches that cause seal leaks.
  • Machining Method Specifiers: Checkmark symbols specify whether material removal is mandatory (machined), prohibited (cast/forged), or optional.

4. Tribological Failures & Over-Specification Costs

Demanding unnecessarily fine finishes (e.g., Ra 0.2μm where Ra 1.6μm is functional) forces shops into secondary cylindrical grinding or lapping operations, multiplying component manufacturing costs 4x to 8x. However, on hydraulic piston rods, relying solely on Ra without specifying Rz can permit sharp valley grooves that shred rubber lip seals.
