Entity Properties

The DNA of your drawing: Managing the visual and technical data associated with every line, circle, and block.

Data-Driven Drafting

Every object in DraftSight has a set of Properties. These determine not just how the object looks (color, linetype) but also its geometric identity (start point, radius, area) and its organizational context (layer, transparency).

Key Property Groups

  • General: Layer, Color, Linetype, Linetype Scale, and Lineweight.
  • Geometry: Coordinates, lengths, and angles specific to the entity type.
  • Advanced: Transparency, Hyperlinks, and custom Metadata (Block Attributes).
  • ByLayer vs. Hardcoded: The best practice of inheriting properties from the layer definition rather than assigning them individually.

The Properties Palette

The Properties Palette (Ctrl+1) is the command center for any power user. It allows you to select multiple objects and change their shared properties in a single operation, such as moving all selected lines to a 'Demolition' layer.
