Solid Edge Synchronous

Agile engineering: Bringing the power of history-free modeling to mainstream mechanical design.

The Core Differentiator

While most mid-range CAD tools (like SolidWorks or Inventor) are strictly history-based, Solid Edge features Synchronous Technology. This allows users to jump between traditional history-based modeling and history-free 'Synchronous' modeling in the same part, providing unmatched flexibility for late-stage design changes and multi-CAD data editing.

Solid Edge Advantages

  • Steering Wheel: Use the intuitive 3D manipulator to move, rotate, and pull geometry directly on the model.
  • Solution Manager: Automatically maintain geometric intent (symmetry, concentricity) during direct edits.
  • Data Reuse: Easily grab and move features from one part to another without re-modeling.
  • Assembly Speed: Perform massive assembly updates faster by avoiding the 'regeneration lag' of complex history trees.

Mainstream Versatility

Solid Edge provides high-end modeling power at a mid-range price point, making it a critical tool for small-to-medium manufacturers who need to react quickly to customer requirements.
