PowerTrim Efficiency

The industry's most intuitive trimming tool: Gesture-based geometry modification for rapid drafting.

Drafting at the Speed of Thought

Traditional trimming requires multiple clicks: select cutting edges, then select the object to trim. PowerTrim in DraftSight changes the paradigm. By simply holding down the mouse button and 'drawing' a line across the entities you want to remove, the software calculates intersections and trims them instantly.

Core Capabilities

  • Dynamic Trimming: Remove any segment of a line, arc, or polyline simply by touching it with the cursor.
  • Intelligent Extending: Hold the 'Shift' key while using PowerTrim to extend entities to the nearest boundary instead of trimming them.
  • Selection History: PowerTrim remembers the cutting context, allowing you to clean up complex intersections in a single continuous stroke.
  • Boundary Awareness: It automatically recognizes blocks, Xrefs, and hatches as valid cutting boundaries.

Why it Matters

Drafters using PowerTrim report a 30-50% reduction in 'clicks per hour' during the cleanup phase of a drawing. It is often cited by users as the single biggest reason for preferring DraftSight over traditional 2D CAD clones.
