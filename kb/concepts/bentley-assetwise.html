Asset Operations

AssetWise

Optimizing the lifecycle: Turning engineering information into operational intelligence for the world's most critical infrastructure.

Data-Driven Operations

While CAD and BIM focus on design and construction, Bentley AssetWise is built for the longest phase of an asset's life: Operations. It manages the 'health' of infrastructure by tracking inspections, maintenance records, and operational risks. It ensures that owners have the right information at the right time to make decisions that extend the life of bridges, plants, and networks.

Core Operational Tools

  • Asset Performance Management (APM): Track the condition of assets and predict when they are likely to fail.
  • Inspection Management: Automate the scheduling and reporting of structural and mechanical inspections.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Ensure that all operational data meets strict environmental and safety regulations.
  • Digital Operations Hub: Connect maintenance teams to the latest 3D models and manuals directly in the field.

Maximizing Value

AssetWise enables infrastructure owners to move from reactive maintenance to a proactive strategy, significantly reducing operational costs and maximizing the value of every public and private asset.
