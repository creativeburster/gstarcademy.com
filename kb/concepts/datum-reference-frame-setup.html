Datum Reference Frame (3-2-1 Principle)

A system of three mutually perpendicular planes that fully constrain the six degrees of freedom of a rigid workpiece during manufacturing and inspection.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-09-30 Editorial process

1. Definition & Engineering Significance

The Datum Reference Frame (DRF) simulates the real-world operational assembly interface. The 3-2-1 rule dictates 3 contact points on the primary datum (restricting 3 DOF), 2 points on the secondary datum (restricting 2 DOF), and 1 point on the tertiary datum (restricting the final rotational DOF).

2. Key Technical Characteristics & Rules

  • Primary Datum: Controls orientation; establishes the master leveling plane.
  • Secondary Datum: Perpendicular to primary; establishes heading direction.
  • Tertiary Datum: Perpendicular to both; locks the lateral translation origin.
