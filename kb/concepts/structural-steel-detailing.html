Structural Steel Detailing

Professional guide to structural steel detailing — tools, workflows, software recommendations, and best practices for engineers and designers.

By Gstarcademy Editorial TeamUpdated 2026-07-28

Definition & Context

Structural-steel detailing produces the fabrication and erection information for a steel-framed structure: member sizes, connections, holes, welds, bolts, camber, and marks. Detailers model every member and connection in 3-D, generating shop drawings, CNC/DSTV data, and bills of material.

Tools such as Tekla Structures, Advance Steel, and SDS/2 drive fabrication machines directly and coordinate with the design (analysis) model and other disciplines.

Why It Matters

Detailing turns the engineer's design into buildable, fabricable information. Accurate 3-D models feed CNC fabrication and clash-free erection, cutting rework and site fit-up problems.

Tools & Software

  • Tekla Structures
  • Autodesk Advance Steel
  • SDS/2
  • CNC/DSTV output

Best Practices

  • Model connections fully in 3-D and check for clashes/access.
  • Follow AISC/Eurocode connection design and detailing rules.
  • Generate CNC/DSTV data and consistent piece marks.
  • Coordinate with the analysis model and other disciplines.

Common Pitfalls

  • Connections that clash or lack bolt/weld access.
  • Piece-mark or BOM errors causing fabrication mistakes.
  • Detailing not matching the engineer's design forces.
  • Manual drawings out of sync with the 3-D model.

Standards & Interoperability

  • AISC 360 / Eurocode 3
  • AISC/AWS detailing
