NX Realize Shape

Sculpting with precision: Combining the ease of subdivision modeling with the high-quality surface standards of automotive CAD.

Creative Freedom

Traditional surfacing (Class-A) is difficult and time-consuming. NX Realize Shape introduces Subdivision Modeling (SubD) into the NX environment. It allows designers to 'sculpt' complex organic shapes—like consumer electronics or ergonomic handles—by simply pushing and pulling on a control cage, while NX automatically maintains professional G2 (Curvature) continuity.

Key Design Workflows

  • Cage Manipulation: Drag vertices, edges, and faces of the subdivision cage to deform the surface in real-time.
  • Symmetry Control: Design one half of a product and have the other half mirror and synchronize perfectly.
  • B-Rep Integration: Convert subdivision surfaces into high-quality B-Rep geometry for downstream engineering and manufacturing.
  • Parametric Context: Sculpt your organic shape while seeing the internal mechanical components (PCB, batteries) for perfect fit.

Aesthetic Engineering

Realize Shape bridges the gap between 'artistic' concept modeling and 'rigorous' engineering, allowing companies to launch beautiful products faster than ever before.
