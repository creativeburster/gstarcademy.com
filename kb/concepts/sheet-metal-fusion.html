Sheet Metal (Fusion 360)

Fusion 360's sheet metal workspace — flange-based modelling with rules-driven bend allowance and flat pattern.

By LearnCAD Editorial Team Reviewed by Mechanical Review Desk Last reviewed 2026-05-24 Editorial process

Definition

Sheet metal mode applies a chosen rule (sheet metal rule: material, thickness, bend radius, K-factor, relief style). Tools include Flange, Edge Flange, Bend, Convert to Sheet Metal (from solid), Flat Pattern. Drawings include the folded view + flat pattern + bend table.

Why it matters

For fabricated brackets, enclosures, and chassis, Fusion 360 sheet metal produces production-ready flat patterns without leaving the parametric environment.

Common pitfalls

  • Wrong rule choice — material thickness or K-factor mismatched to actual machine.
  • Mixing solid and sheet-metal features on one body — flat pattern computation fails.
  • Designing a part with non-developable surfaces — Flat Pattern errors silently or produces wrong result.
