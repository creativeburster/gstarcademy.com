Mechanical Toolbox

Standardized component libraries and engineering symbols for professional mechanical documentation.

The Engineering Toolkit

The Mechanical Toolbox is a specialized add-on for DraftSight Professional and Premium. It automates the creation of standard fasteners and mechanical symbols, ensuring that every hole, bolt, and nut follows strict international drafting standards.

Key Toolsets

  • Fastener Library: Thousands of bolts, screws, nuts, and washers from ISO, ANSI, DIN, and BSI standards.
  • Holes and Slots: Automated hole callouts and slot generation with tolerance data.
  • BOM & Bubbles: Link Toolbox components to a dynamic Bill of Materials and auto-generate item bubbles.
  • Revision Clouds: Smart revision tracking tools designed specifically for mechanical drawing sets.

International Standards

The Toolbox allows you to switch between different standards instantly, making it the perfect tool for global companies that need to produce drawings for multiple markets.
