Piping Design

Flowing with precision: Automating the routing and specification of rigid and flexible fluid systems.

Automated Fluid Networks

Designing piping systems manually is error-prone and time-consuming. Creo Piping automates the process using specification-driven routing. You define the pipe size, material, and schedule, and Creo automatically places the correct elbows, tees, and valves as you route the path in 3D.

Standard-Driven Tools

  • Spec-Driven Routing: Automatically select fittings (like 90° elbows) from a library based on the pipe's technical specification.
  • Insulation & Cladding: Apply insulation layers to pipes with accurate external diameter and interference checks.
  • Flexible Hoses: Route flexible hoses that respect minimum bend radius and change shape as the assembly moves.
  • Isometrics: Automatically generate ISO drawings for manufacturing, including a complete Bill of Materials (BOM) and cut lists.

Industrial Scale

Creo Piping is essential for designing complex hydraulic systems, chemical processing plants, and engine fluid networks where precision and compliance with industrial standards are critical.
