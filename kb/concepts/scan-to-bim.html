Scan to BIM

The process of capturing existing physical structures using laser scanning and modeling them as intelligent BIM elements.

The Digital Twin Pipeline

Scan to BIM bridges the gap between 'as-built' reality and 'as-modeled' data. It involves processing raw LiDAR data into registered point clouds (via ReCap) and then using those points as a 3D template to author parametric walls, floors, and MEP systems in Revit.

Key Workflow Steps

  • Capture: Terrestrial Laser Scanning (TLS) or SLAM-based handheld scanning.
  • Registration: Aligning multiple scans into a single coordinate system (Autodesk ReCap).
  • Decimation: Reducing point count to maintain Revit performance without losing structural detail.
  • Extraction: Manually or semi-automatically tracing geometry to create Revit families.

Level of Accuracy (LOA)

Specify the LOA early in the project. High LOA (e.g., 5mm tolerance) significantly increases modeling time but is critical for industrial piping or historic preservation.
