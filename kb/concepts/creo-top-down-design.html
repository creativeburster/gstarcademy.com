Top-Down Design (TDD)

The Architect's approach: Managing product complexity from the assembly level down to individual parts.

Architecture Over Geometry

In Top-Down Design (TDD), the design starts at the highest assembly level. You define the assembly structure, layout, and critical interface geometry first, and then propagate that information down to individual parts. This is the opposite of 'Bottom-Up' design where parts are made first and then assembled.

The TDD Toolkit

  • Skeletons: Central components containing 3D blueprint geometry.
  • Notebooks: Global parameter files that drive dimensions across multiple parts.
  • Data Sharing: Copy Geometry and Publish Geometry features that move data without creating loops.
  • Simplified Reps: Managing the display of large TDD assemblies for performance.

Scalable Engineering

TDD is essential for complex products like aerospace vehicles or automotive platforms, where consistency across thousands of components is paramount and design changes are frequent.
