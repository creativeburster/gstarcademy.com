License Types

Choosing the right CAD tool: Understanding the functional and commercial differences between DraftSight's tiered offerings.

Tiered Professionalism

DraftSight is available in several versions tailored to different user needs. From basic 2D drafting for individuals to enterprise-grade network licensing with full 3D capabilities, understanding these tiers is essential for cost-effective CAD procurement.

Functional Tiers

  • Professional: Focused on 2D drafting for power users, including LISP support and Mechanical Toolbox.
  • Premium: Everything in Professional plus 3D Modeling and 2D Geometric Constraints.
  • Enterprise / Plus: Network licensing (concurrent usage) designed for organizations with large pools of users.
  • 3DEXPERIENCE Connected: A special version that links directly to Dassault's cloud platform for PLM and collaboration.

Commercial Strategy

Unlike many modern CAD applications that only offer subscriptions, DraftSight continues to offer flexible options (though mostly subscription-based now) that often represent a lower Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) for large departments.
