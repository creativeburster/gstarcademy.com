3D Modeling

Expanding into the third dimension: Creating complex solids and surfaces within the familiar DraftSight interface.

Solid & Surface Creation

While DraftSight is famous for 2D, the Premium and Enterprise versions include robust 3D modeling tools. You can extrude 2D profiles into solids, create complex surfaces, and perform Boolean operations (Union, Subtract, Intersect) to design mechanical parts or architectural masses.

Key 3D Workflows

  • Primitive Solids: Create boxes, cylinders, spheres, and cones as starting points for complex designs.
  • Sweep & Revolve: Generate complex geometry by sweeping a 2D profile along a path or revolving it around an axis.
  • 3D Views: Toggle between Isometric, Perspective, and standard Orthographic views to inspect your model.
  • Visual Styles: Switch between Wireframe, Hidden, Shaded, and Realistic modes for better visualization.

Beyond 2D Constraints

DraftSight's 3D environment uses a 'Spatial' engine, ensuring high performance even when working with complex solid geometry that would lag in lesser CAD software.
