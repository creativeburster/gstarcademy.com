Structural Engineering

STAAD.Pro

The structural standard: Analyzing and designing the world's buildings, bridges, and industrial plants for over 40 years.

Global Compliance

STAAD.Pro is a comprehensive structural analysis and design program. It is capable of performing static, dynamic, and nonlinear analysis on structures made of steel, concrete, timber, and aluminum. It is famous for its extensive library of over 90 international design codes, allowing engineers to design structures for any location on Earth.

Core Analysis Tools

  • Finite Element Analysis (FEA): Advanced mesh generation for complex slabs, plates, and solid structures.
  • Seismic & Wind Design: Automated load generation based on local building codes for natural hazard safety.
  • Steel Autodesign: Automatically select the most efficient steel sections to meet load requirements while minimizing weight.
  • Interoperability: Seamlessly exchange data with Revit, Tekla, and Bentley's own ProSteel and OpenBuildings.

Structural Integrity

STAAD.Pro is the backbone of the global structural engineering community, providing the reliability and precision needed to ensure that our built environment is safe and sustainable.
