Image Tracer

Converting raster data to CAD vectors: Automating the manual 'tracing' process for logos, floor plans, and maps.

From Pixels to Polylines

Manually tracing an image in CAD is time-consuming and prone to error. Image Tracer in DraftSight uses intelligent edge-detection algorithms to automatically generate lines, arcs, and polylines from imported raster images (BMP, JPG, PNG).

Tracing Workflow

  • Import: Place the image in the drawing and select the 'Image Tracer' command.
  • Region Selection: Choose to trace the entire image or just a specific windowed area.
  • Thresholding: Adjust the brightness/contrast threshold to distinguish between geometry and background noise.
  • Line Fitting: Control how the software fits curves and straight segments to the detected edges.

Common Use Cases

Image Tracer is widely used for importing company logos into title blocks, converting scanned paper floor plans for renovation projects, and vectorizing GIS map screenshots for site planning.
