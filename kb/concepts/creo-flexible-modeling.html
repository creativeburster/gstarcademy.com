Flexible Modeling (FMX)

Parametric when you want it, Direct when you need it: The ultimate tool for late-stage design changes and imported data repair.

Editing Without History

Historically, CAD software was either 'Parametric' (hard to change late-stage) or 'Direct' (lacking precision). Flexible Modeling (FMX) in Creo bridges this gap. It allows you to select faces or geometry features and move, rotate, or delete them directly, even if they were part of a complex parametric feature tree or imported from a different CAD system like SolidWorks or NX.

Key FMX Capabilities

  • Recognition: Automatically identify patterns (like holes or ribs) in a non-parametric 'dumb' solid.
  • Move/Offset: Select a face and drag it to a new location; Creo automatically repairs the surrounding geometry and rounds.
  • Edit Rounds: Change the radius of a blend even if you don't have the original 'Round' feature in the history tree.
  • Remove Geometry: Instantly remove features like bosses or pockets while automatically filling the resulting gap to maintain a valid solid.

Why it's a Game Changer

FMX allows engineers to make drastic changes to models in seconds that would previously have required hours of 'feature-hacking' or complete re-modeling. It is the industry standard for working in multi-CAD environments.
