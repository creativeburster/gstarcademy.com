MIRRTEXT System Variable & Mirrored Text Legibility

Technical guide to MIRRTEXT system variable. Control whether text and dimension labels invert backwards or maintain readable left-to-right orientation during MIRROR.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-10-12 Editorial process

1. Definition & Engineering Significance

The MIRRTEXT system variable dictates how text entities (MTEXT, DTEXT, and block attribute tags) behave when subjected to the MIRROR command. In architectural and mechanical design, mirroring symmetrical assemblies—such as left-hand and right-hand automotive suspension brackets or identical mirrored apartment units—is a daily operation. MIRRTEXT guarantees that while the underlying structural geometry mirrors across the reflection axis, annotations remain human-readable.

2. Technical Architecture & Key Parameters

  • Value = 0 (Default - Readable Text): Geometry mirrors correctly, but text objects preserve their natural reading direction (left-to-right, upright orientation). The text insertion point mirrors, but the character glyphs do not invert.
  • Value = 1 (Inverted Reflection): Text objects are mirrored literally, producing reversed, backwards text (mirror writing). Used for specialized manufacturing processes such as backlit automotive gauges, silkscreen masks, and engraving molds.
  • Behavior in Blocks: Attribute text within pre-defined blocks may behave differently depending on whether the block itself is non-uniformly scaled or mirrored.

3. Industry Best Practices & Failure Modes

When MIRRTEXT is erroneously set to 1, mirroring an architectural floor plan flips room names, door schedules, and dimension callouts into backward gibberish. Drafters are forced to delete and re-type dozens of text elements or manually mirror each note backwards a second time. This degrades drafting speed and introduces spelling and coordinate discrepancies.

4. Production Governance & Standard Protocols

Standard corporate drafting protocols require MIRRTEXT=0 across all standard projects. Only dedicated tooling and mold-making departments producing inverted EDM electrodes or clear acrylic casting patterns deliberately switch to MIRRTEXT=1. Verifying this setting ensures fast, error-free symmetrical drawing mirroring.
