Offshore Engineering

Bentley SACS

Engineering the ocean: Specialized structural analysis for oil platforms, offshore wind farms, and marine terminals.

Conquering the Waves

Offshore structures face unique and brutal environmental loads, from massive wave impacts and currents to high-speed winds and ice. Bentley SACS is the definitive software for offshore structural analysis. It handles everything from the initial design and fatigue life prediction to the simulation of complex 'jack-up' and transportation procedures.

Offshore Capabilities

  • Wave & Current Loading: Automatically generate complex loads based on sea state and water depth.
  • Fatigue Analysis: Predict the lifespan of structural joints (Spectral or Deterministic) under constant wave action.
  • Dynamic Response: Analyze how floating structures respond to wind and wave frequency.
  • Foundation Design: Model complex soil-structure interaction for pile and jacket foundations.

Marine Reliability

SACS is the standard for offshore engineering, used to design the world's largest energy assets and ensure they operate safely in the most hostile environments on Earth.
