Maximum Material Condition (MMC) & Bonus Tolerance

Technical guide to Maximum Material Condition (MMC) modifier per ASME Y14.5 and ISO 1101. Learn how bonus tolerance reduces manufacturing scrap.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-10-12 Editorial process

1. Definition & Engineering Significance

In Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T) per ASME Y14.5 and ISO 1101, Maximum Material Condition (MMC, designated by the circled-M modifier symbol Ⓜ) defines the state where a feature of size contains the maximum amount of material within its stated dimensional tolerance limits. For an external shaft or pin, MMC represents its maximum allowable diameter; for an internal hole, bore, or slot, MMC represents its minimum allowable diameter. When MMC is applied to a geometric tolerance in a Feature Control Frame, it unlocks the invaluable manufacturing concept of 'bonus tolerance'.

2. Technical Architecture & Key Parameters

  • MMC Definition for External Features: The maximum limit of size (e.g., a pin specified as $\varnothing 10.00 \pm 0.05$ mm reaches MMC at $\varnothing 10.05$ mm).
  • MMC Definition for Internal Features: The minimum limit of size (e.g., a hole specified as $\varnothing 10.00 \pm 0.05$ mm reaches MMC at $\varnothing 9.95$ mm).
  • Bonus Tolerance Calculation: Bonus tolerance is directly proportional to the feature's departure from MMC: $$\text{Bonus Tolerance} = |\text{Actual Size} - \text{MMC Size}|$$ $$\text{Total Permissible Geometric Tolerance} = \text{Stated Tolerance} + \text{Bonus Tolerance}$$
  • Hard Gaging Feasibility: MMC allows physical inspection using fixed-limit functional 'Go' plug or ring gages, dramatically accelerating high-volume production QC.

3. Industry Best Practices & Failure Modes

Without the MMC modifier (Regardless of Feature Size / RFS mode), a machined part whose position is off by 0.02mm is classified as scrap even if the hole was produced much larger than nominal. Under MMC, because a larger hole provides extra physical clearance for the mating bolt, the inspector adds that unused size allowance as bonus positional tolerance, turning would-be scrap into 100% functional, conforming assemblies.

4. Production Governance & Standard Protocols

Specifying MMC is standard best practice for clearance hole patterns, press-fit locating dowels, and bolted flange assemblies. Drafters and design engineers must verify that functional boundary mating conditions are satisfied while ensuring thin wall sections do not breach minimum structural requirements.
