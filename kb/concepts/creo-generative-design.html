AI in CAD

Generative Design

AI-Driven Engineering: Generating high-performance, lightweight geometry that no human could imagine.

Evolutionary Engineering

Generative Design in Creo uses AI and top-tier physics solvers to grow geometry based on your requirements. You define the 'Keep-Out' zones (where geometry cannot be), the 'Preserved' zones (where the part mounts), and the loads. Creo then iterates through thousands of variations to find the lightest possible structure that survives those loads.

Creo Generative Advantages

  • Seamless Transition: Unlike other tools, Creo's generative output is high-quality B-Rep geometry (not just a messy mesh), meaning you can continue modeling on it immediately.
  • Manufacturing Constraints: Tell the AI whether the part will be cast, 3-axis milled, or 3D printed, and it will ensure the result is actually manufacturable.
  • Cloud Solving: Use PTC's cloud compute to solve multiple design variations in parallel without locking up your workstation.
  • Materials Library: Test how different materials (Aluminum vs Titanium) affect the generated shape and mass.

The New Frontier

Generative design allows companies to create parts that are 30-50% lighter while being significantly stronger, making it a critical tool for the aerospace and electric vehicle industries.
