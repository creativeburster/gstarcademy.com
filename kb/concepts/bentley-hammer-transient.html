HAMMER Transient Analysis

Protecting the pipes: Identifying and mitigating the destructive power of 'Water Hammer' pressure surges.

Solving Transients

Sudden changes in fluid velocity—caused by pump failures or valve closures—can create massive pressure waves known as Water Hammer. These waves can rupture pipes, damage expensive pumps, and cause catastrophic system failure. Bentley HAMMER is the industry's most trusted tool for performing transient analysis to design protection systems like surge tanks and relief valves.

Key Safety Workflows

  • Surge Identification: Locate exactly where high and low-pressure transients will occur in the network.
  • Protection Design: Simulate the impact of different surge control devices (air valves, tanks) to find the optimal solution.
  • Critical Scenario Analysis: Test 'worst-case' scenarios, such as a full power failure at a major pumping station.
  • Integration: Seamlessly import network data from WaterGEMS or AutoCAD to begin transient studies.

Infrastructure Integrity

HAMMER allows engineers to guarantee the safety of water distribution systems, preventing multi-million dollar repair costs and ensuring uninterrupted service for communities.
