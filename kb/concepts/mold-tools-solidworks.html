Mold Tools

SOLIDWORKS mold design tools for core/cavity extraction, parting lines, draft analysis, and injection mold layout.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by GstarCAD Academy Editorial Team Last reviewed 2026-08-16 Editorial Process

Definition & Context

The SOLIDWORKS Mold Tools workflow builds core and cavity from a part: scale for shrinkage, apply and analyze draft, create parting lines and shut-off surfaces, generate parting surfaces, then use Tooling Split to extract core and cavity blocks. Draft Analysis and Undercut Detection guide the process.

It is a surfacing-based toolset that assumes a solid part and a chosen pull direction; side-cores and lifters are modeled manually. It produces the tooling solids, not a full moldbase (which comes from libraries or add-ins).

Why It Matters

Mold Tools let a designer derive core/cavity directly from the part, so shrinkage, draft, and parting decisions are validated on the real geometry before cutting steel.

Catching insufficient draft or undercuts here — not at the toolmaker — is where the real cost savings are.

Best Practices

  • Run Draft Analysis and set the pull direction before building parting geometry
  • Apply the correct shrinkage scale factor for the resin
  • Verify parting lines and shut-off surfaces are complete before Tooling Split
  • Address undercuts (side cores/lifters) deliberately rather than ignoring them

Common Pitfalls

  • Insufficient draft discovered only after the tool is cut
  • Incomplete parting/shut-off surfaces causing Tooling Split to fail
  • Forgetting shrinkage scaling, so molded parts come out undersized

Common Failure Scenarios & Troubleshooting

Failure / Geometric IssueRoot Cause & Mitigation Strategy
Sketch breaks or flips geometry when adjusting dimensionsSketch was under-constrained. Always apply geometric constraints (tangency, horizontal/vertical) before adding driving numerical dimensions.
Shell or Fillet feature fails on complex curved topologyCurvature radius is tighter than fillet radius or minimum wall thickness. Inspect surface curvature using Zebra stripes and eliminate zero-radius sharp corners.
Assembly performance severely lags during rotationLarge assembly mode was disabled. Suppress non-essential cosmetic features (threads, knurls) and use lightweight component representations.
