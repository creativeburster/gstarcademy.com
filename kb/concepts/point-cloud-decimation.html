Point Cloud Decimation

Optimizing massive LiDAR datasets for Revit and AutoCAD by reducing point density while preserving geometric integrity.

Balancing Data vs. Speed

A raw laser scan can contain billions of points. Loading this directly into Revit would crash most workstations. Decimation is the process of intelligently removing redundant points (e.g., in the middle of a flat floor) while keeping high density at edges, corners, and feature points.

Decimation Techniques

  • Spatial Decimation: Ensures points are at least a minimum distance apart (e.g., 5mm).
  • Curvature-Based: Keeps more points where the surface geometry changes rapidly.
  • Random Sampling: A fast but less precise method of reducing the overall percentage of points.
  • Voxel Grid: Divides space into cubes and keeps only one point per cube to ensure uniform density.

ReCap Pro Optimization

When indexing raw scans (FLS, E57, etc.) in Autodesk ReCap Pro, you can set the 'Decimation Grid' value. A higher value (e.g., 10mm) results in a smaller, faster .rcp file, which is often sufficient for architectural context modeling.
