RAM Structural System

Automating the sky: The specialized tool for high-performance analysis and design of complex building structures.

Building-Specific Intelligence

While general-purpose FEA tools can analyze anything, RAM Structural System is optimized specifically for buildings. It automates the calculation of gravity and lateral loads, the design of steel and concrete members, and the generation of complete foundation plans. This allows structural engineers to iterate on building designs much faster than using manual calculations or general-purpose software.

Core Design Features

  • Automated Load Takedown: Instantly calculate how loads from the roof and floors flow through columns to the foundation.
  • Steel Beam & Column Design: Optimize member sizes based on AISC, Eurocode, and other international standards.
  • Slab Analysis (RAM Concept): Advanced finite element analysis for post-tensioned and reinforced concrete slabs.
  • Lateral Load Analysis: Simulate how wind and seismic forces impact the building's stability.

Structural Optimization

RAM Structural System allows engineering teams to design safer, more efficient buildings with less material, reducing both costs and the carbon footprint of construction.
