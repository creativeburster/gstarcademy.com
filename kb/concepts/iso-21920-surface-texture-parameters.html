ISO 21920 Surface Texture Standard: 3D Areal Parameters (Sa, Sq, Sz)

Technical guide to the new ISO 21920 surface texture standard replacing ISO 1302 and ISO 4287. Master 3D areal parameters Sa, Sq, Sz, and Sp for precision manufacturing.

1. Definition & Mathematical Engineering Foundations

The release of the ISO 21920 series (ISO 21920-1, ISO 21920-2, and ISO 21920-3) represents the most significant overhaul of surface texture specifications in four decades, officially superseding legacy 2D profile standards ISO 4287, ISO 4288, and drawing indication standard ISO 1302. While legacy standards relied on 2D stylus line traces ($R_a$, $R_z$), modern optical metrology (confocal microscopy, coherence scanning interferometry) captures 3D areal topography ($S_a$, $S_q$, $S_z$), providing true spatial characterization of machined, ground, and additively manufactured surfaces.

2. Technical Architecture & Key Formulations

  • Sa (Areal Arithmetical Mean Height): The 3D equivalent of Ra across an evaluation area $A$:
    Sa = (1 / A) &iint;A |z(x, y)| dx dy
    Quantifies overall process consistency and general surface finish roughness.
  • Sq (Areal Root Mean Square Height): The standard deviation of surface heights:
    Sq = √[ (1 / A) &iint;A z²(x, y) dx dy ]
    Statistically more significant for optical reflectivity, scatter, and tribological contact simulations.
  • Sz (Maximum Areal Height): The sum of the largest peak height ($S_p$) and deepest pit depth ($S_v$) within the defined area ($S_z = S_p + S_v$). Essential for fluid dynamic seals and fatigue crack initiation prevention.
  • Ssk (Skewness) & Sku (Kurtosis): Measure surface height distribution symmetry and sharpness. Negative skewness ($S_{sk} < 0$) characterizes plateau-honed engine cylinder bores that retain lubrication oil.

3. Industry Failure Modes & Risk Mitigation

A persistent industrial quality issue occurs when engineers specify a legacy 2D $R_a$ on metal 3D printed (PBF-LB) components. Because powder-bed fusion produces anisotropic partially melted powder balls and vertical stair-stepping, a 2D stylus tracing parallel to scan lines registers an deceptively smooth $R_a = 4\, \mu\text{m}$, while an orthogonal areal scan reveals severe $S_z = 45\, \mu\text{m}$ crevices that act as fatal fatigue notches.

4. Production Governance & Metrological Standards

ISO 21920 introduces modernized drawing callout symbols with mandatory filter specifications (e.g., S-filter, L-filter cutoff lengths). Drafting teams updating enterprise templates to ISO 21920 ensure seamless compliance with aerospace and medical device quality audits.
