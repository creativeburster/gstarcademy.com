NX HD3D

Data in context: Visualizing complex non-geometric information directly on the 3D assembly for instant insight.

The Power of Color

Searching through thousands of parts in a spreadsheet is slow. NX HD3D (High-Definition 3D) allows you to visualize metadata directly on your assembly. It uses 'Visual Reports' to color-code components based on specific attributes, such as release status, weight, supplier, or material, allowing engineers to spot trends and issues instantly in the 3D space.

Core Visual Workflows

  • Status Reporting: Color components red if 'In Work' and green if 'Released.'
  • Property Queries: Find all parts weighing more than 1kg or all parts made from Titanium.
  • Teamcenter Integration: Pull live data from the PLM system to show which parts are currently under change.
  • Tag-Based Navigation: Click on a visual tag to see full detailed property lists without opening multiple dialog boxes.

Data-Driven Design

HD3D transforms the 3D CAD model into a visual dashboard, enabling faster decision-making and improving clarity across large, cross-functional engineering teams.
