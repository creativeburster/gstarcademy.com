3DEXPERIENCE Platform

Dassault's 3DEXPERIENCE cloud platform integrating CATIA, SOLIDWORKS, ENOVIA, SIMULIA for enterprise PLM.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-06-30 Editorial process

Definition & Context

The 3DEXPERIENCE platform is Dassault Systèmes' cloud/on-premise environment unifying CATIA, SOLIDWORKS (connected), SIMULIA, ENOVIA (PLM), DELMIA (manufacturing), and collaboration apps around a single data model. Roles and apps are provisioned from a common dashboard with built-in data management and lifecycle.

It replaces file-based workflows with a database-backed model where design, simulation, and PLM share live data, and connected SOLIDWORKS stores designs in the platform rather than local files.

Why It Matters

3DEXPERIENCE consolidates CAD, simulation, and PLM into one connected environment, targeting end-to-end digital continuity — powerful for large enterprises but a significant shift from file-based habits.

Best Practices

  • Use the platform's built-in data management instead of local files.
  • Provision only the roles/apps each user needs to control cost.
  • Adopt bookmarks/collaborative spaces for structured data organisation.
  • Plan change management — the database model differs from files.

Common Pitfalls

  • Treating connected apps like file-based ones and fighting the data model.
  • Over-provisioning roles, inflating subscription cost.
  • Poor data organisation making content hard to find.
  • Underestimating the training/adoption effort.
