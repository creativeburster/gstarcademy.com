Electrical Systems

Cabling & Harness

The central nervous system: Routing complex electrical networks through 3D mechanical assemblies with precision.

Wiring in 3D

In modern products, electrical cabling is often an afterthought that causes assembly issues. Creo Cabling allows you to route wires, cables, and bundles directly through your 3D assembly. It respects bend radius limits and physical space constraints, ensuring that your harness will actually fit during installation.

Routing & Manufacturing

  • Auto-Routing: Use logical data (schematics) to automatically route wires between designated connectors via specific paths.
  • Flattening: Instantly transform a complex 3D harness into a 2D 'Nail Board' layout for manufacturing.
  • Wire Bundling: Group multiple wires into cables or bundles with accurate diameter and mass calculations.
  • Length Accuracy: Get exact cut lengths for every wire, accounting for stripping and crimping allowances.

Electromechanical Synergy

Creo Cabling bridges the gap between electrical schematics and mechanical design, ensuring that the electrical system is an integral part of the product architecture from day one.
