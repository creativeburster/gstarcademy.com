Sheetmetal Design

From 3D form to 2D flat: Precision modeling for laser-cut and brake-folded components.

Folded Intelligence

Sheet metal parts behave differently than solid machined parts. They have constant wall thickness and deform during bending. Creo Sheetmetal provides a specialized environment with features like planar walls, flanges, rips, and punches that respect real-world material physics.

Manufacturing Readiness

  • Flat Patterns: Instantly unfold a complex 3D assembly to a 2D DXF for CNC laser or waterjet cutting.
  • Bend Tables: Define K-factors or Y-factors to account for material stretch based on specific gauge and tooling.
  • Corner Reliefs: Automatically add reliefs to prevent material tearing during the folding process.
  • Form Features: Add louvers, dimples, and extruded holes from a library of standard punch tools.

Design for Fabrication

By using Creo's Sheetmetal tools, engineers ensure that their designs are not just visually correct, but physically possible to fabricate on the factory floor.
