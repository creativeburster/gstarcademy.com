Parasolid vs ACIS Geometric Modeling Kernels

The architectural comparison of the two dominant commercial 3D boundary-representation kernels powering the global CAD software industry.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-09-30 Editorial process

1. Definition & Engineering Significance

Nearly all professional MCAD and AEC software relies on either Siemens Parasolid (SolidWorks, NX, Onshape) or Spatial/Dassault ACIS (GstarCAD, Inventor, AutoCAD, BricsCAD). Understanding their kernel differences explains data exchange behavior and file translation tolerance shifts.

2. Key Technical Characteristics & Rules

  • Parasolid (.x_t): Renowned for robust complex surface blending, high-speed multi-body booleans, and industrial manufacturing dominance.
  • ACIS (.sat): The historical bedrock of AutoCAD 3D solids and Architectural Desktop; highly flexible open C++ class architecture.
  • Kernel Translation Discrepancy: Exporting from Parasolid to ACIS requires approximating spline curve tolerances, occasionally producing micro-gaps.
