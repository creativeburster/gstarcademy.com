NX Progressive Die Wizard

Precision at speed: Automating the design of complex stamping dies for multi-stage sheet metal forming.

Automating the Strip

Designing a progressive die requires planning how a flat metal strip will be punched, bent, and formed across multiple stations. NX Progressive Die Wizard provides an expert-guided workflow that automates strip development, blank layout optimization, and the creation of die structures and inserts.

Wizard Features

  • Blank Layout: Optimize the nesting of parts on the strip to maximize material utilization and minimize waste.
  • Strip Development: Interactively define the sequence of operations (pierce, notch, bend, form) along the strip.
  • Punch & Die Creation: Automatically generate the complex 3D geometry of punches and die inserts based on the strip layout.
  • Standard Tooling: Use comprehensive libraries of standard die components (fasteners, springs, pillars) to build the die base.

Manufacturing Intelligence

By integrating die-making knowledge into the CAD environment, the Progressive Die Wizard allows tooling engineers to focus on strategy rather than geometry, leading to much faster delivery times for complex stamping projects.
