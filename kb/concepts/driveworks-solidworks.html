DriveWorks (Design Automation)

DriveWorks automation for SOLIDWORKS — configure-to-order product design driven by rules and customer specifications.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-07-28 Editorial process

Definition & Context

DriveWorks is design-automation software for SOLIDWORKS that generates configure-to-order variants from rules. You capture a master model's dimensions, features, and configurations, define rules and an input form, and DriveWorks produces new parts, assemblies, and drawings automatically from customer specifications.

It ranges from DriveWorksXpress (free, inside SOLIDWORKS) to DriveWorks Pro (forms, web/DriveWorks Live, document generation). It is a rules engine layered on SOLIDWORKS, not a modeling feature.

Why It Matters

For configure-to-order products, DriveWorks turns the repetitive “change a few dimensions and regenerate drawings” cycle into an automated, error-resistant flow — large time savings on variant-heavy product lines.

Because it is rule-driven, the payoff depends on a robust, well-organized master model that survives parametric changes.

Best Practices

  • Build a robust master model that regenerates cleanly across the full input range
  • Prototype rules in DriveWorksXpress before committing to Pro
  • Keep captured parameters and rules organized and documented
  • Validate generated variants across the input range, not just one case

Common Pitfalls

  • Automating a fragile model that fails to rebuild at rule extremes
  • Under-testing the input space, so edge-case variants break
  • Treating DriveWorks as a modeler rather than a rules layer over SOLIDWORKS
