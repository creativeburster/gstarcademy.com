Real-Time Analysis

Simulation Live

Instant Insight: Breaking the barrier between design and analysis with real-time, solver-less feedback.

Solving at the Speed of Thought

Traditionally, Finite Element Analysis (FEA) required a designer to stop modeling, mesh the part, apply loads, and wait for a solver. Creo Simulation Live (powered by Ansys) eliminates this wait. It runs in the background using your GPU, providing stress, vibration, and thermal results that update instantly as you change dimensions or features.

Key Features

  • Structural Analysis: See von Mises stress and displacement as you add ribs or increase wall thickness.
  • Thermal Analysis: Visualize heat distribution and gradients in electronics enclosures or heat sinks.
  • Modal Analysis: Identify natural frequencies to ensure your part doesn't vibrate dangerously in its operating environment.
  • Fluid Flow: (Plus version) Analyze internal and external fluid flows in real-time.

Validation-Led Design

By providing feedback while you design, Simulation Live helps you catch structural flaws earlier, leading to lighter, stronger, and more reliable products without the traditional 'design-then-analyze' bottleneck.
