Model-Based Design

PMI (MBD)

The 3D master: Embedding full manufacturing intelligence directly into the CAD geometry to eliminate 2D drafting.

Semantic Information

PMI (Product and Manufacturing Information) allows you to define dimensions, Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T), and notes directly on the 3D model. Unlike a simple text note, PMI in NX is 'semantic'—it is linked to the geometry, meaning downstream tools like CAM or CMM can read the tolerance automatically to adjust toolpaths or inspection routines.

MBD Workflows

  • Model Views: Organize annotations into logical 3D 'snapshots' that show only what is relevant for a specific process.
  • Technical Data Package (TDP): Export your annotated 3D model as a 3D PDF or STEP AP242 for suppliers who don't use NX.
  • Validation: Use built-in tools to ensure that your GD&T scheme is fully defined and follows ASME/ISO standards.
  • Sectioning: Add dynamic sections to your 3D views to show internal PMI in complex components.

Digital Transformation

By adopting PMI and MBD, companies can reduce drawing creation time by up to 80% and significantly reduce the risk of manual data entry errors in manufacturing.
