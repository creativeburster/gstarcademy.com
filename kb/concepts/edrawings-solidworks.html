eDrawings (SOLIDWORKS)

A free lightweight viewer/publisher for SOLIDWORKS files — measure, mark up, animate, share without a SOLIDWORKS seat.

By LearnCAD Editorial Team Reviewed by Mechanical Review Desk Last reviewed 2026-05-24 Editorial process

Definition

eDrawings is a free Windows / macOS / iOS / Android viewer for EPRT, EASM, EDRW (the eDrawings native formats) and many other CAD formats. Save As from SOLIDWORKS produces eDrawings files with viewable, measurable geometry but without the original parametric features. eDrawings Pro adds markup, dynamic cross-section, and measurement tools.

Why it matters

eDrawings is how a SOLIDWORKS shop shares design intent with clients, fabricators, and suppliers who don't have CAD licenses. It also produces lightweight files (much smaller than STEP) for review-only contexts.

Common pitfalls

  • Sending an .eprt expecting downstream editing — eDrawings is review-only by design.
  • Sharing eDrawings files with embedded original geometry that should have been hidden (IP concern).
  • Confusing eDrawings with 3D Markup or 3DEXPERIENCE review apps.
