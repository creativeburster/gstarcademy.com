The Topological Naming Problem in Parametric CAD

The notorious software engineering challenge where upstream feature edits re-index internal face/edge identifiers, causing downstream fillets and sketches to fail.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-09-30 Editorial process

1. Definition & Engineering Significance

When an engineer alters an upstream sketch dimension, the modeling kernel regenerates the B-Rep body. Without robust persistent naming algorithms, 'Face_12' may inadvertently become 'Face_14', breaking subsequent sketches, mates, or chamfers that referenced that specific face. Solving this is what distinguishes production-grade CAD kernels.

2. Key Technical Characteristics & Rules

  • Root Cause: Kernels assign volatile integer IDs to faces/edges during iterative boolean evaluation.
  • Persistent Naming Solutions: Tracking geometric derivation histories (e.g., 'the face generated by sweeping Edge_3 along Path_1').
  • Design Best Practice: Anchor sketches and mates to master coordinate planes and datums rather than volatile model faces.
