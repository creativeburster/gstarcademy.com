iTwin & Digital Twin

Bentley's iTwin platform for creating digital twins from MicroStation infrastructure models with IoT and analytics.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-07-28 Editorial process

Definition & Context

Bentley's iTwin platform creates infrastructure digital twins from engineering models (including MicroStation and OpenXXX applications): it aggregates design geometry with reality data, IoT/sensor feeds, and analytics into a continuously updated virtual representation of an asset. iTwin.js and iModels underpin the data layer.

iModels are the versioned, distributed database that carries the twin's data; MicroStation models feed geometry, while iTwin services add change tracking, visualization, and analytics across the asset lifecycle.

Why It Matters

Digital twins extend a design model into operations — monitoring, simulation, and prediction on live data — which is where much of infrastructure's lifecycle value sits.

Because iTwin builds on iModels and versioning, it connects design authoring (MicroStation) to an operational, analyzable twin rather than a static deliverable.

Best Practices

  • Feed clean, well-structured MicroStation/iModel data so the twin is reliable
  • Integrate reality and IoT data deliberately, matching coordinate systems
  • Use iModel versioning/change tracking to keep the twin current
  • Scope analytics to real operational questions rather than collecting data for its own sake

Common Pitfalls

  • Treating a static model export as a digital twin (a twin is continuously updated)
  • Coordinate/format mismatches when fusing reality and design data
  • Twin data drifting stale without a change-tracking pipeline
