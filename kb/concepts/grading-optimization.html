Grading Optimization

Automating the complex site grading process to minimize earthwork costs while meeting structural and drainage constraints.

Constraint-Based Leveling

Traditional site grading is a trial-and-error process. Grading Optimization allows civil engineers to define 'Rules' for specific areas (e.g., 'This parking lot must have a 2% slope for drainage'). The engine then runs thousands of simulations to find the surface that balances cut/fill volumes while adhering to all rules.

Key Design Elements

  • Grading Objects: Pads, ponds, curbs, and zones that contain specific slope or elevation rules.
  • Balance Volume: Set a target for net earthwork (e.g., 1000m³ extra fill or perfectly balanced).
  • Drainage Lines: Force the optimization engine to direct water along specific paths.
  • Visualization: Real-time heatmaps show where the surface is changing as the optimizer runs.

Workflow Integration

Once satisfied with the result, the optimized surface is brought back into Civil 3D as a standard Surface object, ready for detailed cross-sectioning and documentation.
