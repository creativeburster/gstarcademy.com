NURBS Degree & Geometric Continuity (G0, G1, G2, G3)

The mathematical conditions governing positional, tangential, curvature, and acceleration transitions between adjacent freeform CAD curves and surfaces.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-09-30 Editorial process

1. Definition & Engineering Significance

Automotive exterior styling and consumer industrial design mandate smooth light reflections without visual creases. Geometric continuity levels define how adjacent NURBS patches meet: G0 (positional touch), G1 (tangent slope), G2 (equal radius of curvature), and G3 (equal rate of curvature change).

2. Key Technical Characteristics & Rules

  • G0 (Point Continuity): Surfaces touch at their boundary edge, but form a sharp crease or razor ridge.
  • G1 (Tangential Continuity): Surface normals match along the seam; zebra analysis stripes reflect with angled elbows.
  • G2 (Curvature Continuity): Radius of curvature transitions smoothly; zebra stripes align with smooth continuous bends.
  • G3 (Class-A / Torsion Continuity): Acceleration of curvature is continuous; eliminates all subtle highlights on luxury automotive body panels.

3. Geometric Continuity Hierarchy & Evaluation

Surface continuity governs how adjacent patches meet and reflect light across their shared boundary curves:

  • G0 (Positional Continuity): Patches share a coincident edge boundary with no gap, but can exhibit a sharp angle or crease.
  • G1 (Tangency Continuity): Surface normal vectors align along the seam, producing a smooth transition with no crease (visible as a sharp kink in zebra reflection stripes).
  • G2 (Curvature Continuity): Normal vectors and radii of curvature match continuously across the boundary, yielding seamless reflection highlights mandatory for automotive Class-A surfaces.
  • G3 (Torsion / Acceleration Continuity): The rate of curvature change is continuous, eliminating microscopic highlight acceleration shifts.

4. Real-Time Inspection & Zebra Diagnostic Analysis

In high-end industrial design, always activate dynamic Zebra stripe shaders in the CAD viewport. Discontinuous zebra stripes indicate G0 gaps; sharp zigzag breaks indicate G1 tangency; smooth continuous zebra ribbons confirm true G2/G3 curvature compliance.
