AutoCAD vs AutoCAD LT: Feature Comparison, 3D, LISP Scripting & Value Analysis

Detailed comparison of full AutoCAD vs AutoCAD LT: 3D solid modeling, AutoLISP scripting, specialized toolsets, network licensing, pricing, and alternative DWG platforms.

Author: Gstarcademy Engineering Editorial Board Updated: 2026-08-16

⚡ Quick Answer / Key Takeaway

AutoCAD LT is restricted to 2D drafting and annotation with limited scripting. Full AutoCAD includes 3D modeling and rendering, full AutoLISP/VBA/.NET API programming, specialized industry toolsets (Architecture, MEP, Electrical, Mechanical), and action recording. If you need 3D or custom LISP automations, full AutoCAD or perpetual alternatives (GstarCAD) are essential.

Step-by-Step Instructions & Commands

1. 2D Drafting vs. 3D Solid Modeling

AutoCAD LT provides complete 2D drawing, editing, dimensioning, and plotting capabilities identical to full AutoCAD. However, LT contains zero 3D modeling tools—no 3D solid primitives (Box, Cylinder), no Boolean operations (Union, Subtract), no 3D orbit, and no photorealistic rendering.

2. Programming & Customization APIs (AutoLISP, .NET, ARX)

Full AutoCAD supports AutoLISP, Visual LISP, VBA, .NET (C#/VB), and ObjectARX (C++) for building enterprise custom commands and automated workflows. AutoCAD LT recently added basic AutoLISP support, but lacks ARX, .NET, and advanced API capabilities required for third-party industry plugins.

3. Specialized Toolsets Included with Full AutoCAD

Since Autodesk consolidated AutoCAD subscriptions, full AutoCAD licenses include 7 specialized industry toolsets: Architecture, Mechanical, Electrical, MEP, Plant 3D, Map 3D, and Raster Design. AutoCAD LT does not include any specialized toolsets.

4. Pricing & High-Value Alternatives (GstarCAD / BricsCAD)

AutoCAD costs ~$1,975/year, while AutoCAD LT costs ~$505/year. For firms needing full 2D+3D+AutoLISP capabilities without steep annual subscription fees, alternative DWG platforms like GstarCAD and BricsCAD provide perpetual licenses with full DWG and LISP compatibility at a fraction of the cost.

Opening a 3D DWG file in AutoCAD LT AutoCAD LT can view existing 3D wireframes and shaded models in read-only projection, but users cannot edit 3D solid geometry, push/pull faces, or perform Boolean operations without full AutoCAD.

Cross-CAD Compatibility & Alternatives

GstarCAD Professional includes full 2D drafting, 3D ACIS solid modeling, AutoLISP/GRX APIs, and dynamic blocks under a perpetual license, serving as a powerful drop-in replacement for both AutoCAD LT and full AutoCAD.

Engineering Best Practices Checklist

  • Choose AutoCAD LT only if your scope is strictly 2D drafting with no third-party plugin requirements.
  • Choose full AutoCAD or GstarCAD if your workflow relies on custom AutoLISP scripts or 3D solid operations.
  • Calculate multi-year ROI comparing annual subscription software against perpetual license options.
