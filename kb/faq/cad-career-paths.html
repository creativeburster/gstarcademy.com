CAD Career Paths — Roles, Skills & Salary Ranges

Career guide for beginners: job roles (drafter, designer, BIM manager, CAE analyst), required skills, and industry salary expectations.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-06-30

What CAD careers are available?

CAD Drafter/Technician (entry), Design Engineer (mid), Senior Designer/Lead (senior), BIM Manager/Coordinator (BIM), CAE Analyst (simulation), CAD Manager (IT/standards), Application Engineer (vendor side), and Computational Design Specialist (parametric/generative).

What certifications matter?

Autodesk Certified Beginner (ACP) for AutoCAD/Revit/Inventor, SOLIDWORKS CSWA/CSWP/CSWE progression, Siemens NX Associate/Specialist, and PTC Creo certification. For BIM: buildingSMART Beginner Certification, BIM Level 2 qualifications (UK).

What salary can I expect?

Varies by region. US ranges: CAD Drafter $40-60K, Design Engineer $65-95K, BIM Manager $85-130K, CAE Analyst $80-120K, Computational Designer $90-150K. Add 20-40% for senior/lead roles. Specialized skills (CFD, generative design) command premium rates.
