How to Resolve ANSYS Mechanical Contact Penetration & Rigid Body Motion Errors?

Rigid body motion errors (unconstrained degrees of freedom) and excessive contact penetration happen when initial gaps exist in frictional contact pairs or boundary fixtures are insufficient. Resolve by running Contact Tool initial status, applying Weak Springs, and switching to Augmented Lagrange with normal stiffness tuning.

Direct Diagnostic Solution & Protocol

Quick Resolution: Rigid body motion errors (unconstrained degrees of freedom) and excessive contact penetration happen when initial gaps exist in frictional contact pairs or boundary fixtures are insufficient. Resolve by running Contact Tool initial status, applying Weak Springs, and switching to Augmented Lagrange with normal stiffness tuning.

Step-by-Step Engineering Execution

Step 1: Check Initial Contact Status via Contact Tool

Under Connections, right-click → Insert → Contact Tool. Click 'Generate Initial Contact Results'. Look for contact pairs flagged as 'Far Open' or 'Near Open'.

Step 2: Adjust Pinball Region & Interface Treatment

In Contact Pair details, change 'Interface Treatment' to 'Adjust to Touch' or 'Add Offset, Ramped Effects'. Expand the Pinball Radius so the solver detects mating faces across CAD tolerance gaps.

Step 3: Switch Formulation to Augmented Lagrange

Change Contact Formulation from Pure Penalty to 'Augmented Lagrange'. Set Normal Stiffness factor to 1.0 (or 0.1 for soft materials), preventing unphysical penetration while ensuring convergence.

Step 4: Enable Weak Springs & Multi-Step Preload

Under Analysis Settings, toggle 'Weak Springs' to ON. In multi-body assemblies, solve Step 1 with small bolt preload or displacement to establish contact before applying full rated loads.
