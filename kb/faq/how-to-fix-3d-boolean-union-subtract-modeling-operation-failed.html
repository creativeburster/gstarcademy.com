How to Fix 3D Boolean UNION, SUBTRACT & INTERSECT 'Modeling Operation Error' in CAD

Resolve ACIS 3D solid Boolean failures, non-manifold edges, and self-intersecting sweep/loft errors in GstarCAD using SOLIDEDIT Check, Clean, and Separate.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by GstarCAD Academy Editorial Team Last reviewed 2026-11-03 Editorial Process
⚡ Quick Engineering Answer (TL;DR)

When UNION or SUBTRACT fails with a Modeling Operation Error, run SOLIDEDIT -> Body -> Check and SOLIDEDIT -> Body -> Clean on both solids to strip redundant coplanar edges, avoid zero-thickness knife edges (overlap cutters by 0.1 mm), and move geometry near 0,0,0 before performing Boolean operations.

1. Root Cause & DWG Database Symptom Analysis

During 3D mechanical or structural modeling in GstarCAD, Boolean operations (UNION, SUBTRACT, INTERSECT) occasionally abort with an ACIS kernel alert: "Modeling Operation Error: Non-manifold edge detected" or "Inconsistent face-body relationships".

2. Step-by-Step Resolution in GstarCAD & AutoCAD

Step 1: Avoid Zero-Thickness 'Knife-Edge' Tangency & Coplanar Cutter Faces

The #1 cause of Boolean `SUBTRACT` failure is making the cutting solid end exactly flush with the face of the target body, or making a hole tangent to a cylinder wall (creating a zero-thickness non-manifold edge). Always extend subtracting tool bodies 0.5 mm to 1.0 mm past the target surface so the intersection loop is clean.

Step 2: Run SOLIDEDIT -> Body -> Check & Clean

Type SOLIDEDIT → Body → Check and select the problematic 3D solid to verify ACIS topological validity. Then run SOLIDEDIT → Body → Clean to delete redundant imprinted edges and vertices left behind by previous edits.

Step 3: Perform 3D Booleans Near Origin (0,0,0) — Not at Civil Coordinates

If your 3D bridge pier sits at UTM coordinates $X = 650,000,000\text{ mm}$, double-precision floating-point rounding leaves only 6–7 digits of mantissa for ACIS surface intersection math. Move or construct the 3D solid near 0,0,0 and Xref it into the civil site plan!

4. One-Click AutoLISP Fix Script (AutoLISP 3D Solid ACIS Topology Cleaner & Interference Checker)

(defun c:CLEAN-3DSOLIDS ( / ss i ent)
  (if (setq ss (ssget '((0 . "3DSOLID"))))
    (progn
      (setq i 0)
      (while (< i (sslength ss))
        (setq ent (ssname ss i) i (1+ i))
        (command "_.SOLIDEDIT" "_B" "_L" ent "" "_X")))) ; Body -> Clean
  (princ "\n[ACIS 3D Doctor] Cleaned redundant edges and vertices on selected 3DSOLIDs.")
  (princ)
)
