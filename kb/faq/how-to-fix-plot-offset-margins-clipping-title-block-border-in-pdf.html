How to Fix Plot Offset & Printable Margins Clipping Title Block Borders in PDF

Prevent cut-off title block borders when plotting to PDF in GstarCAD and AutoCAD by selecting ISO Full Bleed paper sizes, Plot to Extents, and Center the Plot.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by Zhang Mingyuan, B.Eng. (Distinguished DWG Graphics Kernel & GRX SDK Architect) Last reviewed 2026-11-03 Editorial Process & Testbed
⚡ Quick Engineering Answer (TL;DR)

In the Page Setup / Plot dialog, switch Paper Size from standard 'ISO A1 / A3' to 'ISO full bleed A1 (841.00 x 594.00 MM)' or 'ISO expand A3', set Plot Area to 'Extents' or 'Layout', check 'Center the plot', and keep Scale strictly at 1:1 (never 'Fit to paper' on scaled drawings).

1. Root Cause & DWG Database Symptom Analysis

When printing a Paper Space layout to PDF, the outer right or bottom border line of your title block frequently disappears or gets clipped off—even though the border rectangle fits inside an $841 \times 594\text{ mm}$ A1 sheet. This happens because standard ISO/ANSI paper definitions reserve a 5 mm to 17 mm unprintable hardware roller margin around the sheet perimeter.

2. Step-by-Step Resolution in GstarCAD & AutoCAD

Step 1: Always Select 'ISO Full Bleed' Paper Sizes for PDF Plotting

Open PAGESETUP or PLOT in GstarCAD with the built-in DWG To PDF.pc3 plotter selected. In the Paper size dropdown, scroll past standard `ISO A1` and choose ISO full bleed A1 (841.00 x 594.00 MM) or ISO full bleed A3 (420.00 x 297.00 MM). Full Bleed sizes have 0.00 mm margins on all four sides!

Step 2: Configure Plot Area = Window/Extents + Center the Plot + 1:1 Scale

Set What to plot to Extents (or a precise `Window` snapped to the outer title block corners), check Center the plot, and verify that Plot scale is locked to 1:1. Never check Fit to paper on engineering sheets, as it shrinks your `1:100` bar scale to `1:103.4`!

Step 3: Zero Out Custom Paper Printable Area in .PC3 Properties

If using a custom physical plotter `.pc3`, click Properties → Modify Standard Paper Sizes (Printable Area), select your sheet size, and set Top, Bottom, Left, and Right margins to 0.00.

3. System Variables & Command Diagnostic Table

Variable / Command Default / Target Value Diagnostic Function & Fix Behavior
ISO full bleed A1 / A3 0.00 mm Margins Eliminates clipped outer title block borders in PDF output
Standard ISO A1 / A3 5 mm .. 17 mm Margins Clips geometry near sheet edges to simulate physical printer rollers
Plot Scale = 1:1 Mandatory for Engineering Preserves exact metric/imperial bar scale calibration on paper
Plot Offset (X=0, Y=0) Or 'Center the plot' Prevents asymmetric left/bottom border shifts
