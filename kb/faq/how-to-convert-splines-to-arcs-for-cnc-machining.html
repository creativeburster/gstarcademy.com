How to Convert Splines to Bi-Arcs for CNC Laser & Plasma Cutting?

CNC controllers execute G01 linear and G02/G03 circular arc moves, but cannot process high-degree NURBS splines directly without freezing. Convert splines to smooth bi-arc polylines using PEDIT with Decurve/Spline precision, or utilize specialized Spline-to-Arc commands with defined chord tolerance.

Direct Diagnostic Solution & Protocol

Quick Resolution: CNC controllers execute G01 linear and G02/G03 circular arc moves, but cannot process high-degree NURBS splines directly without freezing. Convert splines to smooth bi-arc polylines using PEDIT with Decurve/Spline precision, or utilize specialized Spline-to-Arc commands with defined chord tolerance.

Step-by-Step Engineering Execution

Step 1: Check Why CNC Controls Reject Splines

G-code post-processors unequipped with NURBS interpolation convert splines into tens of thousands of micro-segments (G01), causing controller buffer starvation and faceted cut edges.

Step 2: Execute PEDIT Spline Conversion

Type PEDIT, select the spline. When prompted 'Do you want to turn it into one? <Y>', press Enter. Specify precision (e.g., 5 to 10).

Step 3: Apply Spline-to-Arc Bi-Arc Approximation

In GstarCAD, use the specialized SPLINEMERG / FLATTEN or Express Tool 'Spline to Polyline'. Specify a chordal tolerance (e.g., 0.01 mm) to replace spline spans with true tangent G02/G03 arcs.

Step 4: Verify Segment Count & G-Code Output

Type LIST to verify the resulting object is a lightweight 2D Polyline (LWPOLYLINE) containing arc bulges, reducing CNC file size by up to 85%.
