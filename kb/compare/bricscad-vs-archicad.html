BricsCAD vs ArchiCAD

A structured comparison of BricsCAD and ArchiCAD for professionals evaluating CAD software options.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-06-30 Editorial process

Overview

BricsCAD and ArchiCAD are both established tools in the DWG-CAD / BIM space, but they serve different market segments and design philosophies. This comparison breaks down their key differences to help you make an informed decision.

BricsCAD is focused on 2D/3D + BIM and is primarily used by Multi-discipline firms. ArchiCAD focuses on Architectural BIM and targets Architects, small practices. Understanding these distinctions is critical before committing to a software ecosystem that may define your workflow for years.

BricsCAD — Key Strengths

BricsCAD (Perpetual licenses) excels in 2D/3D + BIM workflows. It is the preferred choice for Multi-discipline firms who need:

  • DWG-native with BIM capabilities
  • AI-assisted modeling
  • Sheet metal + mechanical
  • Perpetual licensing

ArchiCAD — Key Strengths

ArchiCAD (Subscription + perpetual) excels in Architectural BIM workflows. It is the preferred choice for Architects, small practices who need:

  • OpenBIM/IFC native
  • Morph modeling tool
  • Teamwork server
  • Algorithmic design (Param-O)

When to Choose BricsCAD

Choose BricsCAD if your primary requirements include:

  • Your team or industry standard is built around the DWG-CAD ecosystem
  • You need dwg-native with bim capabilities as a core capability
  • Your workflow involves 2D/3D + BIM as the primary design activity
  • Budget allows for perpetual licenses pricing

When to Choose ArchiCAD

Choose ArchiCAD if your primary requirements include:

  • Your team or industry standard is built around the BIM ecosystem
  • You need openbim/ifc native as a core capability
  • Your workflow involves Architectural BIM as the primary design activity
  • Budget allows for subscription + perpetual pricing

File Compatibility & Interoperability

When evaluating BricsCAD vs ArchiCAD, file exchange is a key consideration:

  • Native formats: Each software uses its own native file format optimized for internal performance
  • Exchange formats: Standard exchange formats (STEP, IGES, IFC, DXF) enable cross-software workflows
  • Data loss considerations: Parametric history, custom properties, and software-specific features may not transfer between platforms
  • Collaboration strategy: For mixed environments, establish a neutral exchange format protocol early in the project lifecycle

Verdict & Recommendation

Both BricsCAD and ArchiCAD are capable tools with distinct advantages. The right choice depends on your specific context:

  • For multi-discipline firms: BricsCAD offers a mature ecosystem with dwg-native with bim capabilities
  • For architects: ArchiCAD provides openbim/ifc native as its primary differentiator
  • For teams transitioning: Consider trial periods with both and evaluate based on your actual project files and workflows

We recommend testing both platforms with your actual production files before committing to a multi-year licensing decision.
