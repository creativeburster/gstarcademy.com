Sheet Metal K-Factor, Bend Allowance (BA) & Bend Deduction (BD) Chart for CAD/CAM

Exact sheet metal flat pattern development equations, K-Factor lookup tables by material and V-die ratio, and clean DXF export rules for CNC laser and press brake programming.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by GstarCAD Academy Editorial Team Last reviewed 2026-11-03 Editorial Process

1. Engineering Architecture & Core Methodology

Unfolding a 3D sheet metal bracket into a 2D flat pattern in GstarCAD for CNC fiber laser cutting and press brake bending requires exact compensation for plastic neutral-axis shift. Using a wrong K-Factor or mixing up Bend Allowance ($BA$) with Bend Deduction ($BD$) causes flange lengths to miss tolerance by $0.5\text{ mm}$ to $2.0\text{ mm}$ per bend.

2. Step-by-Step Production Workflow & Command Modules

1. The Neutral Axis & K-Factor Definition ($K = t_n / T$)

During bending, the inner face compresses while the outer face stretches. The zero-strain Neutral Axis shifts inward to a distance $t_n$ from the inside surface. The K-Factor is the dimensionless ratio $K = t_n / T$ (typically $0.33$ for air-bent cold-rolled steel with $R = T$, and $0.44$ for $R \ge 2T$).

2. Bend Allowance ($BA$) vs. Outside Setback ($OSSB$) & Bend Deduction ($BD$)

For bend angle $A$ (in degrees), inside bend radius $R$, material thickness $T$, and K-Factor $K$:

$$BA = \frac{\pi \cdot A}{180^\circ} \cdot (R + K \cdot T)$$$$BD = 2 \cdot (R + T) \cdot \tan\left(\frac{A}{2}\right) - BA$$

When dimensioning flanges to the virtual sharp apex (Outside Mold Line $L_1, L_2$), total flat length is $L_{\text{flat}} = L_1 + L_2 - BD$.

3. CNC Laser DXF Layer Preparation in GstarCAD

Place outer cut contours on layer CUT_OUTER (Color 7, closed zero-width LWPOLYLINE), internal cutouts on CUT_INNER (Color 3), and press-brake bend centerlines on BEND_UP (Color 1) and BEND_DOWN (Color 5) so Trumpf/Bystronic/Amada CAM software parses bend directions automatically.

4. Automation Script & Reference Implementation (AutoLISP Sheet Metal Bend Allowance (BA) & Bend Deduction (BD) Calculator)

(defun c:CALC-SHEET-BEND ( / t r k ang rad ba ossb bd)
  (setq t   (getreal "\nEnter Sheet Thickness T (mm): ")
        r   (getreal "\nEnter Inside Bend Radius R (mm): ")
        k   (getreal "\nEnter K-Factor (e.g. 0.33 to 0.44): ")
        ang (getreal "\nEnter Bend Angle A in Degrees (e.g. 90): "))
  (setq rad  (* pi (/ ang 180.0))
        ba   (* rad (+ r (* k t)))
        ossb (* (+ r t) (/ (sin (* 0.5 rad)) (cos (* 0.5 rad))))
        bd   (- (* 2.0 ossb) ba))
  (princ (strcat "\n[Sheet Metal] BA = " (rtos ba 2 3) " mm | OSSB = " (rtos ossb 2 3)
                 " mm | Bend Deduction BD = " (rtos bd 2 3) " mm"))
  (princ)
)

5. Production QA Verification & Competency Checklist

  • Confirm with your fabrication shop whether their V-die width is 6T, 8T, or 12T before locking the inside bend radius R.
  • Convert all splines to tangent arcs (PEDIT / SPLINEDIT) and run OVERKILL before exporting sheet metal DXFs.
  • Export flat pattern DXFs in R12 or 2004 ASCII DXF format at 1:1 scale with zero title blocks or dimensions on cut layers.

Enterprise AC1032 Database Internals & Cross-Version Diagnostics

1. DWG Header & Registry Persistence Scope

Variables and object properties fall into three persistence scopes: Drawing-Saved (DXF Header) variables that travel with the .dwg file to subcontractors, Registry-Saved (Profile .arg) settings that persist per Windows workstation user, and Session-Volatile states that reset on every launch unless locked inside acaddoc.lsp. Always verify persistence scope via SYSVDLG before issuing sheets to an ISO 19650 Common Data Environment (CDE).

2. Multi-Core Regeneration & Memory Hygiene

When processing large civil corridors, dense plant P&IDs, or 50-sheet architectural Xref trees, unpurged Registered Application IDs (REGAPP), orphaned DGN stroke dictionaries, and redundant annotation scales degrade spatial index (INDEXCTL = 3) lookup speed. Executing a pre-submittal AUDIT, command-line -PURGE → Regapps, and OVERKILL pass keeps file open times under 2 seconds on GstarCAD's multi-threaded graphics kernel.

3. Hybrid AutoCAD + GstarCAD Team Coexistence
