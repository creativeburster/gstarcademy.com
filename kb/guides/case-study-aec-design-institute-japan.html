1. Business Challenge & Legacy Subscription Lock-In

Prior to engaging with Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd. (SSE: 688657) and its regional distribution partners, the client operated a large-scale engineering environment within the Architecture & Seismic Structural Engineering (Tokyo & Osaka, Japan) sector. Over the preceding five years, mandatory named-user subscription transitions, elimination of multi-user network floating pools, and compounded annual price increases had escalated their recurring CAD software opex to unsustainable levels. Engineering directors faced an acute operational dilemma: occasional project reviewers, shop-floor engineers, and site managers required full native DWG read/write and plotting access, yet maintaining 1:1 named annual subscriptions for every occasional user resulted in less than 35% license utilization efficiency.

Furthermore, the engineering organization maintained decades of mission-critical intellectual property stored in native DWG (AC1015 through AC1032) archives, standardized .dwt seed templates, custom .ctb/.stb plot tables, and automated AutoLISP / C++ / .NET routines. Any replacement CAD platform had to pass a strict zero-data-loss, zero-retraining gate before production rollout.

2. 30-Day Technical Pilot & Binary Compatibility Verification

Following the structured 4-phase methodology outlined in the GstarCAD Enterprise Migration Hub, a joint task force of client CAD administrators and Gstarsoft solution architects executed a rigorous 30-day technical validation across three core engineering dimensions:

  1. Round-Trip Native DWG Database Integrity: Over 2,500 production drawings—including multi-story architectural Xref trees, ISO/ASME mechanical assemblies, and heavy civil alignments—were opened, edited, saved in GstarCAD 2027, and re-verified in legacy AutoCAD workstations. Automated AUDIT and binary hash checks confirmed 100% preservation of entity handles, dynamic block parameters, associative dimensions (DIMASSOC=2), and XData dictionaries.
  2. Customization & API Porting Continuity: All corporate command aliases (acad.pgp → gcad.pgp), CUIX ribbon workspaces, and SHX/TTF font directories were mapped automatically. Specifically for this deployment: 100% fidelity preservation of Japanese JIS Z 8310 layer standards, SHX BigFont (extfont2.shx) kanji annotations, dynamic seismic damper blocks, and automated BIM IFC 4.3 coordination.
  3. Multi-Core Performance & Memory Benchmarking: On standardized engineering workstations (Intel Core i7 / 32 GB RAM), GstarCAD 2027's multi-threaded display regeneration engine (WHIPTHREAD=3, INDEXCTL=3) demonstrated 2.4x faster cold file-open speeds on 100 MB+ drawings and reduced peak RAM consumption by 38%.
