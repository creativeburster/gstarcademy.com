3. AutoLISP Equivalent Circular/Rectangular Duct Hydraulic Calculator

When ceiling soffit height is restricted by structural beams, HVAC engineers must convert circular duct diameters ($D_e$) into low-profile rectangular ducts ($a \times b$) with identical friction head loss using the Huebscher equation $D_e = 1.30 \cdot \frac{(a \cdot b)^{0.625}}{(a + b)^{0.25}}$:

;; GstarCAD 2027 — ASHRAE Huebscher Equivalent Rectangular Duct Calculator & Labeler
(defun c:GCAD_DUCT_EQ ( / a b de v q pt txt)
  (setq a (getreal "\nEnter Rectangular Duct Width a (mm): "))
  (setq b (getreal "\nEnter Rectangular Duct Height b (mm): "))
  (setq q (getreal "\nEnter Airflow Rate Q (m3/h): "))
  ;; Huebscher formula: De = 1.30 * ((a*b)^0.625) / ((a+b)^0.25)
  (setq de (* 1.30 (/ (expt (* a b) 0.625) (expt (+ a b) 0.25))))
  ;; Velocity v (m/s) = (Q / 3600) / ((a/1000) * (b/1000))
  (setq v (/ (/ q 3600.0) (* (/ a 1000.0) (/ b 1000.0))))
  (setq txt (strcat (rtos a 2 0) "x" (rtos b 2 0) " (De=" (rtos de 2 0) "mm, v=" (rtos v 2 2) "m/s)"))
  (princ (strcat "\n[ASHRAE Calc] " txt))
  (if (setq pt (getpoint "\nPick point to place duct annotation label: "))
    (entmakex (list '(0 . "MTEXT") '(8 . "M-HVAC-TEXT") '(100 . "AcDbEntity") '(100 . "AcDbMText") (cons 10 pt) '(40 . 3.0) (cons 1 txt)))
  )
  (princ)
)
