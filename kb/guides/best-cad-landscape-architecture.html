CAD for Landscape Architecture Beginners

Starting landscape architecture? Learn which CAD tools beginners should study: SketchUp for visualization, Civil 3D for grading, and Vectorworks for design-build.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-06-30 Editorial process

Who This Guide Is For

Landscape architecture combines artistic site design with technical grading, planting plans, and drainage engineering. Different software handles different aspects — here's how to navigate as a beginner.

Software Covered

SketchUp Civil 3D Vectorworks

Key Things to Consider

  • Are you focused on design (planting, hardscape) or engineering (grading, drainage)?
  • Is a free tool sufficient for learning?
  • What does your program teach?
  • Do you need 3D visualization for client presentations?

Your Options Explained

SketchUp

Best for 3D site visualization and concept presentations. Easy to learn. Free web version. Add-ons for landscape design. Great for communicating design intent to clients.

Civil 3D

For technical site work: grading, drainage, earthwork calculations. Free for students. Essential if you work on site engineering aspects. Built on AutoCAD.

Vectorworks Landmark

All-in-one landscape architecture tool. BIM-capable. Plant databases, irrigation design, hardscape detailing. Free for students. Popular in landscape-specific firms.

Our Recommendation

Start with SketchUp for 3D thinking (week 1-2). Add AutoCAD for 2D plan production. Then specialize: Vectorworks Landmark for design-focused firms, or Civil 3D for engineering-focused site work.
