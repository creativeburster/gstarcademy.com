Course 23: 3D Surface Modeling — Loft, Sweep, Continuity (G0/G1/G2) & NURBS Patch Workflows

Design aerodynamic transitions, HVAC square-to-round duct adapters, and architectural freeform canopies in GstarCAD 2027 using LOFT, SWEEP, SURFNETWORK, and G1/G2 continuity.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by Chen Haoran, M.Sc., GDTP-S (Lead Mechanical CAD & ASME GD&T Specialist) Last reviewed 2026-11-03 Editorial Process & Testbed

1. Engineering Architecture & Core Methodology

While prismatic mechanical brackets are easily modeled with `EXTRUDE` and `REVOLVE`, industrial sheet-metal transitions (such as a rectangular-to-circular HVAC plenum hood), ergonomic consumer enclosures, and freeform stadium roofs require **3D Procedural & NURBS Surface Modeling**. GstarCAD 2027's ACIS kernel supports both **Procedural Associative Surfaces (`SURFACEMODELINGMODE = 0`)** and **Control-Vertex NURBS Surfaces (`SURFACEMODELINGMODE = 1`)** with `G0` (Position), `G1` (Tangent), and `G2` (Curvature) boundary continuity.

2. Step-by-Step Production Workflow & Command Modules

Module 1: Multi-Section LOFT with Guide Curves & Normal Controls

Run LOFT and select cross-section profiles in sequence (e.g., a `600x400 mm` rectangle at `Z=0` and a `Ø350 mm` circle at `Z=500`). Choose **Guides** to constrain sidewall profiles along 3D splines, or open **Settings** to enforce `Normal to Start and End Cross Sections` so flange collars remain vertical for welding.

Module 2: 3D Helical & Twisted SWEEP Pipelines

Run SWEEP to extrude a closed 2D profile along any 2D/3D path (`HELIX`, `3DPOLY`, or `SPLINE`). Use the **Alignment**, **Base Point**, **Scale** (tapered sweep from `1.0` to `0.6`), and **Twist** (`270°` helical twist) options for screw conveyors, springs, and twisted structural columns.

Module 3: Surface Trimming, Filleting, Patching & Solidification (SURFSCULPT)

Use SURFNETWORK, SURFPATCH (with `G1`/`G2` continuity), SURFTRIM, and SURFFILLET to assemble intersecting surface sheets. Once the surface boundary forms a closed watertight volume, run SURFSCULPT (or `THICKEN`) to convert the surface quilt into a true volumetric `3DSOLID`.

3. Command, Variable & Standard Reference Matrix

Command / Parameter Scope / Target Engineering Behavior & Best Practice
LOFT Cross-Sections + Guides Interpolates smooth 3D solids or surfaces across dissimilar planar/non-planar profiles.
SWEEP Profile + 3D Path Sweeps cross-sections along 3D curves with optional Twist angle and Scale taper.
SURFPATCH / SURFNETWORK G0 / G1 / G2 Continuity Caps open surface loops with Positional (G0), Tangent (G1), or Curvature (G2) smoothness.
SURFSCULPT / THICKEN Surface-to-Solid B-Rep Converts watertight surface quilts or single sheets into volumetric ACIS 3DSOLIDs.

5. Production QA Verification & Competency Checklist

  • Keep cross-section vertex counts equal (or align start points) before running `LOFT` to prevent unwanted helical surface twisting.
  • Set `DELOBJ = 0` when experimenting with `LOFT` and `SWEEP` so defining 2D generator curves are retained for parametric edits.
  • Verify watertight closure via `SURFSCULPT` and `MASSPROP` before exporting `.STEP` files for CNC machining.
