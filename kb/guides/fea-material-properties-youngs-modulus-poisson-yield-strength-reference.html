FEA Material Properties Reference: Young's Modulus, Poisson's Ratio & Yield Strength

Verified structural and mechanical FEA material property tables (E, nu, rho, Sy, Su, alpha) for steels, aluminum alloys, titanium, plastics, and concrete with unit consistency rules.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by GstarCAD Academy Editorial Team Last reviewed 2026-11-03 Editorial Process

1. Engineering Architecture & Core Methodology

Every Finite Element Analysis (FEA) solver—whether Ansys, Abaqus, Nastran, or CalculiX—is unitless at the matrix level. Feeding a 3D solid exported from GstarCAD in Millimeters (mm) requires a strictly consistent material property system (such as mm-tonne-s-N-MPa) alongside verified isotropic elastic constants ($E, \nu, \rho, S_y$).

2. Step-by-Step Production Workflow & Command Modules

1. The FEA Unit Consistency Matrix (SI Meter vs. CAD Millimeter)

Because $1\text{ MPa} = 1\text{ N/mm}^2$, engineers meshing CAD models in millimeters must enter Young's Modulus $E$ and Yield Strength $S_y$ in MPa ($ ext{N/mm}^2$), Force in Newtons ($ ext{N}$), and Mass Density $\rho$ in $\text{tonne/mm}^3$ ($10^{-9}\text{ kg/mm}^3$) so $F = m \cdot a$ holds for gravity and modal frequency calculations!

2. Isotropic Elastic Constants Relation ($E, G, K, \nu$)

Shear Modulus $G$ and Bulk Modulus $K$ are linked to Young's Modulus $E$ and Poisson's Ratio $\nu$ by:

$$G = \frac{E}{2(1 + \nu)}, \qquad K = \frac{E}{3(1 - 2\nu)}$$

Note that all structural steels share nearly identical stiffness ($E \approx 200\text{–}210\text{ GPa}$); heat treatment increases Yield Strength $S_y$ without increasing stiffness $E$!

3. Preparing Watertight 3D ACIS Solids in GstarCAD for FEA Meshing

Before running STEPOUT or IGESOUT, suppress cosmetic fillets ($R < 1\text{ mm}$), threads, and embossed text that create sliver tetrahedral elements, and run INTERFERE to verify zero overlapping volumes.

4. Automation Script & Reference Implementation (AutoLISP Isotropic Shear & Bulk Modulus (G, K) Calculator from (E, nu))

(defun c:FEA-MODULUS-CALC ( / e nu g k)
  (setq e  (getreal "\nEnter Young's Modulus E (GPa, e.g. 200): ")
        nu (getreal "\nEnter Poisson's Ratio nu (e.g. 0.30): "))
  (setq g (/ e (* 2.0 (+ 1.0 nu)))
        k (/ e (* 3.0 (- 1.0 (* 2.0 nu)))))
  (princ (strcat "\n[FEA Isotropic] Shear Modulus G = " (rtos g 2 2)
                 " GPa | Bulk Modulus K = " (rtos k 2 2) " GPa"))
  (princ)
)

5. Production QA Verification & Competency Checklist

  • When meshing in millimeters (mm), always enter Density in tonnes/mm3 (e.g. 7.85e-9 for steel) if applying gravity or running modal analysis.
  • Remember that upgrading from mild steel (S235) to high-strength steel (4140) reduces von Mises stress safety factor issues, but will NOT reduce elastic deflection.
  • Defeature small chamfers, tapped threads, and engraved logos in GstarCAD before exporting STEP files for FEA.

1. DWG Header & Registry Persistence Scope

Variables and object properties fall into three persistence scopes: Drawing-Saved (DXF Header) variables that travel with the .dwg file to subcontractors, Registry-Saved (Profile .arg) settings that persist per Windows workstation user, and Session-Volatile states that reset on every launch unless locked inside acaddoc.lsp. Always verify persistence scope via SYSVDLG before issuing sheets to an ISO 19650 Common Data Environment (CDE).

2. Multi-Core Regeneration & Memory Hygiene

When processing large civil corridors, dense plant P&IDs, or 50-sheet architectural Xref trees, unpurged Registered Application IDs (REGAPP), orphaned DGN stroke dictionaries, and redundant annotation scales degrade spatial index (INDEXCTL = 3) lookup speed. Executing a pre-submittal AUDIT, command-line -PURGE → Regapps, and OVERKILL pass keeps file open times under 2 seconds on GstarCAD's multi-threaded graphics kernel.

3. Hybrid AutoCAD + GstarCAD Team Coexistence
