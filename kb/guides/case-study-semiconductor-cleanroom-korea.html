1. Industrial Challenge & Recurring License Rental Bottleneck

Designing semiconductor wafer fabrication plants (Fabs) involves an extraordinary level of spatial and technical density. Cleanroom facilities must accommodate automated material handling systems (OHT), ultra-high-purity (UHP) gas lines, toxic chemical delivery systems (CDS), scrubbed exhaust ductwork, and deep sub-fab vibration-isolated pedestals. Engineering drawings routinely exceed 1GB in size, stacking over 120 disciplinary external references into single coordination models.

The firm previously used AutoCAD and AutoCAD MEP. However, recurring annual subscription costs approached $715,000, and workstation performance deteriorated severely on dense multi-layer layouts. During critical fast-track fab construction phases, site engineers wearing cleanroom bunny suits faced constant cloud-license authentication drops on factory floor terminals. Corporate leadership mandated an immediate shift to high-performance, perpetual floating network CAD licensing.

2. 30-Day Technical Verification & Standard Compliance Protocol

The institute's facility engineering division subjected GstarCAD 2027 to a high-stress 30-day trial on a state-of-the-art 3nm Mega-Fab project in Pyeongtaek:

  • 1.2GB Multi-Discipline Composite DWG Handling: GstarCAD’s multi-core memory architecture opened composite cleanroom ceiling MEP coordination drawings in 14.6 seconds (compared to 49.2 seconds in AutoCAD), maintaining a steady 60 FPS frame rate during rapid pan and zoom operations.
  • SEMI S2/S8 Safety Clearance & Clashing Validation: Cleanroom piping draftsmen verified that complex dynamic blocks representing double-containment UHP gas piping and fire sprinkler clearances maintained accurate geometric collision envelopes without proxy entity distortion.
  • DWG FastView Cleanroom Tablet Integration: Field inspection teams utilized DWG FastView on intrinsically safe tablets inside operating Class 1 cleanrooms, reviewing 3D STEP and 2D DWG hook-up layouts offline without introducing paper contamination or risking cloud disconnects.
  • Proprietary Korean Font (Hangul Gulim/Batang) Precision: Automated testing across 8,500 legacy Korean-language fabrication detail sheets verified 100% text fidelity with zero character overlapping or missing glyph errors.
