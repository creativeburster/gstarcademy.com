Best CAD Software for Furniture Design Beginners

Want to design furniture? Compare beginner-friendly CAD tools for woodworkers and furniture makers: SketchUp, Fusion 360, FreeCAD — from simple sketches to CNC-ready files.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-06-30 Editorial process

Who This Guide Is For

Whether you're a hobbyist woodworker wanting to plan projects or a furniture design student, CAD software helps you visualize, refine, and produce accurate cut lists and joinery details.

Software Covered

SketchUp Fusion 360 FreeCAD

Key Things to Consider

  • Hand tools or CNC/machine fabrication?
  • Simple projects (shelves, tables) or complex joinery?
  • Need renderings for client approval?
  • Budget: free tools only or willing to invest?
  • Do you need cut lists and material optimization?

Your Options Explained

SketchUp

Most popular among woodworkers. Easy to learn. Free web version. Huge furniture design community. Plugins for cut lists, joinery, and rendering. Start here.

Fusion 360

Best for CNC-furniture and complex parametric designs. Free for personal use. T-spline surfaces for organic furniture forms. Direct CAM output for CNC routing.

FreeCAD

Free, open-source. Good for precise joinery modeling. Steeper curve than SketchUp but fully capable for furniture. No cost, ever.

Our Recommendation

Hand-tool woodworkers: SketchUp Free for planning. CNC furniture makers: Fusion 360 for design-to-manufacture. Budget-conscious: SketchUp Free → FreeCAD as you advance.
