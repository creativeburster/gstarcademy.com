Course 08: Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T), ISO 21920 Surface Texture & Welding Symbols

Author compliant ASME Y14.5-2018 and ISO 1101 Feature Control Frames (MMC/LMC/Datum Reference Frames), power dimensions (AMPOWERDIM), ISO 286 shaft/hole fits, and AWS/ISO welding symbols.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by Dr. Marcus Vance, Ph.D. (Lead Computational Mechanics & FEA/CFD Reviewer) Key Commands: AMPOWERDIM, AMGDT, AMSURFSYM, AMWELDSYM, AMFITSLIST Last reviewed 2026-10-19 Editorial Process & Testbed

Course Technical Scope & Production Competencies

Ambiguous tolerances on a 2D manufacturing print cause costly machine-shop scrap and quality inspection disputes. Course 08 trains mechanical designers to author legally binding geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T), shaft/hole fit limits, surface roughness symbols, and multi-process welding callouts inside GstarCAD Mechanical 2027.

  • Execute AMPOWERDIM to place intelligent dimensions integrated with ISO 286-2 shaft/hole fit calculators (e.g., Ø50 H7/g6 automatically displaying +0.025/0 and -0.009/-0.025 micron limits).
  • Construct ASME Y14.5-2018 and ISO 1101 Feature Control Frames (AMFCFRAME) enforcing flatness, cylindricity, true position at Maximum Material Condition (MMC Ⓜ), and 3-2-1 Datum Reference Frames (A|B|C).
  • Annotate machined surfaces with ISO 1302 /ISO 21920 surface texture symbols ($Ra\,1.6$, $Rz\,6.3$) and AWS A2.4 / ISO 2553 compound fillet/bevel weld symbols (AMWELDSYM).

Module 1: Power Dimensioning, ISO 286 Limits/Fits, ASME Y14.5 GD&T Frames & AWS/ISO Weld Symbols

Under ISO 286 and ASME B4.1, manually looking up upper and lower micron deviations for a transition fit like Ø80 H7/k6 in a paper handbook is slow and error-prone. GstarCAD Mechanical's AMPOWERDIM embeds the complete international tolerance tables in memory: selecting the fit class automatically calculates the exact micrometer upper/lower limits based on the nominal dimension diameter and updates them dynamically if the geometry is stretched.

Similarly, the AMFCFRAME dialog validates syntax rules in real time, preventing invalid combinations such as applying a datum reference to a pure form tolerance (flatness, straightness, circularity, or cylindricity).

Module 2: Step-by-Step Hands-On Command Execution Lab

Follow this structured 4-step laboratory procedure directly at the GstarCAD command line or dynamic input prompt:

  1. Step 1 — Environment & System Variable Initialization: At the command prompt, verify the active state of core commands and variables: AMPOWERDIM, AMGDT, AMSURFSYM, AMWELDSYM, AMFITSLIST. Ensure that object snap bitcodes (OSMODE=175), associative dimensioning (DIMASSOC=2), and multi-core display regeneration (WHIPTHREAD=3) are active.
  2. Step 2 — Primary Geometry & Metadata Authoring: Execute the primary workflow on standardized ISO 13567 or discipline-specific layers. Never place production geometry or block definitions on Defpoints, and keep internal block geometry on Layer 0 so instances inherit host layer properties dynamically.
  3. Step 3 — Associative Verification & Revision Auditing: Test parametric associativity by stretching or modifying parent geometry and confirming that linked dimensions, attributes, schedules, or section views update automatically without disassociation warnings (ANNOMONITOR=2).
  4. Step 4 — Pre-Transmittal Quality Assurance (QA/QC): Prior to saving or publishing the deliverable, run OVERKILL to eliminate duplicate vectors, -PURGE (RegApps and All) to strip orphaned symbol table bloat, and AUDIT to verify 100% AcDbObject handle integrity.
