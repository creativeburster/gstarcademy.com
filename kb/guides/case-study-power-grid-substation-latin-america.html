1. Industrial Challenge & Recurring License Rental Bottleneck

Constructing high-voltage AC and DC transmission substations across the Andes and Brazilian coastal corridors, the engineering firm employs over 750 electrical designers, protection and control (P&C) specialists, and civil foundation engineers. Typical project packages involve thousands of single-line diagrams (SLD), three-line AC/DC schematics, relay panel wiring tables, and substation physical layout drawings.

The firm previously used AutoCAD and AutoCAD Electrical. However, mandatory transition to individual Named User licenses severely crippled their collaborative multidisciplinary delivery model. Substation drafting requires flexible team scaling between project milestones, and sharing floating licenses across regional offices in Santiago, Lima, and São Paulo was vital. Recurring Autodesk software rental costs exceeded $750,000 per year, prompting executive leadership to mandate an immediate transition to perpetual floating network licenses.

2. 30-Day Technical Verification & Standard Compliance Protocol

The technical steering committee executed a thorough 30-day trial on the 500kV Atacama Solar Interconnection project:

  • IEC 60617 & IEEE Graphical Symbol Consistency: Electrical engineers verified that thousands of proprietary dynamic electrical blocks—including transformer winding symbols, circuit breakers, and protection relay contacts—retained all connection attributes and visibility states without corruption.
  • Automated Terminal Strip & Cable Schedule LISP Porting: The firm’s 84 in-house AutoLISP routines that automate cross-reference terminal block numbering, wire tagging, and cable pulling schedules operated in GstarCAD 2027 with 100% execution fidelity.
  • Lightning Protection & Grounding Grid DWG Calculation: Heavy grounding grid layout drawings containing dense conductor meshes and IEEE 80 soil resistivity calculation overlays loaded and zoomed instantly without viewport lag.
  • Secondary Relay Panel C# .NET Plugin Migration: A custom C# .NET terminal jumper checking tool was recompiled for GstarCAD in less than half a day by updating assembly references to GsMgd.dll and GsDbMgd.dll.

Enterprise deployment was rolled out via Active Directory GPO in three days. GstarLM floating license servers configured in Santiago and São Paulo allowed engineers across Chile, Peru, and Brazil to draw from a shared license pool according to project workload spikes.

Protection and control drafting teams delivered substation documentation packages two weeks ahead of schedule. The $2.28M+ preserved capital enabled the company to build a modern IEC 61850 substation digital simulator test facility.
