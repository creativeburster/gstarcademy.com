Custom Multi-Ply Thermal Wall Styles, Parametric Openings & Automated Area/Door Schedules in GstarCAD Architecture

Author multi-layer insulated cavity wall styles, configure priority T-junction cleanup rules, and generate live room area and door/window quantity takeoff tables in GstarCAD Architecture 2027.

1. Priority-Based Multi-Ply Wall Cleanup Mechanics

In architectural construction documentation, an exterior envelope wall is rarely a single homogeneous slab; it comprises a structural reinforced-concrete or CMU masonry core, a vapor barrier, rigid mineral-wool thermal insulation, an air cavity, and exterior brick veneer or rainscreen cladding. GstarCAD Architecture 2027 models these assemblies as intelligent, multi-component WALL entities.

Each material layer inside a Wall Style is assigned a numerical Cleanup Priority Index. When an interior gypsum partition intersects an exterior thermal wall in a T-junction, GstarCAD's topological solver compares priority indices: high-priority structural cores fuse continuously, while lower-priority plaster and insulation plies automatically miter and wrap around the intersection without manual `TRIM` or `FILLET` operations.

2. Recommended Wall Component Priority Hierarchy

Wall Component Ply Priority Index Hatch Pattern & Intersection Cleanup Rule
Reinforced Concrete Core (200mm) Priority 1 (Highest) AR-CONC + Diagonal Slash; cuts through all insulation and finish layers.
CMU / Brick Masonry Infill (100mm) Priority 2 ANSI31 Crosshatch; stops cleanly at structural concrete columns and shear walls.
Rigid Thermal Insulation & Plaster Priority 3 – 4 Wraps around window/door jamb reveals and stops at masonry/concrete faces.

3. Automated Door/Window Quantity Takeoffs & Net Floor Area Auditing

Once walls, doors (`DOOR`), and windows (`WINDOW`) are placed across floor plans, GstarCAD Architecture automates quantity extraction:

  • Live Door & Window Schedules (`DOORSCHEDULE` / `WINDOWSCHEDULE`): Scans all story drawings, groups openings by Mark ID (`D-01`, `W-04`), calculates rough masonry opening widths/heights, and draws scaled 2D elevation graphics inside the schedule table.
  • Room Net/Gross Area Tables (`AREATABLE`): Automatically detects closed room perimeters, deducts structural column footprints, places formatted room tags, and exports BOMA/ISO area schedules directly to Excel.

2D Conversion for Non-AEC Subcontractors (`EXPORTTOAUTOCAD`): When transmitting architectural floor plans to landscaping, signage, or audiovisual subcontractors using standard 2D CAD seats, run the built-in **Explode AEC Objects to 2D** utility on a detached copy. This flattens parametric walls, doors, and stairs into clean, standard `AcDbPolyline` and `AcDbBlockReference` entities at the active cut-plane scale (`1:100` or `1:50`).
