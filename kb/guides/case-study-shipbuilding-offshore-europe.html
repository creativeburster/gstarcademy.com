1. Industrial Challenge & Recurring License Rental Bottleneck

Operating ship design studios across Norway and the Netherlands, the maritime group produces detailed structural steel nestings, hull lofting drawings, piping schematics, and electrical cable runs for offshore fabrication yards. Structural hull drawings involve dense longitudinal stiffeners, transverse frames, bracket details, and complex weld callouts subject to classification society certification by DNV and Lloyd's Register.

Autodesk's transition to Named User subscription licensing severely damaged operational flexibility. Naval projects fluctuate between intensive multi-month hull design phases and lighter maintenance support periods. Paying fixed annual subscription fees for occasional shipyard liaison engineers inflated annual CAD costs past €950,000. Corporate management mandated a return to perpetual floating network licenses that could be allocated dynamically across engineering offices and fabrication slipways.

2. 30-Day Technical Verification & Standard Compliance Protocol

The Marine Systems Evaluation Team conducted a rigorous 30-day technical trial on a 15MW Floating Offshore Wind Substructure project:

  • DNV Structural Weld Symbol & Plate Nesting Fidelity: The team validated that complex weld symbols (penetration bevels, fillet throat sizes, backing bars according to AWS D1.1 and DNV-OS-C101) rendered identically in GstarCAD with zero font or scaling anomalies.
  • Tribon & AVEVA Marine 2D Drafting Interoperability: 2D structural and pipe spools exported from specialized 3D shipbuilding software (Tribon, AVEVA Marine, NAPA) opened and plotted in GstarCAD with 100% line weight and layer fidelity.
  • Automated Hull Weight & Center of Gravity (CoG) LISP Tools: The group’s proprietary AutoLISP routines for calculating steel plate cross-sectional areas, plate thickness attributes, and longitudinal centers of gravity executed seamlessly via native vla- and entget functions.
  • Gigabit Network Concurrent File Locking: Testing across shared multi-gigabyte project directories on central NAS storage verified that GstarCAD handled simultaneous DWG read/write locks flawlessly, preventing drawing corruption when 40+ draftsmen referenced the same hull master Xref.
