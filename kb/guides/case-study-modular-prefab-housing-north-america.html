1. Industrial Challenge & Recurring License Rental Bottleneck

Operating three high-throughput offsite fabrication facilities across Washington State and British Columbia, the company manufactures volumetric modular building units complete with factory-installed electrical, plumbing, and structural light-gauge steel (LGS) wall framing. From architectural concepts to factory floor execution, hundreds of production drawings must be issued weekly to automated roll-forming machines and assembly line work cells.

Autodesk's Named User licensing model severely strained factory floor operations. Factory terminals utilized by multiple assembly shift workers required costly individual subscriptions, while office designers experienced constant license churn. Software costs escalated past $630,000 annually. Corporate leadership initiated a search for a robust, developer-friendly 2D/3D CAD engine that offered cost-effective perpetual licensing and deep programmatic integration with production ERP systems.

2. 30-Day Technical Verification & Standard Compliance Protocol

During an intensive 30-day technical validation across active modular hospital ward projects, GstarCAD proved its manufacturing automation capabilities:

  • High-Speed Programmatic BOM Extraction via .NET API: Engineering developers developed a custom C# plugin utilizing GstarCAD’s native .NET SDK (Gssoft.GstarCAD.DatabaseServices). The tool scanned 100,000+ structural framing blocks, fastener attributes, and drywall sheets across complete module drawings in 0.38 seconds, exporting validated JSON data to the ERP database.
  • IBC 2024 & AISC 360 Structural Framing Tolerances: Precision dimensioning, weld symbols, and shear wall anchor bolt schedules rendered with zero tolerance deviation, ensuring full compliance with state and provincial modular building inspection codes.
  • Dynamic Block Compatibility: The manufacturer’s extensive library of 850+ dynamic blocks—featuring lookup tables, visibility states, and parametric stretch grips for wall panel configurations—functioned flawlessly without conversion or degradation.
  • Shop-Floor Touchscreen Performance: GstarCAD’s lightweight footprint enabled instant drawing navigation on dust-resistant industrial touchscreen terminals on the factory floor, allowing assemblers to inspect 3D framing sub-assemblies in real time.
