High-Speed Curve Stationing, Offset & 3D Intersection Solvers with vlax-curve-* in AutoLISP (dev-cookbook-27)

Use native C++-backed vlax-curve-* and vla-IntersectWith functions in GstarCAD 2027 AutoLISP to compute arc-length stations, first/second derivatives, and curve intersections.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by Zhang Mingyuan, B.Eng. (Distinguished DWG Graphics Kernel & GRX SDK Architect) Last reviewed 2026-10-26 Editorial Process & Testbed

1. Why `vlax-curve-*` Functions Are 40× Faster Than DXF List Math

In civil alignment stationing, bridge deck setting-out, and automated cable-tray routing, AutoLISP developers frequently need to compute points at exact arc-length intervals along complex `LWPOLYLINE` (containing arc bulge segments), `SPLINE` (NURBS), `ELLIPSE`, or `HELIX` entities, as well as tangent/normal vectors (`firstDeriv`) and closest perpendicular projections. Manually parsing DXF group `10` vertices and group `42` bulge tangents ($ an( heta/4)$) in pure LISP is both mathematically fragile and slow.

In GstarCAD 2027, the **`vlax-curve-*`** family calls the underlying C++ `AcDbCurve` virtual geometry evaluator directly in compiled machine code—accepting either a classic `ENAME` (`(car (entsel))`) or a `VLA-OBJECT` without COM marshaling overhead.

2. Production AutoLISP: Automated Station Ticks & Perpendicular Offset Labels

The following Visual LISP routine places perpendicular station tick marks and rotated chainage annotations (`0+000.00m`, `0+020.00m`, ...) at user-specified intervals along *any* curve (`LWPOLYLINE`, `SPLINE`, `ARC`, or `3DPOLY`) using exact differential tangent vectors (`vlax-curve-getFirstDeriv`):

;; StationAlignment.lsp - Place orthogonal chainage ticks & labels along any AcDbCurve
(vl-load-com)

(defun C:GCSTATION ( / ent step totalLen curDist pt param deriv normAngle pTick1 pTick2 msp doc)
  (setq doc (vla-get-ActiveDocument (vlax-get-acad-object))
        msp (vla-get-ModelSpace doc))
  (if (and (setq ent (car (entsel "
Select alignment curve (Polyline/Spline/Arc): ")))
           (not (vl-catch-all-error-p (vl-catch-all-apply 'vlax-curve-getEndParam (list ent))))
           (setq step (getdist "
Specify station interval distance <20.0>: ")))
    (progn
      (if (null step) (setq step 20.0))
      (setq totalLen (vlax-curve-getDistAtParam ent (vlax-curve-getEndParam ent))
            curDist  0.0)
      (vla-StartUndoMark doc)
      (while (<= curDist totalLen)
        ;; 1. Evaluate exact 3D coordinate and curve parameter at arc length curDist
        (setq pt    (vlax-curve-getPointAtDist ent curDist)
              param (vlax-curve-getParamAtDist ent curDist)
              deriv (vlax-curve-getFirstDeriv ent param))
        ;; 2. Compute tangent angle and orthogonal normal (+pi/2)
        (setq normAngle (+ (atan (cadr deriv) (car deriv)) (* 0.5 pi))
              pTick1    (polar pt normAngle 1.5)
              pTick2    (polar pt (+ normAngle pi) 1.5))
        ;; 3. Draw perpendicular tick line & station text
        (entmakex (list '(0 . "LINE") '(8 . "C-ALGN-TICK") (cons 10 pTick1) (cons 11 pTick2)))
        (entmakex (list '(0 . "TEXT")
                        '(8 . "C-ALGN-TEXT")
                        (cons 10 (polar pt normAngle 2.5))
                        '(40 . 2.0)
                        (cons 50 (- normAngle (* 0.5 pi)))
                        (cons 1 (strcat "STA " (rtos curDist 2 2) "m"))))
        (setq curDist (+ curDist step))
      )
      (vla-EndUndoMark doc)
      (princ (strcat "
Generated stationing along " (rtos totalLen 2 2) "m alignment."))
    )
  )
  (princ)
)

4. Handling Non-Planar 3D Splines & Variable-Width Bulge Polylines

When evaluating `vlax-curve-getPointAtProjection` on 3D utility pipelines (`3DPOLY` or non-planar `SPLINE`) that slope across varying elevations (`Z != 0`), pass the WCS projection normal `'(0.0 0.0 1.0)` so horizontal stationing distances match civil plan-view chainages rather than inclined 3D slope lengths. Conversely, when calculating true cable or pipe cut lengths for material procurement schedules, use `(vlax-curve-getDistAtParam ent (vlax-curve-getEndParam ent))`, which integrates the true 3D arc length along all three axes.
