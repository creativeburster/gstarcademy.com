3. AutoLISP Sequential Terminal & Wire Number Generator

;; GstarCAD 2027 — Rapid Sequential Terminal Block & Wire Tag Numbering (e.g., X1:01, X1:02...)
(defun c:GCAD_WIRE_NUM ( / prefix num pt str)
  (setq prefix (getstring "\nEnter Terminal/Wire Prefix (e.g., X1: or W24-): "))
  (if (= prefix "") (setq prefix "X1:"))
  (setq num (getint "\nEnter Starting Number <1>: "))
  (if (not num) (setq num 1))
  (while (setq pt (getpoint (strcat "\nPick insertion point for " prefix (itoa num) " (Enter to exit): ")))
    (setq str (strcat prefix (if (< num 10) (strcat "0" (itoa num)) (itoa num))))
    (entmakex (list '(0 . "TEXT") '(8 . "E-WIRE-TAGS") (cons 10 pt) '(40 . 2.5) (cons 1 str) '(62 . 2)))
    (setq num (1+ num))
  )
  (princ)
)

Electrical QA Check IEC 60617 / IEC 81346 Rule GstarCAD 2027 Automated Verification
Duplicate Device Tag (`TAG`) Every primary contactor/relay coil (`-K1`, `-Q4`) must have a unique `=Func+Loc-Tag` identifier. `DATAEXTRACTION` grouped by `TAG` + `FUNC` + `LOC` flags any count > 1 on primary coil definitions.
Contact Capacity Overflow Auxiliary contacts placed across sheets cannot exceed the physical NO/NC poles of the selected part number. Cross-reference script compares child contact count against catalog attribute `MAX_NO_NC` (e.g., `3NO+1NC`).
Terminal Strip Continuity Every internal panel wire and external field cable core must terminate at a numbered `-X` terminal block. Exports netlist from schematic block connections directly to Weidmüller M-Print or Phoenix Contact Project Complete.
