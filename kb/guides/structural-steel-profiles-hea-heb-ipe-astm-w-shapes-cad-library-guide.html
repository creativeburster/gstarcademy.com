Structural Steel Profiles (EN HEA/HEB/IPE vs. ASTM AISC W-Shapes) CAD Guide

Structural steel cross-section geometry guide covering European EN 10365 (IPE, HEA, HEB, UNP) and American AISC (W, C, HSS, L) profiles with exact flange/web/fillet drafting dimensions.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by Elena Rostova, M.Eng., PE (Senior Civil Infrastructure & Geomatics Engineer) Last reviewed 2026-11-03 Editorial Process & Testbed

1. Engineering Architecture & Core Methodology

Structural steel detailing in GstarCAD requires accurate cross-sectional geometry—especially the root fillet radius ($r$) and flange thickness ($t_f$)—to verify bolt gauge clearances, stiffener plates, and beam-to-column welded connections across both European EN 10365 (IPE / HEA / HEB) and American AISC (W-Shapes).

2. Step-by-Step Production Workflow & Command Modules

1. European Wide-Flange & I-Beam Series (IPE vs. HEA vs. HEB)

European structural steel uses parallel-flange metric profiles:

  • IPE (e.g., IPE 300: depth $h=300\text{ mm}$, width $b=150\text{ mm}$): Narrower flanges optimized for bending as secondary floor beams and purlins.
  • HEA (light) & HEB (standard, e.g., HEB 300: $h=300\text{ mm}, b=300\text{ mm}$): Wide square H-sections with high weak-axis moment of inertia $I_z$ for columns and heavy girders.

2. American AISC W-Shapes (Nominal Depth x Weight in lb/ft)

An AISC designation like W14x90 indicates a Wide-Flange section with a nominal depth of $14\text{ in}$ ($d = 14.02\text{ in} / 356\text{ mm}$) and a linear mass of $90\text{ lb/ft}$ ($134\text{ kg/m}$). Always look up the exact decimal depth $d$ and k-distance (outer flange face to web toe of fillet) before detailing clip angles.

4. Automation Script & Reference Implementation (AutoLISP Parametric Structural I/H-Beam Section Generator (IPE/HEB/W))

(defun c:DRAW-IBEAM ( / pt h b tw tf x0 y0)
  (setq pt (getpoint "\nPick Beam Centroid Insertion Point: ")
        h  (getreal "\nEnter Total Depth h (mm, e.g. 300): ")
        b  (getreal "\nEnter Flange Width b (mm, e.g. 150): ")
        tw (getreal "\nEnter Web Thickness tw (mm, e.g. 7.1): ")
        tf (getreal "\nEnter Flange Thickness tf (mm, e.g. 10.7): "))
  (setq x0 (car pt) y0 (cadr pt))
  (command "_.PLINE"
    (list (- x0 (* 0.5 b)) (- y0 (* 0.5 h)))
    (list (+ x0 (* 0.5 b)) (- y0 (* 0.5 h)))
    (list (+ x0 (* 0.5 b)) (+ (- y0 (* 0.5 h)) tf))
    (list (+ x0 (* 0.5 tw)) (+ (- y0 (* 0.5 h)) tf))
    (list (+ x0 (* 0.5 tw)) (- (+ y0 (* 0.5 h)) tf))
    (list (+ x0 (* 0.5 b)) (- (+ y0 (* 0.5 h)) tf))
    (list (+ x0 (* 0.5 b)) (+ y0 (* 0.5 h)))
    (list (- x0 (* 0.5 b)) (+ y0 (* 0.5 h)))
    (list (- x0 (* 0.5 b)) (- (+ y0 (* 0.5 h)) tf))
    (list (- x0 (* 0.5 tw)) (- (+ y0 (* 0.5 h)) tf))
    (list (- x0 (* 0.5 tw)) (+ (- y0 (* 0.5 h)) tf))
    (list (- x0 (* 0.5 b)) (+ (- y0 (* 0.5 h)) tf)) "_C")
  (princ "\n[Structural Steel] Parametric I/H section generated.")
  (princ)
)

5. Production QA Verification & Competency Checklist

  • Always include the root fillet radius r (or AISC k-dimension) when checking whether stiffener plates or bolt washers clear the web-flange transition.
  • Remember that HEA 300 has an actual depth h = 290 mm, whereas HEB 300 has h = 300 mm.
  • Place structural steel profiles on dedicated S-COLS or S-BEAM layers with centroid reference points.
