GstarCAD Mechanical vs AutoCAD Mechanical: ACM Compatibility, BOM & Standard Parts

In-depth engineering comparison of GstarCAD Mechanical and AutoCAD Mechanical covering legacy ACM drawing compatibility, ISO/DIN/ANSI part libraries, associative balloons, and ERP BOM integration.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-10-12 Editorial process

1. Full Legacy AutoCAD Mechanical (ACM) Drawing Compatibility

Manufacturing enterprises hold millions of legacy DWG files created in AutoCAD Mechanical (ACM 2015–2026). Opening these files in vanilla 2D CAD turns intelligent part references, balloons, and BOM tables into uneditable proxy graphics. GstarCAD Mechanical natively reads and writes ACM entity data structures, allowing engineers to edit existing balloons, update part quantities, and modify hole charts seamlessly.

2. Intelligent Layer Management & Automatic Hidden Line Calculation

GstarCAD Mechanical automates mechanical layer governance: placing a dimension, centerline, hatch, or standard screw automatically routes the entity to its designated ISO/DIN mechanical layer with pre-configured color and lineweight. Furthermore, 2D Hide Situation (GMHIDE) automatically converts obscured assembly contours to dashed hidden lines and updates dynamically when foreground components move.

3. Associative Balloons, Parts Lists, and Multi-Border Sheets

Unlike manual text leaders, GstarCAD Mechanical balloons attach directly to component metadata. Adding a new subassembly, merging duplicate fasteners, or re-sorting item numbers automatically updates both the graphical balloons and the on-sheet Parts List—even across drawings containing multiple scaled title borders within a single model space.

4. Engineering Calculators, Shaft Generators, and Cam Design

Built-in mechanical generators construct stepped transmission shafts, involute gears, belt/chain drives, and tension/compression springs from engineering parameters, outputting both 2D manufacturing views and tabular tolerance charts in seconds.
