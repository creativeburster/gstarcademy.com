ISO 128 & ASME Y14.2 Lineweights, Linetypes & Hatch Standards Handbook

Definitive engineering reference for technical drawing line thicknesses, √2 metric pen hierarchies, hidden/center line conventions, and section hatching rules.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by GstarCAD Academy Editorial Team Last reviewed 2026-11-03 Editorial Process

1. Engineering Architecture & Core Methodology

Visual hierarchy on a printed or PDF engineering drawing is governed by international line conventions: ISO 128 (metric $\sqrt{2}$ series) and ASME Y14.2 (inch/metric visible vs. thin line hierarchy). Configuring these exact lineweights inside your GstarCAD .ctb/.stb plot styles and layer templates guarantees unmistakable legibility at A1, A3, and ANSI D scales.

2. Step-by-Step Production Workflow & Command Modules

1. The ISO 128 Metric Line Group Hierarchy (0.13 mm to 1.40 mm)

ISO 128 steps line thicknesses by a factor of $\sqrt{2} \approx 1.414$ so that when an A1 sheet is reduced 50% to A3, the lineweights step down cleanly by one standard pen size. For standard mechanical and architectural drawings, the 0.5 / 0.25 / 0.18 mm or 0.7 / 0.35 / 0.25 mm Line Group is mandatory.

2. ASME Y14.2 Visible, Hidden, Center & Phantom Conventions

ASME Y14.2 specifies a strict 2:1 width ratio between Thick lines (0.60 mm / 0.024 in) for visible outlines, cutting planes, and short break lines, and Thin lines (0.30 mm / 0.012 in) for hidden lines, centerlines, dimension/extension lines, and section hatches.

3. Section Hatching Angles & Material Patterns (ISO 128-50 / ANSI Y14.3)

General metal cross-sections use ANSI31 or ISO02W100 at 45° to the principal axis. Adjacent mating parts in an assembly section must alternate hatch angles (45°, 135°, 30°, 60°) and pitch spacing so part boundaries stand out clearly.

3. Command, Variable & Standard Reference Matrix

Command / Parameter Scope / Target Engineering Behavior & Best Practice
01.2 Continuous Thick 0.50 mm (A3) / 0.70 mm (A1) 0.60 mm — Visible outlines, edges, crests of threads
01.1 Continuous Thin 0.25 mm (A3) / 0.35 mm (A1) 0.30 mm — Dimension lines, leaders, hatching, root of threads
02.1 Dashed Thin (HIDDEN) 0.25 mm 0.30 mm — Hidden outlines and invisible edges
04.1 Long-Dashed Dotted (CENTER) 0.18 mm / 0.25 mm 0.30 mm — Axes of symmetry, pitch circles, centerlines
04.2 Cutting Plane Thick 0.50 mm / 0.70 mm 0.60 mm — Section cutting plane traces and viewing arrows

5. Production QA Verification & Competency Checklist

  • Maintain at least a 2:1 thickness ratio between visible object outlines and dimension/hatch lines.
  • Set PSLTSCALE = 1 and MSLTSCALE = 1 so dashed HIDDEN and CENTER lines plot at identical dash lengths across all viewport scales.
  • Alternate hatch angles (45 deg vs 135 deg) on adjacent components in assembly cross-sections.
