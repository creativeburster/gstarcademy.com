Course 30: Compression/Extension/Torsion Spring & Cam Profile Generators in GstarCAD Mechanical

Calculate wire diameter, shear stress, active coils, and motion follower curves using the built-in Spring and Cam Design Generators in GstarCAD Mechanical 2027.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by GstarCAD Academy Editorial Team Last reviewed 2026-11-03 Editorial Process

1. Engineering Architecture & Core Methodology

Rather than switching to external spreadsheets to calculate Wahl curvature stress correction factors, spring rates ($k = \frac{G d^4}{8 D^3 n_a}$), or cam follower acceleration jerk curves ($s, v, a, j$), GstarCAD Mechanical 2027 embeds engineering calculation wizards that simultaneously verify mechanical strength and draw the exact 2D representation (detailed cross-section or simplified ISO 2162 schematic) plus load-deflection specification tables directly in the drawing.

2. Step-by-Step Production Workflow & Command Modules

Module 1: Helical Compression & Extension Spring Sizing (`AMSPRING`)

Launch the Compression/Extension Spring Generator, choose a standard spring steel material (`EN 10270-1 DH/SH`, `50CrV4`, or `Stainless 1.4310`), and enter the operating envelope (`Preload F1`, `Working Stroke s`, `Max Working Load F2`, and `Mean Coil Diameter D`). The solver filters valid wire diameters $d$, checks torsional shear stress $\tau_{k2} < \tau_{\text{allow}}$, verifies buckling slenderness, and inserts the spring at its exact installed length $L_1$ or $L_2$.

Module 2: Torsion Springs & Belleville Disc Spring Stacks (`DIN 2093`)

For high-load compact clamping actuators (such as CNC spindle drawbars or circuit-breaker mechanisms), select **Belleville Disc Springs (`DIN 2093`)**. Combine series stacks (multiplying deflection) and parallel stacks (multiplying force) while GstarCAD Mechanical plots the non-linear load-deflection curve and draws the exact stacked cross-section inside your housing bore.

Module 3: Disc & Cylindrical Cam Profile Synthesis & CNC Path Export (`AMCAM`)

Run the **Cam Design & Calculation** wizard, define segments using **Modified Sinusoid**, **Modified Trapezoid**, **5th-Order Polynomial (3-4-5)**, or **Cycloidal** motion laws to eliminate infinite jerk spikes ($j = da/dt$). The engine checks maximum pressure angle ($\alpha_{\max} \le 30^\circ$ for translating roller followers), checks minimum radius of curvature ($\rho_{\min} > R_{\text{roller}}$ to prevent undercutting), and outputs the exact cam pitch/working contour polyline for CNC wire-EDM or milling.

3. Command, Variable & Standard Reference Matrix

Command / Parameter Scope / Target Engineering Behavior & Best Practice
Compression / Extension Springs DIN 2089 / ISO 2162 Computes wire diameter d, active coils na, solid length Lc, and buckling safety.
Belleville Disc Springs DIN 2093 Series/Parallel Models stacked disc springs (e.g., 2 parallel x 6 series) with friction hysteresis.

Pressure Angle & Curvature Cam Undercut Check Verifies pressure angle <= 30 deg and minimum pitch radius > roller radius.

4. Automation Script & Reference Implementation (Helical Spring Stiffness & Wahl Factor Calculator LISP)

(defun C:SPRINGRATE ( / d Dmean na G c kw kRate)
  (setq d     (getreal "\nEnter spring wire diameter d (mm): ")
        Dmean (getreal "\nEnter mean coil diameter D (mm): ")
        na    (getreal "\nEnter number of active coils na: ")
        G     81500.0) ;; Shear modulus for spring steel (MPa)
  (if (and d Dmean na (> d 0) (> Dmean d) (> na 0))
    (progn
      (setq c     (/ Dmean d)
            kw    (+ (/ (- (* 4.0 c) 1.0) (- (* 4.0 c) 4.0)) (/ 0.615 c))
            kRate (/ (* G (expt d 4)) (* 8.0 (expt Dmean 3) na)))
      (princ (strcat "\nSpring Index C = D/d        : " (rtos c 2 2) " (Ideal: 4 to 12)"))
      (princ (strcat "\nWahl Stress Factor Kw       : " (rtos kw 2 3)))
      (princ (strcat "\nSpring Stiffness Rate R (k) : " (rtos kRate 2 2) " N/mm"))
    )
  )
  (princ)
)

5. Production QA Verification & Competency Checklist

  • Keep the **Spring Index ($C = D/d$)** between `4.0` and `12.0` (`6–9` optimal); indices below `4` cause severe inner-fiber curvature shear stress (`Wahl factor Kw > 1.40`), while indices above `12` tangle easily.
  • Always place the generated **Spring Parameter Table** (`d, De, L0, na, R, F1, F2, tau2`) on the drawing sheet per ISO 2162-2.
  • In Cam design, never use simple Constant Velocity or Parabolic segments on high-speed machinery without cycloidal/polynomial transitions—infinite jerk spikes cause severe follower vibration and cam wear.
