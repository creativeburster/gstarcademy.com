1. Industrial Challenge & Recurring License Rental Bottleneck

Operating across major hydrocarbon production hubs in the Arabian Gulf, the EPC contractor manages multi-year engineering, procurement, and construction contracts for major national oil companies (NOCs) including Saudi Aramco, ADNOC, and SABIC. Production deliverables require thousands of piping and instrumentation diagrams (P&ID), piping isometric drawings, equipment plot plans, and structural steel fabrication schedules.

As Autodesk transitioned to rigid Named User subscription licensing, multi-national engineering centers in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and design back-offices in India faced severe operational and compliance roadblocks. Cross-border license sharing was heavily penalized, and intermittent internet connectivity at desert plant construction sites regularly caused subscription license timeouts. Confronting escalating recurring fees that approached $1.2 million annually, the IT executive committee mandated a switch to an enterprise perpetual licensing architecture with global floating server support.

2. 30-Day Technical Verification & Standard Compliance Protocol

The contractor’s Engineering Systems Group conducted an exhaustive 30-day technical validation against Saudi Aramco Engineering Standards (SAES):

  • ASME B31.3 Process Piping Isometrics & P&ID Fidelity: The team verified that PCF (Piping Component File) isometrics exported from Intergraph SmartPlant 3D (SP3D) and AVEVA E3D opened seamlessly in GstarCAD. Pipe spool lengths, weld numbers, and valve tag dynamic blocks rendered with zero vector discrepancy.
  • Saudi Aramco Standard Drawing Format Compliance: All corporate SAES border templates, font styles (Standard, Romand, Greek), attribute tags, and revision block fields mapped 100% without proxy object alerts or font substitution glitches.
  • Cross-Border GstarLM License Server Clustering: IT architects successfully tested a resilient, multi-subnet GstarLM floating server configuration connecting Al Khobar, Abu Dhabi, and Mumbai engineering centers, enabling dynamic time-zone-based license sharing across 600 concurrent users.
  • High-Speed Batch Extraction for Bulk MTO: Automated batch extraction routines extracted valve counts, pipe schedule ratings, and flange pressure classes across 2,400 drawing sheets in under 6 minutes, directly feeding SAP material management modules.
