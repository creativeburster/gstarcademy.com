Asian Precision Tooling & Mold Manufacturer Migrates 420 Seats to GstarCAD Mechanical

A world-class precision tooling manufacturer supplying optical lens molds, medical device enclosures, and micro-switch components migrated 420 mold design and CNC programming stations to GstarCAD Mechanical 2027. 1. Industrial Challenge & Recurring License Rental Bottleneck

Operating state-of-the-art mold-making facilities in Dongguan, Kunshan, and Taoyuan, the corporation builds high-cavitation injection molds and ultra-precision progressive stamping dies for global consumer electronics and medical device brands. Tooling tolerances routinely demand ±0.002 mm accuracy across core/cavity inserts, ejector pin matrices, and multi-channel conformal cooling channels.

The manufacturing engineering division previously relied on AutoCAD Mechanical. However, Autodesk’s aggressive enforcement of Named User subscriptions collided head-on with 24/7 CNC machining shifts. CNC operators, wire EDM programmers, and bench fitting technicians only needed intermittent access to verify 2D dimensions and export clean wireframe profiles, yet required full annual subscriptions. Annual licensing fees reached $830,000. Corporate procurement ordered a comprehensive evaluation of GstarCAD Mechanical as a direct, high-precision replacement.

2. 30-Day Technical Verification & Standard Compliance Protocol

The engineering directorate instituted a strict 30-day technical validation on an active 64-cavity medical syringe mold project:

  • Micron-Level Geometric Accuracy & DXF/CAM Compatibility: Wire EDM and CNC milling require clean 2D vector boundaries without micro-gaps or self-intersecting loops. GstarCAD's OVERKILL, FLATTEN, and PEDIT routines generated flawless 2D toolpath DXF files, completely eliminating cutter path pauses and surface gouges.
  • MISUMI, HASCO & DME Standard Mold Base Generators: Toolmakers verified that GstarCAD Mechanical’s built-in standard parts libraries (mold bases, guide pins, ejector sleeves, sprue bushings) matched international catalogs exactly, reducing mold layout drafting time by 30%.
  • Automated Hole Chart & Coordinate Table Generation: Using GstarCAD’s native HOLELIST command, engineers generated complete coordinate tables for 480 ejector and water line holes in 4.2 seconds, with automated bubble callouts matching CNC drill cycle formats.
  • DWG Drawing Compare (DWGCOMPARE): When revision changes occurred on 3D customer STEP models, toolmakers utilized GstarCAD's graphic comparison engine to highlight modified 2D cavity contours instantly in green and magenta.

4. Production Rollout Blueprint & Long-Term Strategic Value

The enterprise deployment was rolled out seamlessly across all plants. Centralized GstarLM floating license servers accommodated rotating three-shift manufacturing schedules without requiring extra software seat purchases.

Tooling design lead time shrank by 18%, and mold trial rework rates dropped by 12% due to precise DXF profile exports. The $2.52M in retained capital was reinvested into high-speed 5-axis Makino machining centers and ultra-accurate Zeiss optical coordinate measuring machines (CMM).
