ASME Y14.5-2018 GD&T 14 Geometric Characteristic Symbols & Modifiers Handbook

Complete mechanical engineering reference for the 14 Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T) symbols, Feature Control Frames, MMC/LMC bonus tolerance, and ISO 1101 updates.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by Chen Haoran, M.Sc., GDTP-S (Lead Mechanical CAD & ASME GD&T Specialist) Last reviewed 2026-11-03 Editorial Process & Testbed

1. Engineering Architecture & Core Methodology

ASME Y14.5-2018 (and ISO 1101) establishes the mathematical language of functional mechanical tolerancing. Noticeably, the 2018 revision retired Concentricity and Symmetry—leaving 12 active geometric characteristic symbols (14 historically)—in favor of Position and Profile of a Surface. In GstarCAD and GstarCAD Mechanical, you construct compliant Feature Control Frames via the TOLERANCE command.

2. Step-by-Step Production Workflow & Command Modules

1. Form Controls (Flatness, Straightness, Circularity, Cylindricity)

Form controls govern the shape of an individual feature relative to itself and never reference a Datum. By Rule #1 (Envelope Principle), a feature of size at Maximum Material Condition (MMC) must have perfect form unless overridden by an explicit straightness-of-derived-median-line or independency symbol $\text{\textcircled{I}}$.

2. Orientation, Location & Profile Controls (Position, Perpendicularity, Profile)

Position ($\oplus$) locates holes, pins, and slots relative to a 3-plane Datum Reference Frame (Primary A, Secondary B, Tertiary C). When modified with $\text{\textcircled{M}}$ (MMC), any departure of the actual hole size toward Least Material Condition (LMC) adds 1:1 Bonus Tolerance:

$$T_{\text{total}} = T_{\text{geo}} + |D_{\text{actual}} - D_{\text{MMC}}|$$

3. Runout Controls (Circular Runout vs. Total Runout)

Used on rotating shafts, bearings, and gears referenced to a datum axis (such as A-B coaxial journals). Total Runout controls cumulative surface variations across the entire cylinder simultaneously.

3. Command, Variable & Standard Reference Matrix

Command / Parameter Scope / Target Engineering Behavior & Best Practice
Straightness / Flatness / Circularity / Cylindricity Form No Datum Allowed — Controls surface or derived median line shape
Perpendicularity / Parallelism / Angularity Orientation Requires Datum(s) — Controls tilt of surfaces or axes relative to datum
Position (True Position) Location Requires Datum(s) (usually) — Locates features of size; supports MMC/LMC bonus
Profile of a Surface / Line Profile Optional Datum(s) — Controls size, form, orientation, and location simultaneously
Circular Runout / Total Runout Runout Requires Datum Axis — Controls coaxial surface deviation during 360-deg rotation

4. Automation Script & Reference Implementation (AutoLISP Bonus Tolerance Calculator for MMC Hole Inspection)

(defun c:GDT-BONUS-CALC ( / mmc actual geo bonus total)
  (setq mmc    (getreal "\nEnter Hole MMC Limit (Smallest Diameter mm): ")
        actual (getreal "\nEnter Measured Actual Mating Envelope Diameter (mm): ")
        geo    (getreal "\nEnter Feature Control Frame Position Tolerance (mm): "))
  (setq bonus (max 0.0 (- actual mmc))
        total (+ geo bonus))
  (princ (strcat "\n[ASME Y14.5] Bonus Tolerance = " (rtos bonus 2 3)
                 " mm | Total Allowable Position Zone = Dia " (rtos total 2 3) " mm"))
  (princ)
)

5. Production QA Verification & Competency Checklist

  • Never attach a Datum reference to Form tolerances (Flatness, Straightness, Circularity, Cylindricity).
  • Replace legacy Concentricity and Symmetry callouts with Position or Total Runout / Profile per ASME Y14.5-2018.
  • Verify that Primary, Secondary, and Tertiary datums constrain all 6 degrees of freedom (3 translation, 3 rotation).
