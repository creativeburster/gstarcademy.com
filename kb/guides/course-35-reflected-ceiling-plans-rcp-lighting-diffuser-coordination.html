Course 35: Reflected Ceiling Plans (RCP), Lighting Photometric Layouts & Diffuser Coordination

Coordinate architectural acoustic ceiling grids, recessed LED troffers, HVAC supply/return diffusers, fire sprinklers, and access hatches in GstarCAD 2027.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by Dr. Lin Wei, Ph.D., PE (Principal BIM & Interoperability Architect) Last reviewed 2026-11-03 Editorial Process & Testbed

1. Engineering Architecture & Core Methodology

The **Reflected Ceiling Plan (`RCP`)** is the single most crowded multi-discipline coordination drawing in commercial buildings: architecture owns the `600x600 mm` acoustic T-bar grid and gypsum bulkheads; electrical owns recessed LED luminaires, emergency exit signs, and smoke detectors; HVAC owns supply/return air diffusers and linear slot diffusers; and fire protection owns pendent sprinklers. In GstarCAD 2027, mastering Xref layer states, `HATCHSETORIGIN` ceiling grid centering, and access-panel clearance zones eliminates unsightly field cutting.

2. Step-by-Step Production Workflow & Command Modules

Module 1: Architectural Xref Layer State Setup for RCPs (Hiding Doors & Furniture)

Attach the architectural floor plan as an `XREF Overlay`. In your RCP layout viewport, freeze floor-level layers (`A-FURN`, `A-DOOR-PANEL`, `A-FLOR-PATT`, `A-SANITARY`) while turning on `A-DOOR-HEADER` (so wall headers show continuous above door openings) and setting `A-WALL` to `VP Color = 252` halftone.

Module 2: Centering 600x600mm Acoustic Ceiling Grids via HATCHSETORIGIN

Never leave a `600x600 mm` ceiling hatch (`NET` user-defined double hatch at `600 mm` spacing) anchored at WCS `0,0,0`—doing so often leaves ugly `45 mm` sliver tiles along perimeter walls! Instead, snap the **Hatch Origin (`HATCHSETORIGIN`)** to the exact room center (or shifted by `300 mm`) so perimeter border tiles on opposite walls are symmetrical and $\ge 300\text{ mm}$ wide.

Module 3: Multi-Discipline Grid Snapping & Ceiling Access Door (`600x600`) Placement

Insert `600x600 mm` LED panels, `600x600 mm` 4-way supply diffusers, and centered sprinkler blocks directly into the aligned ceiling grid cells. Wherever a VAV box, fan-coil unit (`FCU`), fire damper, or isolation valve sits above a non-removable gypsum board ceiling, place a mandatory `600x600 mm` hinged **Ceiling Access Hatch (`A-CLNG-ACCS`)** directly below its service side.

3. Command, Variable & Standard Reference Matrix

Command / Parameter Scope / Target Engineering Behavior & Best Practice
HATCH (User-Defined Double) 600x600mm Acoustic Grid Creates orthogonal T-bar grid lines at exact 600mm (or 24 inch) spacing.
HATCHSETORIGIN Symmetric Perimeter Tiles Centers hatch origin at room midpoint (or +300mm) so edge tiles are >= 300mm wide.
A-DOOR-HEADER vs. A-DOOR RCP Door Header Layer Hides door swings while showing continuous wall soffits above door frames.
A-CLNG-ACCS (600x600) Maintenance Access Hatch Marks mandatory ceiling access panels below concealed FCU/VAV/Fire Damper equipment.

4. Automation Script & Reference Implementation (Symmetric Ceiling Tile Border Calculator & Hatch Origin Centerer LISP)

(defun C:CENTERCEILING ( / ent obj minPt maxPt cx cy)
  (vl-load-com)
  (if (and (setq ent (car (entsel "\nSelect 600x600 ceiling grid HATCH to center symmetrically in room: ")))
           (= (cdr (assoc 0 (entget ent))) "HATCH"))
    (progn
      (setq obj (vlax-ename->vla-object ent))
      (vla-GetBoundingBox obj 'minPt 'maxPt)
      (setq minPt (vlax-safearray->list minPt)
            maxPt (vlax-safearray->list maxPt)
            cx    (* 0.5 (+ (car minPt) (car maxPt)))
            cy    (* 0.5 (+ (cadr minPt) (cadr maxPt))))
      (vla-put-Origin obj (vlax-3d-point cx cy 0.0))
      (vla-Evaluate obj)
      (princ (strcat "\n[Course 35 RCP] Centered ceiling grid hatch origin at room midpoint ("
                     (rtos cx 2 1) ", " (rtos cy 2 1) ")."))
    )
  )
  (princ)
)

5. Production QA Verification & Competency Checklist

  • Verify that perimeter acoustic ceiling border tiles on opposite walls are equal in width and **never narrower than half a tile (`>= 300 mm`)**—if a border tile is `<300 mm`, shift the hatch origin by `300 mm` along that axis!
  • Confirm every concealed Fan Coil Unit (FCU), VAV box, motorized fire/smoke damper, and balancing valve above a fixed gypsum ceiling has a dedicated `600x600 mm` access panel on the RCP.
  • Maintain at least `1,200 mm` separation between cold supply air diffusers and wall-mounted thermostats or smoke detectors to prevent false readings and draft stratification.
