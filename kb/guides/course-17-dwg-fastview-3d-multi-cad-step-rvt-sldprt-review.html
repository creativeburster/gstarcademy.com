Enable procurement, sales, and quality engineers to orbit, explode assemblies, cut dynamic XYZ cross-sections, and measure PMI tolerances across 20+ 3D formats (.sldprt, .rvt, .catpart, .step).

Course Technical Scope & Production Competencies

Purchasing $6,000+ authoring licenses of CATIA, Siemens NX, SOLIDWORKS, Creo, or Inventor for procurement buyers, tooling estimators, and shop-floor assembly technicians is a massive waste of IT capital. Course 17 covers FastView 3D as an all-in-one, lightweight multi-CAD 3D inspection workstation.

  • Open 30+ native and neutral 3D formats—including CATIA (.CATPart/.CATProduct), NX (.prt), SOLIDWORKS (.sldprt/.sldasm), Creo, Inventor, Revit (.rvt), IFC, STEP AP242, IGES, and Parasolid (.x_t)—in seconds.
  • Interrogate internal assembly clearances using 3-Plane Dynamic Sectioning, minimum distance/wall-thickness analysis, bounding-box stock sizing, and mass-property center-of-gravity (CoG) calculation.
  • Generate interactive 3D Exploded Views, inspect embedded 3D Semantic PMI (GD&T tolerances), and check for volumetric hard interferences across multi-part mechanical assemblies.

Module 1: FastView 3D Multi-CAD Review: Native CATIA/NX/SOLIDWORKS/STEP Sectioning, PMI & Exploded Views

In mold-making, sheet-metal fabrication, and contract machining, quoting engineers receive customer RFQ packages in every conceivable 3D CAD format. FastView 3D eliminates translation bottlenecks by reading native B-Rep boundary representations and assembly product structures directly without requiring the originating CAD software to be installed on the machine.

Estimators immediately compute the exact 3D Stock Bounding Box ($L \times W \times H$), Volume ($ ext{cm}^3$), Surface Area (for electroplating or powder-coating cost estimation), and Total Mass (applying custom material densities such as $7.85\,\text{g/cm}^3$ for steel or $2.70\,\text{g/cm}^3$ for 6061-T6 aluminum) in three clicks.

Module 2: Step-by-Step Hands-On Command Execution Lab

Follow this structured 4-step laboratory procedure directly at the GstarCAD command line or dynamic input prompt:

  1. Step 1 — Environment & System Variable Initialization: At the command prompt, verify the active state of core commands and variables: FastView 3D, Exploded View, Section Plane, PMI Measurement, Safe Encrypted Share. Ensure that object snap bitcodes (OSMODE=175), associative dimensioning (DIMASSOC=2), and multi-core display regeneration (WHIPTHREAD=3) are active.
  2. Step 2 — Primary Geometry & Metadata Authoring: Execute the primary workflow on standardized ISO 13567 or discipline-specific layers. Never place production geometry or block definitions on Defpoints, and keep internal block geometry on Layer 0 so instances inherit host layer properties dynamically.
  3. Step 3 — Associative Verification & Revision Auditing: Test parametric associativity by stretching or modifying parent geometry and confirming that linked dimensions, attributes, schedules, or section views update automatically without disassociation warnings (ANNOMONITOR=2).
  4. Step 4 — Pre-Transmittal Quality Assurance (QA/QC): Prior to saving or publishing the deliverable, run OVERKILL to eliminate duplicate vectors, -PURGE (RegApps and All) to strip orphaned symbol table bloat, and AUDIT to verify 100% AcDbObject handle integrity.
