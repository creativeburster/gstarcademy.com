1. Industrial Plant Challenges: From 3D Model to Fabrication Spool

In oil & gas refineries, LNG terminals, and chemical processing facilities, an EPC piping project involves tens of thousands of individual pipe spools that must be prefabricated off-site and field-welded. While 3D plant modeling tools (AVEVA E3D, Intergraph Smart 3D) maintain the master 3D spatial plant model, pipe fabricators require standardized 2D isometric spool drawings (ISOs) containing precise cut lengths, weld identification numbers, non-destructive examination (NDE) percentages, and bill of materials (BOM). Generating these drawings manually is impossibly slow and error-prone.

2. Automated PCF Ingestion & Spool Drawing Engine

The Piping Component File (PCF) is the universal plaintext standard developed for piping data transfer. An automated GstarCAD .NET engine ingests PCF files and generates verified fabrication drawings:

  • Coordinate Parse & Centerline Routing: Reads PIPE, ELBOW, TEE, FLANGE, and VALVE blocks with start/end 3D coordinates. Calculates true 3D cut lengths using $L = \sqrt{\Delta X^2 + \Delta Y^2 + \Delta Z^2}$ while subtracting fitting make-up dimensions.
  • Skew Angle & Falling Gradient Calculation: Detects 2D and 3D rolling offsets and calculates horizontal/vertical triangle annotations and falling slope ratios (e.g., 1:100 gravity drainage lines).
  • Weld ID & NDE Allocation: Automatically tags every field weld (FW) and shop weld (BW) with sequential tracking numbers, assigning radiographic testing (RT) or ultrasonic testing (UT) symbols per ASME B31.3 design severity.
  • Automated Bill of Material (BOM) Generation: Generates categorized parts lists (Pipes, Flanges, Fittings, Gaskets, Stud Bolts) mapped to ASME/ASTM material specs (e.g., ASTM A106 Gr. B pipe, A105 flanges).
