GstarCAD Mechanical BOM Automation: Multi-Standard (ISO/DIN/ASME) Part References & SAP/ERP Sync

Configure GstarCAD Mechanical 2027 AMBOM databases, custom part attribute schemas, associative leader balloons, and automated XML/CSV exports for SAP and Oracle ERP integration.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by Chen Haoran, M.Sc., GDTP-S (Lead Mechanical CAD & ASME GD&T Specialist) Last reviewed 2026-10-26 Editorial Process & Testbed

1. Architecture of the `AMBOM` Relational Database in GstarCAD Mechanical

In mechanical equipment manufacturing, manually typing bill-of-materials (BOM) rows into Excel or static CAD tables causes a 3% to 5% procurement mismatch rate whenever an engineer adds a fastener or changes a shaft material grade. GstarCAD Mechanical 2027 embeds a relational AMBOM database directly inside the native AC1032 DWG file—maintaining 100% binary compatibility with legacy AutoCAD Mechanical (ACM) drawings.

Every mechanical component on the drawing is linked to the BOM via an AMPARTREF (Part Reference) node. Whether attached to a custom 2D contour, a block reference, or inserted automatically from the 100,000-part ISO/DIN/ANSI standard catalog (`AMSTDPLIB`), the Part Reference tracks live instance counts and pushes updates immediately to associative AMBALLOON callouts and AMPARTLIST tables.

3. Automated ERP Export Pipeline & Quality Verification

Before releasing a mechanical assembly drawing to production procurement, execute the following verification protocol in GstarCAD Mechanical 2027:

  1. Collect & Renumber Balloons (`AMBALLOON`): Use the Collect and Horizontal/Vertical Align options to group fastener stacks (Bolt + Washer + Spring Washer + Nut) onto a single multi-tag leader arrow.
  2. Zero Unballooned Part Audit: Open the `AMBOM` dialog; components present in the database that lack a visual balloon on the sheet are highlighted so no required part is omitted from assembly instructions.
  3. Structured CSV/XLSX Export: Click Export inside `AMPARTLIST` (or run batch extraction) to output UTF-8 CSV/Excel schedules mapped directly to your SAP/Oracle import template.

4. Multi-Standard Merging & Custom Company Standard Templates (`.DWT`)

Global machinery OEMs frequently design assemblies in Europe under **ISO / DIN** metric standards while manufacturing sub-assemblies in North America under **ASME / ANSI** inch standards. GstarCAD Mechanical 2027 allows engineers to switch the active drafting standard dynamically within `AMOPTIONS → AM:Standards`—or maintain dedicated `.dwt` company templates pre-configured with custom title blocks, parts list column formulas, and surface texture libraries.
