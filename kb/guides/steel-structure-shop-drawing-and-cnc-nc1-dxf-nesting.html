Structural Steel Shop Drawings, Gusset Plate Detailing & Zero-Gap CNC Plasma/Laser DXF Nesting in GstarCAD 2027

Prepare AISC 360 / Eurocode 3 structural steel connection details, high-strength bolt hole gauges, and clean CNC plate cutting DXF files in GstarCAD 2027.

1. From 3D Structural Models to Shop-Floor CNC Plate Fabrication

In heavy structural steel fabrication (industrial warehouses, truss bridges, offshore jackets), 3D models authored in Tekla Structures or Advance Steel output thousands of 2D assembly shop drawings (`.dwg`) and gusset/stiffener plate cutting profiles (`.dxf`). GstarCAD 2027 serves as the workhorse fabrication detailing station—allowing shop engineers to verify bolt edge distances, weld bevel symbols, and sanitize 2D DXF contours before feeding SigmaNEST, Lantek, or ProNest CNC plasma/fiber-laser tables.

2. EN 1090-2 / AISC 360 Bolt Hole Clearance & CNC Layer Mapping

CNC Nesting Feature Standard DXF Layer & Color GstarCAD Geometry Requirement
Outer Plate Cut Perimeter CUT_OUTER (ACI Color 7 White) Single closed `LWPOLYLINE` (`Flag 70 = 1`), zero self-intersections, $Z = 0.000$.
Internal Bolt Holes & Slots CUT_INNER (ACI Color 1 Red) True `CIRCLE` entities ($d_0 = d + 2\,\text{mm}$ for M16–M24 per EN 1090-2) for drill/pierce recognition.
Powder/Zinc Scribing & Part ID SCRIBE_MARK (ACI Color 3 Green) Single-stroke `simplex.shx` text or stiffener layout lines etched before cutting.

3. AutoLISP Steel Plate Weight, Surface Paint Area & Loop Verifier

;; GstarCAD 2027 — Calculate Steel Plate Mass (kg) & Verify Closed CNC Cutting Loop
(vl-load-com)
(defun c:GCAD_STEEL_PLATE_AUDIT ( / ent obj thk areaM2 massKg)
  (setq ent (car (entsel "\nSelect outer steel plate polyline contour: ")))
  (if ent
    (progn
      (setq obj (vlax-ename->vla-object ent))
      (if (= (vla-get-Closed obj) :vlax-false)
        (princ "\n[ALERT] Contour is OPEN! Closing polyline automatically for CNC nesting...")
      )
      (vla-put-Closed obj :vlax-true)
      (setq thk (getreal "\nEnter Steel Plate Thickness t (mm): "))
      (setq areaM2 (/ (vla-get-Area obj) 1000000.0))
      ;; Density of structural carbon steel (S235/S355/A36) = 7.85 kg per m2 per mm thickness
      (setq massKg (* areaM2 thk 7.85))
      (princ (strcat "\n[Steel Plate Audit] Net Area = " (rtos areaM2 2 4) " m2 | Theoretical Weight = " (rtos massKg 2 2) " kg."))
    )
  )
  (princ)
)

4. Kerf Compensation, Pierce Lead-Ins & ERP Plate Traceability

Before releasing sanitized `.dxf` plate contours from GstarCAD 2027 to the CNC plasma/fiber-laser nesting queue, fabrication engineers must verify how kerf width compensation and heat-number traceability are handled between the CAD geometry and the CAM post-processor:

  • True Nominal Geometry (`1:1`) in DXF: Never offset plate outer contours inside the `.dwg` to account for plasma or laser kerf width (`1.2 mm` to `3.5 mm`). Export exact `1:1` nominal geometry (`LWPOLYLINE` with `Closed = Yes` on `CUT_OUTER` and `CUT_INNER` layers) so the CAM nesting software ( Lantek, SigmaNEST, Pronest) can dynamically apply `G41` / `G42` cutter radius compensation based on the active nozzle amperage and plate thickness.
  • Slotted Bolt Holes vs. Drilled Holes: For high-strength friction-grip (HSFG / A325 / Grade 10.9) connections requiring CNC drilling rather than thermal cutting (to avoid heat-affected zone hardening around bolt holes per EN 1090-2 Execution Class EXC3/EXC4), place circle entities on a dedicated `DRILL_HOLES` layer while keeping non-structural drainage or lifting holes on `CUT_INNER`.
  • Powder Marking & Heat Number Etching (`SCRIBE` Layer): Single-line text (`SHX` stick font `txt.shx`) placed on the `SCRIBE` layer inside the closed plate contour instructs the CNC machine's pneumatic scribe or low-power laser marker to etch the assembly mark (e.g., `PL-1042 | S355J2 | t=16`) before cutting the outer perimeter.
