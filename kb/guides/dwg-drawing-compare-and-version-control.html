DWG Drawing Compare (CMP) & Engineering Revision Version Control Guide

Master automated DWG drawing comparison in GstarCAD and AutoCAD: geometric vs attribute diffing, revision cloud generation, graphic compare for Xrefs, and QA/QC sign-off workflows.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-10-12 Editorial process

1. Eliminating Human Error in Engineering Revision Reviews

In complex architectural, structural, and piping projects, subcontractors frequently submit revised DWG files containing subtle geometric shifts, altered pipe diameters, or modified block attributes that are never documented in revision clouds. Manual visual inspection across 50+ layers is slow and error-prone. GstarCAD's built-in Drawing Compare (`CMP` / `COMPARE`) engine performs mathematical entity-by-entity and pixel-level comparisons in seconds.

Comparison ModeCommand / ToolDetection MechanismBest Engineering Use Case

Visual Graphic CompareGRAPHICCOMPAREHigh-speed pixel/vector overlay buffer diffingComparing exploded blocks, proxy objects, or dense hatches
Xref Revision CompareXREF CompareHighlights changes in updated external reference filesAuditing incoming architectural background updates

2. Color-Coded Diff Visualization & Automated Revision Clouds

When executing `COMPARE` between a baseline drawing (`Rev_A.dwg`) and a revised submittal (`Rev_B.dwg`), the engine generates a color-coded inspection workspace: Green highlights newly added entities, Red indicates deleted entities, and Gray dims unchanged background geometry. Engineers can automatically wrap all detected differences in polygonal Revision Clouds (`REVCLOUD`) and export a permanent diff snapshot.

3. Detecting Non-Graphical Attribute & Text Changes

A major hazard in manufacturing and MEP engineering occurs when a drafter leaves geometry untouched but edits a block attribute (e.g., changing a valve pressure rating from PN16 to PN25 or a structural beam callout from W12x26 to W12x40). By enabling Attribute and Text comparison filters in the Compare settings panel, QA/QC engineers immediately catch non-geometric specification changes.

4. Enterprise DWG Naming & Check-In Governance

Integrate Drawing Compare into formal ISO 19650 gate reviews: require lead engineers to run an automated diff check before promoting any DWG container from Work-in-Progress (WIP) to the Shared project directory.
