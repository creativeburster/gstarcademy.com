PBR Material IOR, Fresnel F0 & Physically Plausible Albedo Calibration Chart

Physically Based Rendering (PBR) calibration reference for architectural and product visualization: Index of Refraction (IOR), Fresnel F0 reflectance, and sRGB/Linear albedo bounds.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by GstarCAD Academy Editorial Team Last reviewed 2026-11-03 Editorial Process

1. Engineering Architecture & Core Methodology

When exporting 3D architectural and mechanical models from GstarCAD into physically based path tracers (V-Ray, Twinmotion, Lumion, Blender Cycles, D5 Render), photorealism depends on obeying energy conservation: Diffuse Albedo must never reach pure white (`255`) or pure black (`0`), and dielectric Fresnel $F_0$ must match real-world Index of Refraction (IOR) measurements.

2. Step-by-Step Production Workflow & Command Modules

1. Schlick's Fresnel $F_0$ Equation from Index of Refraction (IOR)

For any non-metallic (dielectric) surface viewed at normal incidence ($0^\circ$), the specular reflectance $F_0$ is determined by its Index of Refraction $n$:

$$F_0 = \left(\frac{n - 1}{n + 1}\right)^2$$

For standard architectural glass ($n = 1.50$), $F_0 = (0.5 / 2.5)^2 = 0.04$ ($4\%$ linear reflectance, which maps to Specular = 0.5 in Unreal/Twinmotion/Principled BSDF).

2. The PBR Albedo Luminance Law (No Pure Black < 30 sRGB, No Pure White > 240 sRGB)

In the physical world, fresh charcoal/asphalt reflects $\sim 4\%$ of light (sRGB 50–55), and fresh snow or matte white titanium dioxide wall paint reflects at most $80\%\text{–}85\%$ of light (sRGB 235–242). Setting architectural white walls to RGB 255,255,255 breaks GI photon bounce decay and blows out highlights.

3. Organizing GstarCAD Layers by Material Assignment before Export

Assign every 3D solid in GstarCAD to a material-specific layer (e.g., 3D-MAT-GLASS-CLEAR, 3D-MAT-ALU-ANODIZED, 3D-MAT-CONCRETE-CAST) so your renderer's Collapse by Layer importer links PBR shaders automatically across design revisions.

3. Command, Variable & Standard Reference Matrix

Command / Parameter Scope / Target Engineering Behavior & Best Practice
Water / Ice IOR = 1.333 / 1.310 F0 = 2.0% (0.020) — Specular slider = 0.25
Architectural Float Glass IOR = 1.520 F0 = 4.26% (0.043) — Transmission = 1.0, Roughness = 0.02
Clear Polycarbonate / Acrylic IOR = 1.586 / 1.490 F0 = 5.1% / 3.9% — Interior glazing & light diffusers
Matte White Architectural Paint IOR = 1.50 (Dielectric) Albedo = sRGB 235 (80% Linear) — Never exceed sRGB 242 on walls
Asphalt / Dark Charcoal Metal Dielectric / Metal Albedo = sRGB 52..60 (4% Linear) — Never drop below sRGB 35 on diffuse

4. Automation Script & Reference Implementation (AutoLISP Fresnel F0 Reflectance Calculator from Index of Refraction (IOR))

(defun c:CALC-FRESNEL-F0 ( / ior f0 spec)
  (setq ior (getreal "\nEnter Material Index of Refraction IOR (e.g. 1.333 Water, 1.52 Glass): "))
  (setq f0   (expt (/ (- ior 1.0) (+ ior 1.0)) 2.0)
        spec (/ f0 0.08)) ; Unreal/Principled BSDF Specular slider (0.5 = 4% F0)
  (princ (strcat "\n[PBR Fresnel] Linear F0 = " (rtos (* f0 100.0) 2 2)
                 "% | Principled BSDF Specular = " (rtos spec 2 3)))
  (princ)
)

5. Production QA Verification & Competency Checklist

  • Keep all dielectric diffuse albedo textures strictly within sRGB 35–242 (4%–85% linear reflectance).
  • Use Metallic = 1.0 (pure conductor) or Metallic = 0.0 (pure dielectric) except at oxidized/dusty transition masks.
  • Set FACETRES = 10 in GstarCAD before exporting curved 3D solids to FBX/OBJ/3DS so curved glass reflections do not facet.
