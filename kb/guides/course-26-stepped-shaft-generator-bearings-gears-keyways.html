Course 26: Stepped Shaft Generator (AMSHAFT), Rolling Bearings, Gears & Keyway Automation

Design complete power-transmission shaft assemblies in GstarCAD Mechanical 2027 using AMSHAFT2D, ISO/DIN standard rolling bearings, involute gear profiles, and parallel keyways.

By GstarCAD Academy Engineering Editorial Board (Suzhou Gstarsoft Co., Ltd.) Reviewed by GstarCAD Academy Editorial Team Last reviewed 2026-11-03 Editorial Process

1. Engineering Architecture & Core Methodology

Manually drafting a multi-stepped gearbox transmission shaft—complete with thread relief undercuts (`DIN 76`), grinding relief grooves (`DIN 509`), retaining ring circlip grooves (`DIN 471`), parallel feather keys (`ISO 2491` / `DIN 6885`), and paired angular-contact ball bearings—takes hours in basic 2D CAD. In GstarCAD Mechanical 2027, the parametric **Shaft Generator (`AMSHAFT2D`)** constructs both the longitudinal front view and associative left/right cross-section views in minutes while automatically routing centerlines (`AM_7`), hidden contours (`AM_3`), and hatches (`AM_8`).

2. Step-by-Step Production Workflow & Command Modules

Module 1: Building Stepped Cylinders, Cones & Wrench Flats in AMSHAFT2D

Launch AMSHAFT2D, specify the shaft centerline start point and rotation angle, and add sequential segments (`Cylinder`, `Cone`, `Polygon/Wrench Flat`, `Thread`). As you enter each diameter and length (`Ø45 x 60`, `Ø50k6 x 85`, `Ø40h7 x 50`), GstarCAD Mechanical automatically draws vertical shoulder lines and updates the centerline overhang.

Module 2: Inserting Undercuts (DIN 509), Circlip Grooves & Keyways (DIN 6885)

Select the **Sub-Features** tab inside the Shaft Generator to attach standard DIN/ISO/ANSI shaft shoulder undercuts (`Form E` for cylindrical grinding or `Form F` for planar+cylindrical grinding), shaft end chamfers, internal blind tapped center holes (`DIN 332`), and parallel/Woodruff keyseats that automatically filter valid cross-sections based on the host shaft diameter.

Module 3: Mounting Associative Rolling Bearings, Seals & Spur/Bevel Gears

Run AMSTDPLIB (Standard Parts Library) → `Bearings → Rolling Bearings`, pick the `Ø50` shaft journal, and select a deep-groove (`ISO 15` / `6210`) or tapered roller bearing (`30210`). GstarCAD Mechanical automatically locks the inner bore `d = 50 mm`, applies `AM2DHIDE` foreground occlusion over the shaft, and registers the bearing part number into the BOM database.

3. Command, Variable & Standard Reference Matrix

Command / Parameter Scope / Target Engineering Behavior & Best Practice
AMSHAFT2D Parametric Shaft Engine Generates stepped shafts, internal bores, threads, and cross-section views.
DIN 509 (Form E / F) Grinding Relief Undercut Prevents grinding wheel corner interference at stepped bearing shoulders.
DIN 6885 / ISO 2491 Parallel Keyway Seat Sizes key width (b) and depth (t1/t2) automatically from shaft diameter.
AM2DHIDE Automatic Shaft Occlusion Hides underlying shaft contour lines when bearings or gears are mounted.

5. Production QA Verification & Competency Checklist

  • Always insert `DIN 509 Form E/F` grinding relief undercuts on bearing journals that require cylindrical grinding (`Ra <= 0.8 um`).
  • Generate associative cross-section cuts (`Section A-A`) directly from the `AMSHAFT2D` dialog so keyway depth dimensions remain linked to the main shaft.
  • Verify that mounted bearings and circlips appear automatically inside `AMPARTLIST` with accurate ISO/DIN designations.
