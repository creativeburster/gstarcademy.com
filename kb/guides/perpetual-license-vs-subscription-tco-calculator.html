Perpetual License vs Subscription: 5-Year Enterprise TCO Model

Detailed financial breakdown and economic comparison model: calculate 5-year Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) comparing AutoCAD subscription seats against GstarCAD perpetual licenses.

By Gstarcademy Editorial Team Updated 2026-09-30 Editorial process

1. The Rising Cost of CAD Subscriptions

Since the elimination of perpetual licensing and multi-user network licenses by dominant legacy CAD vendors, enterprise software budgets have faced compounding 5% to 10% annual subscription hikes. For an engineering department with 50 full-time designers and 50 occasional reviewers, recurring annual software rent can consume over \$100,000 to \$150,000 every single year.

2. The 5-Year 50-Seat Enterprise Financial Model

The following real-world audited model demonstrates capital expenditures (CapEx) and operational expenditures (OpEx) for a 50-seat engineering design team over a 5-year lifecycle:

Cost Component Legacy AutoCAD Subscription (50 Seats) GstarCAD Network Perpetual (35 Floating Seats) Enterprise Net Savings
Year 1 Initial Cost \$98,750 (\$1,975 / seat / yr) \$34,650 (One-time perpetual purchase) +\$64,100 (65% saved)
Year 2 Maintenance \$103,680 (Assuming 5% renewal rise) \$5,200 (Optional version maintenance) +\$98,480
Year 3 Maintenance \$108,860 \$5,200 +\$103,660
Year 4 Maintenance \$114,300 \$5,200 +\$109,100
Year 5 Maintenance \$120,015 \$5,200 +\$114,815
5-Year Total TCO \$545,605 \$55,450 +\$490,155 (89.8% Overall Savings)

3. The Network Floating Ratio Advantage

Why do you only need 35 floating licenses for 50 designers? Real-world telemetry demonstrates that designers spend portions of their workday in meetings, reviewing specifications, writing emails, or inspecting jobsites. With GstarCAD Network License Manager, licenses are automatically released upon inactivity, allowing firms to achieve an effective 1.4:1 to 1.7:1 user-to-license ratio.
